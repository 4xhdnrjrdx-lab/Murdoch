СЛАЙД 14 — «The 7Ps» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 15 — «7 Ps of Marketing» (метка «What?»): Product: goods/services offered. Price: amount customers pay. Place: where/how sold. Promotion: activities to communicate/promote. People: staff involved in service delivery. Process: systems/procedures in delivery. Physical Evidence: tangible elements of the service experience (environment, facilities).

СЛАЙД 16 — «Porter's Five Forces» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 17 — «Porter's 5 Forces» (метка «What?»): Threat of New Entrants — how easy to enter; high barriers (capital, brand loyalty) reduce threat. Bargaining Power of Suppliers — few/unique suppliers → high power, squeezes profits. Bargaining Power of Buyers — many choices/large volumes → high power, pressures prices. Threat of Substitutes — availability increases competition, limits pricing power. Rivalry Among Existing Competitors — number of competitors, growth rate, differentiation.

СЛАЙД 18 — «SWOT analysis» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 19 — «SWOT Analysis vs SWOT Synthesis» (метка «What?»): Strengths — internal positive; Weaknesses — internal negative; Opportunities — external exploitable; Threats — external harmful.

СЛАЙД 20 — «SWOT SYNTHESIS» (диаграмма). Два ряда синих блоков, соединённых линиями. Верхний ряд: (1) Strength/Weakness/Neutral points/Blind spots; (2) Strength/Weakness/In connection to the three environments; (3) Threats/Opportunities; (4) Threats/Opportunities/How do they relate to the three environments and the SW; (5) Threats/Opportunities/How do they relate to the buyer. Нижний ряд: Map the internal environment (Systems/Human capital/Financial resources/Internal culture/Departments/Specialised knowledge/Etc.); Map the micro environment (Competitors Primary/Secondary/Informal / Suppliers / Community / Complementors/Contributors / Etc.); Map the external environment (PESTLE); Synthesise (большой пустой блок). Три «среды» (internal, micro, external/PESTLE) питают SWOT-факторы → сводятся в Synthesise и соотносятся с тремя средами, SW и покупателем.

СЛАЙД 21 — «Synthesising the "So What?"»: The goal of the SWOT Synthesis is to find the Strategic Fit. We use systems thinking: "How do our Strengths allow us to capture Opportunities while mitigating the Cortisol of our Threats?" Neutral Points & Blind Spots: every business has neutral facts that aren't yet strengths or weaknesses. The "Expert" monitors these so they don't become blind spots that allow a New Entrant to slip in. Friction Mapping: map external Threats (External Friction) against internal Strengths (Friction-reduction tools). If we can't use our strengths to lower the customer's Cortisol, we are not marketing; we are just existing.

СЛАЙД 22 — «PESTLE Analysis» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 23 — «PESTLE» (метка «What?»): framework for macro-environmental factors. Political: policies, regulations, stability, trade agreements. Economic: growth, inflation, interest rates, unemployment, exchange rates. Social: cultural trends, demographics, lifestyle, consumer behaviour. Technological: advancements, automation, innovation, infrastructure. Legal: laws, regulations, legal frameworks. Environmental: regulations, sustainability, climate change, resource availability.

СЛАЙД 24 — «STP Model» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 25 — «STP» (метка «What?»): Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning. Dividing the market into distinct groups, selecting the most promising, creating a compelling message that resonates.

СЛАЙД 26 — «Customer Journey Mapping» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 27 — «Customer Journey Map» (метка «What?»): visually outlining the steps a customer goes through when interacting with company/product/brand; all touchpoints from initial awareness to post-purchase and beyond, across channels (website, social media, customer service).

СЛАЙД 28 — «Brand Identity and Brand Equity» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 29 — «Brand Identity and Equity» (метка «What?»): Brand identity = collection of all elements a company creates to portray the right image. Brand equity = the value premium a company generates from a product with a recognisable name vs a generic equivalent. 🔗 https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/brandequity.asp

СЛАЙД 30 — «ROI, CTR, CLV, PPC» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 31 — «ROI, CTR, CLV, PPC» (метка «What?»): ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100. CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) × 100. CLV = predicted net profit attributed to the entire future relationship with a customer. PPC = advertising model where you pay a fee each time an ad is clicked.

СЛАЙД 32 — «SEO, Social Media, Email Marketing» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 33 — «SEO, Social Media, Email Marketing» (метка «What?»): SEO — optimising website/content to rank higher in SERPs (keywords, backlinks, valuable content aligned with search intent). Social Media Marketing — using platforms (Facebook, Instagram, X) to connect, build awareness, drive engagement. Email Marketing — targeted emails to nurture leads, build relationships, promote (list-building, segmentation, compelling campaigns).

СЛАЙД 34 — «Differentiation and UVP» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 35 — «Differentiation and UVP» (метка «What?»): Differentiation — what sets your brand/product apart; unique qualities that make you stand out. UVP — clear statement of specific benefits; why customers should choose you over competitors.

СЛАЙД 36 — «CSR» (раздел-заголовок).

СЛАЙД 37 — «CSR» (метка «What?»): commitment to ethical and sustainable practices that benefit society and environment. As a marketing tool: communicating initiatives to build positive brand image and reputation.

СЛАЙД 38 — «CSR» (метка «How?» — зелёная): Patagonia — outdoor clothing company known for environmental sustainability. Recycled materials, donate profits to environmental causes, advocate for environmental protection. Core part of brand identity and marketing, attracting customers who share values.
