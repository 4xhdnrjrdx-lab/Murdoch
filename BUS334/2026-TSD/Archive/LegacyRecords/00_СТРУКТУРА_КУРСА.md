## Секция 4 — Session 1 - The World Of Data

_In order to successfully grow a business, one must plan carefully. What is more, in today’s digital world business decision makers need direct access to real time, reliable, relevant and actionable data in order to make critical business decisions every day. Data helps us to understand the area the business is in, its main competitors, its market niche, its customers, its strategies, its risks, how experience the management team, return on investment, return on equity and so on. In summary, the best way to predict the future is to create it. {%} Prior to session one, complete all required readings and review the materials contained in the pre-workshop section of LMS. {%} By the end of this session you will be able to: - Review the unit scope. - Develop an understanding of the unit assessment requirements. - Begin to understand how to create value for business with data analytics. - Gain an understanding of how we could use data for decision-making. {%} To undertake study for this topic, it is recommended that you read the Learning Module (Topic 1 (see LMS)). {%}_

## Секция 5 — Session 2 - Data Analytics for Business

_Data analytics (DA) is the process of examining data sets to draw conclusions about the information they contain, increasingly with the aid of specialized systems and software. Data analytics is widely used in business to enable organizations make better business decisions and by scientists and researchers to verify or disprove scientific hypotheses. First step in DA is to gather the data that we want to analyse. {%} Prior to session two, complete all required readings and review the materials contained in the pre-workshop section of LMS. {%} - Develop an understanding on Data Analytics and it’s relevance to business strategy planning and decision-making. - Develop ability to collect required data to help to drive business planning and strategy. - Consider the characteristics of a successful questionnaire. {%} To undertake study for this topic, it is recommended that you read the Learning Module (Topic 2 (see LMS)). {%}_

## Секция 6 — Session 3 - Analysing the Business Environment

_Broad environmental factors impact upon all businesses. Inflation, global warming, political unrest and demographic shifts in a local community influence economies, businesses and the way we live our lives. The challenge for businesses seeking to make sense of what is often a very long and detailed list of ‘macro’ and often ‘micro’ local environmental factors is identifying which of these is most profoundly shaping success or failure in the present and which will shape success or failure in the future. {%} Prior to session three, complete all required readings and review the materials contained in the pre-workshop section of LMS. {%} - Review the macro drivers of environmental change. - Explore the scenario planning process and consider how this can impact upon key business decisions. {%} To undertake study for this topic, it is recommended that you read the Learning Module (Topic 3 (see LMS)). {%}_

## Секция 7 — Session 4 - Analysing the Business Itself

_The main purpose of analysing the firm is to pinpoint its resources and investigate whether these are effectively utilised in building competitive advantage. This is an important step in establishing a company’s core competencies and thus aligning it towards these activities. Upon establishing its core competencies, the firm should then move to establish its core activities. Through this analysis, the firm is able to identify those activities within the value chain that the business is the strongest. {%} Prior to session four, complete all required readings and review the materials contained in the pre-workshop section of LMS. {%} - Explore the firm’s resources and how these can be used to achieve a competitive advantage. - Evaluate the core competencies of a firm. {%} To undertake study for this topic, it is recommended that you read the Learning Module (Topic 4 (see LMS)). {%}_

## Секция 8 — Session 5 - Analysing The Industry

_When working in business, one must have a good understanding of the industry in which the firm is involved in as well as a sound knowledge of its competitors. To do this, several key questions should be asked and then carefully answered: 1. What is the current size and what are the major trends in the industry? 2. What are the main competitive forces? 3. Who are the firm’s competitors and what are their relative strengths? 4. How should the firm’s strategy be adapted to respond to industry changes? {%} Prior to session five, complete all required readings and review the materials contained in the pre-workshop section of LMS. {%} - Review the macro/ micro drivers of industry change. - Explore the competitive environment and the competitors in an industry. - An assessment of change. {%} To undertake study for this topic, it is recommended that you read the Learning Module (Topic 5 (see LMS)). {%}_
