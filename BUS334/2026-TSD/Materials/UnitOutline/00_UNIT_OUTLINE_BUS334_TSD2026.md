## 1 Unit Details

Welcome to: **BUS334 Business Analytics**

### 1.1 Unit Overview

Understanding how a business functions can be challenging. Producing a product and service, profiling your customers and developing a marketing strategy might seem straightforward but the rationale behind any of these actions can be complex.

This unit helps students understand how business analytics can guide and inform business decisions. In particular it focuses on developing one’s ability to collect the data, analyse it and use the results of analysis in practice. Workshops of this course are very practical and help students develop analytical skills and be able to work on various business analysis tasks.

Finally, this unit offers students challenging but exciting opportunities to enhance their business management skills, work collaboratively and creatively.

### 1.2 Learning Outcomes

This unit enables you to demonstrate your achievement of the following unit learning outcomes.

| Unit Learning Outcomes |
| --- |
| 1. Explain how the internal and external factors affect organisations and how they can be managed |
| 2. Locate and analyse business data to inform decision-making |
| 3. Analyse business and its environment and apply this understanding to practice |
| 4. Develop ability to create value for an organisation by using visual storytelling with data |
| 5. Use professional, interpersonal and communication skills to collect and present data |

### 4.2 Assessment information

#### 4.2.1 Assessment 1 - Engagement (Individual 10%)

**ASSESSMENT DESCRIPTION**

Your engagement will be assessed based on your class participation and workshop activities. Your class participation will be assessed for your contribution to the classes throughout the unit. You are expected to participate in each session and complete workshop activities. The Lecturer(s) will observe and note your contribution, including asking questions, answering questions, and engaging in discussion.

#### 4.2.2 Assessment 2 - Questionnaire Development (Individual 20%)

**ASSESSMENT DESCRIPTION**

The task is to individually develop a questionnaire to investigate a certain business idea. You are allowed to choose a business question for which to create a questionnaire. For example, you could create a questionnaire to investigate whether a grocery store could work at Murdoch campus or to investigate whether selling alcoholic beverages at Starbucks is a viable business idea. Before starting to work on questions, please confirm your topic with the local teaching staff.

**GUIDELINES for SUCCESS**

The questionnaire has to contain at least 20 questions and cover all the required information for you to examine whether your business idea could actually work. Make sure all questions are relevant, clear and measurable. This questionnaire may later be used to collect the data in Assignment 3.

**HOW TO SUBMIT**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Weighting | This assignment section accounts for 20% of the overall assessment in this unit. A penalty of 10% per day will be applied for late submissions. |
| Due | Session 4 |
| Submission | Online, via LMS |
| Format | Written questionnaire in Word or PDF format |

**HOW IT IS ASSESSED (summary)**

You will be assessed on these key criteria:

- The required number of questions developed (20)
- Relevance of questions for your business idea

- Presentation of the questionnaire
- English language use

#### 4.2.3 Assessment 3 - Data Collection and Preliminary Data Analysis (Individual 30%)

**ASSESSMENT DESCRIPTION**

The task is to collect data using the questionnaire developed in Assignment 2 and conduct some data analysis. **Please note**: although you will work with your group members on the choice of topic and the development of the joint questionnaire, the data analysis part should be conducted and submitted individually.

**GUIDELINES for SUCCESS**

1. Form team consisting of 4~ 5 members from the same class.
2. Inform your group information (i.e. members’ names and IDs) to the local teaching staff and assign on LMS.
3. Look at individual questionnaires developed in Assignment 2 and choose the business idea that your group agrees to work on.
4. Work together to compile a questionnaire consisting of the 20 best questions for the selected business idea. Try to incorporate the feedback received from Assignment 2.
5. Use the SurveyMonkey tool (or another tool) to add your questions and send the questionnaire to potential respondents. Alternatively, you could print out the questionnaire and hand it out to your respondents. Make sure that your respondents are relevant to your business idea. For example, if you’re investigating whether a grocery store is a good idea on campus – your respondents should be Murdoch University’s students.
6. Each person please collect at least 15 responses (respondents should answer all questions – if you have some questionnaires that are not fully answered, please collect more responses that are fully answered). Briefly summarise the results (your questions, number of respondents, and main findings) and upload them to LMS. You can choose to only use the responses collected by yourself or the joint dataset collected by all group members together – Note: please complete your data analysis individually in this assignment, which will also be marked individually.

**HOW TO SUBMIT**

| Weighting | This assignment section accounts for 30% of the overall assessment in this unit. A penalty of 10% per day will be applied for late submissions. |

#### 4.2.4 Assessment 4 - Data Analysis and Presentation (Group) (40%)

**ASSESSMENT DESCRIPTION**

Using the knowledge obtained and skills developed throughout this unit, students thoroughly analyse the data that they have collected in Assignment 3. Analysis should be presented in a clear and compelling manner. This presentation should be face to face. The main goal is to use the data to help a business make informed decisions. This task should be completed in pairs.

**GUIDELINES for SUCCESS**

1. Work with the same group as in Assignment 3.
2. Combine the responses each person collected in Assignment 3 and generate the final data set for this group assignment (this step is not needed if group members have jointly collected data in assignment 3)
3. Analyse the data collected - Does it confirm the viability of your business idea? Compare your individual analysis in Assignment 3, and conduct your analysis together in this assignment. Further analysis may be needed which have been missed in Assignment 3 - attempt to incorporate the feedback each of you have received from Assignment 3 in your data analysis here.
4. Imagine that you have to do a pitch for an investor (or your boss) and present your findings. This presentation will be delivered in person in class.
5. You can be as creative as you wish, to ensure that your results are clearly and accurately presented. You may use slides, whiteboards, posters, or any other supporting materials to help communicate your message effectively.

Other important guidelines:

- The duration of the presentation should be up to 15~20 minutes, each student present at least 3 minutes and no longer than 5 minutes;
- This presentation will be delivered in person in class by group;
- Make sure you introduce yourselves (state your given name, surname and student ID) at the beginning of the presentation;
- Speak clearly, be informative and engaging.

**HOW TO SUBMIT**

| --- | --- |

| **Due** | Session 13/14 |
| **Submission** | Please submit the supporting material (e.g. PPT slides) 24 hrs before the presentation, via the submission portal on LMS |
| **Peer Evaluation** | Each student, please make sure to submit your peer evaluation form independently and individually. |

**HOW IT IS ASSESSED (summary)**

You will be assessed on these key criteria:

- Compelling nature of your presentation
- Clarity of communication / English language use
- Ability to answer the question with your analysis
- Overall level of preparedness
- Level of understanding of the unit content
- Creativity

The assessment includes a moderation process to ensure reliable, just, and fair outcomes.

Your assessment outcome will be provided to you via LMS.

### 4.3 Extensions and late submissions

This unit follows Murdoch policies and procedures, particularly the [Student Assessment Support Procedure](https://goto.murdoch.edu.au/StudentAssessmentSupport) and [Assessment Procedure](https://goto.murdoch.edu.au/AssessmentProcedure), with regards to assessment submission and extensions, supplementary and deferred assessment, and other expectations.

In this unit, extensions and late submissions follow these requirements:

- Requests for extensions to submission deadlines must be made before the due date to the local lecturer, in writing, providing evidence to substantiate the reasons for the request. It is at the local lecturer/unit coordinator’s discretion whether or not an extension is granted.
- Any assignment that is received after the due date and time is deemed to be late. The time and date as recorded by LMS will be used to make the determination of timeliness. Late assignments will be penalised 10% of the total available mark for each calendar day including weekends.
- Where an assignment is submitted later than one week past the due date, without a granted extension, the unit coordinator may choose to provide feedback only on the assignment and not provide a mark.

Students who feel that their disability, health condition or disability caring responsibilities may impact on their capacity to meet assessment submission are strongly advised to visit [Access and Inclusion](https://goto.murdoch.edu.au/accessibility-services) as early as possible to discuss potential needs and assistance.
