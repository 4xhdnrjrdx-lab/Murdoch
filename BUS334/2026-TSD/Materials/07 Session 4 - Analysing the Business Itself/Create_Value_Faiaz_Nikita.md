# Create Value for a Grocery Store at Murdoch University Dubai

Prepared for the BUS334 Business Model Canvas classroom activity by Faiaz and Nikita.

Scope: Key Partners, Key Activities and Key Resources, following the Create Value section on slide 8 of the Session 4 lecture. The business topic follows Ashok Mitra's email dated 7 September 2026: a grocery store at the Murdoch University Dubai campus.

These are proposed operating arrangements. Supplier agreements, campus space, demand and financial viability have not yet been confirmed.

## Key Partners

- **University administration:** agree on a suitable campus location, operating hours and delivery access.
- **Grocery wholesalers and local food suppliers:** supply snacks, drinks, basic groceries, fresh fruit and packaged meals at competitive prices.
- **Payment and inventory system providers:** support card payments, sales recording and stock tracking.
- **Maintenance and waste collection providers:** keep refrigeration and equipment working and arrange waste collection and recycling.

## Key Activities

- **Select and purchase stock:** use student feedback and, after launch, sales data to choose products and order quantities.
- **Manage deliveries and inventory:** receive and store goods, monitor stock levels and reorder popular items before they run out.
- **Maintain freshness and hygiene:** check expiry dates, rotate stock and reduce avoidable food waste.
- **Run daily store operations:** restock shelves, assist shoppers and provide a quick checkout during class breaks.
- **Adjust operations to campus demand:** plan staff shifts and deliveries around class times, and reduce orders during holidays or quieter periods.

## Key Resources

- **Campus premises:** an accessible retail space with electricity, storage and shelving.
- **Equipment:** refrigerators, a checkout terminal, a card reader and a basic inventory system.
- **People:** a store supervisor and trained staff for checkout, replenishment and customer assistance.
- **Inventory and working capital:** an initial product range and enough funds to replenish stock and cover early operating needs.
- **Information:** supplier price lists, student survey findings and, after launch, sales and stock records.

## Speaking split

### Faiaz — Key Partners and the first two Key Activities

“Our Create Value section explains what the grocery store would need to operate. First, we would need an agreement with the university for a suitable location, opening hours and delivery access. We would also need reliable grocery suppliers, payment and inventory system providers, and maintenance support. The store would use student feedback to select products, then track sales and stock levels to decide what to reorder. This would help us avoid empty shelves and buying too much of products that do not sell.”

### Nikita — Remaining Key Activities and Key Resources

“The daily activities would include restocking shelves, checking expiry dates, keeping the store clean and helping customers. Staffing and deliveries should match class times, with smaller orders during quieter periods. The main resources would be a campus retail space, shelves, refrigerators, a checkout system, trained staff and working capital. Sales and inventory records would help the store reduce waste and keep useful products available. These arrangements support the business idea, but we still need demand and cost data to assess whether it would be viable.”
