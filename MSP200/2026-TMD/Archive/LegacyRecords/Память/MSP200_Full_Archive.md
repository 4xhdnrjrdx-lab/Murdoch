### Вводный блок

### Таблица (3 колонки: Skill | Definition (Examples) | Steps you can take to develop this skill)

**Строка 1 — Self-Management**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Taking ownership for finding or creating work and increasing employability (including job search skills and establishing and maintaining professional relationships).
- Awareness of your strengths, limitations and areas that require development.
- Recognising and effectively managing your own stress levels and emotions.
- Having the resilience to cope and learn from negative experiences.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Completing "Boost your employability" modules – see "Student help" tab in your units.
- Seeking networking and career development opportunities to better understand the world of work and where might be a good fit; address any skill or knowledge gaps and plan next steps to achieve career goals.
- Practicing self-care and stress management strategies.

**Строка 2 — Initiative, Enterprise & Entrepreneurship**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Being proactive in creating a new business idea or social enterprise opportunity.
- Viewing challenges as opportunities.
- Being comfortable with uncertainty and adapting to new situations / opportunities.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Creating a "start-up" and/or operating a successful small business.
- Identifying inefficiencies in processes and making improvements.
- Entering social enterprise competitions.

**Строка 3 — Teamwork & Leadership Potential**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Working collaboratively to achieve common goals; sharing information; supporting and empowering other team members.
- Communicating respectfully and responding constructively to the opinions of others, fostering a positive and inclusive team environment.
- Leading group activities by example, exhibiting a strong work ethic; motivating others and using the different strengths within the group.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Volunteering as a student club / society, sporting club or community organisation committee member.
- Being part of a work team (i.e. part-time / casual job).
- Participating in a student team competition / challenge.
- Playing a leading role in a group project / assignment.
- Participating in a youth leadership program.

**Строка 4 — Digital Literacy & Financial Literacy**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Proficiency in various computer software systems and packages to interpret data and make informed decisions.
- Using digital technologies to develop new ideas, projects and business opportunities.
- Making use of digital learning resources and online collaboration tools.
- Negotiating salary / employment conditions / consultancy fee.
- Effectively managing your digital identity and professional online presence.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Learning how to use specialised industry software packages and tools through YouTube instructional videos, forums, industry certifications etc.
- Enrolling in an IT or Finance unit.
- Researching industry salaries, tax etc.
- Creating a LinkedIn profile or your own website / blog / e-portfolio.
- Improving personal budgeting skills.
- Understanding finance for small business / self-employment – see WA Small Business Development Corporation.

**Строка 5 — Critical / Creative Thinking & Problem Solving**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Thinking logically and critically when analysing problems to identify underlying causes; using existing knowledge from diverse sources to propose evidence based solutions.
- Proactively anticipating needs or recognising issues and developing innovative solutions.
- Knowing how to access the effectiveness of solutions and what to do when not successful.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Working in customer service and managing complaints.
- Participating in a student competition / challenge which involves a real world problem.
- Analysing case studies related to your discipline.

**Строка 6 — Communication & Interpersonal**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Communicating clearly and expressing complex ideas concisely with others both individually and when presenting to groups.
- Producing professional documents, reports, emails, assignments, essays etc. using the right structure, language and grammar.
- Being culturally aware / sensitive or proficient in another languages.
- Using active listening to develop relationships built on trust (recognising and knowledging how others may be feeling).
- Positively influencing others.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Presenting / participating in class discussions.
- Writing research reports and professional emails with university staff, workplace supervisors or industry representatives.
- Enrolling in a language unit.
- Being part of multi-cultural university and/or work team.
- Becoming a Peer Academic Coach (PAC) or Peer Assisted Study Sessions (PASS) Leader.

**Строка 7 — Learning and Applying Knowledge**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Operating independently and taking responsibility for your own learning.
- Keeping up to date with new developments in your discipline or industry of preference.
- Developing and/or applying disciplinary skills, practices and knowledge in a real world setting; reflecting on experiences to self-evaluate and identify gaps or improvement.
- Having the emotional intelligence to benefit from feedback from others.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Enrolling in a Work Integrated Learning (WIL) placement / project unit.
- Applying for paid Vacation Work / Internships / Clerkships etc.
- Volunteering in research or for a not-for-profit related to your discipline.
- Joining a Professional Association and attending PD events or programs.
- Learning reflective practice techniques.

**Строка 8 — Planning and Organisation**
_Definition (Examples):_

- Managing timelines; prioritising time and resources for optimal productivity and timeliness.
- Effective use of scheduling tools, record keeping, electronic calendars etc.
- Handling multiple conflicting demands and managing others' expectations.
- Project or event management and proactive planning for contingencies and future needs.
  _Steps to develop:_
- Juggling study and other commitments and prioritising time for each.
- Coordinating events as a student club / society committee member.
- Travelling overseas as part of a study abroad / exchange opportunity and organising independent living and study responsibilities.
- Planning group projects / assignments.
