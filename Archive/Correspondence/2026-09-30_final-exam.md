Dear students,
Please see below the TSD2026 (Trimester September 2026) final exam information :
1.      Exam Timetable:
The final exam timetable for TSD 2026 is now published on the university's website https://www.murdoch.edu.au/admin/timetables/exams/  . Please follow these steps to retrieve your exam timetable:
Step 1: Under "Select a timetable", select "Trimester S, 2026".
Step 2: Enter all your TSD 2026 enrolled unit codes in the available boxes under "Enter up to nine Unit Codes".
Step 3: Under "Select Display Options", select your preferred format for displaying your exam schedule.
Step 4: Click "Search". Your exam schedule will then be displayed, including the exam date, time, duration, and exam type. You should also check the exam aids permitted for each exam by clicking on "Exam Aids" under the relevant Closed Book or Open Book exam classification.
Please ensure that you check the Exam Venue listed under Dubai ISC to check details for your exam.
If any of your enrolled units are not displayed in the Final Exam Timetable, please refer to the relevant Unit Guide and contact your unit lecturer for clarification.
Please do not miss out any exam details as all exams are compulsory. Students are also advised to check the LMS for final exam details announced by the respective UC.
2.      Mode of Exam:
Exams require students to attend face-to-face exams on campus as mentioned in the timetable. Please refer to the exam details published on Exam Timetable to check all details.
a) Important notes for face-to-face exams:
Units with a face-to-face exam are identified in the Exam Timetable<https://www.murdoch.edu.au/admin/timetables/exams/> as Campus Written (Theory) or Campus eExam (Theory).
Campus Written

  *   Paper-based exam held at your campus.
Campus eExam

  *   Computer-based exam held at your campus.
  *   Requires you to use your mobile phone only to complete the multi-factor authentication before start of test time.
  *   Does not include 10 minutes reading time.
The specific room will be communicated to students closer to the examination period. Please make sure you follow the below final exam guidelines for face to face exams :

  *   Students are advised to arrive at the venue at least 15 min prior to start of exam.
  *   Please bring your own stationary (pens/pencils/rulers) and financial calculator (if permitted for the exam). The university WILL NOT provide any stationary or calculators to students who forget to bring their to the exam venue.
  *   Student MUST carry their Murdoch Student ID with them for the final exam. Students with no Student IDs WILL NOT be permitted to attend the exam. No digital copies of ID are allowed.
  *   Only clear water bottles and clear stationery cases are permitted inside the venue.
Additionally please check  https://www.murdoch.edu.au/mymurdoch/support-advice/student-admin/exams/sitting-exams for guidelines to be followed for on campus exams.
3.      Deferred Exam Procedure
For students who are not able to sit for any exam(s) as scheduled and have a valid reason, you may be eligible to apply for a deferred exam. A copy of the application form for Deferred Exam can be found in Murdoch's website at https://www.murdoch.edu.au/mymurdoch/support-advice/student-admin/exams/deferred-assessments. The application procedure and other necessary details about deferred exams can be found in the same link. Applications must be submitted to Murdoch University directly at deferred@murdoch.edu.au<mailto:deferred@murdoch.edu.au>  within 3 University working days from the exam date.
4.      Examination Misconduct
Examination misconduct refers to the breaching of the Murdoch University and Dubai Campus regulations during the examination. All incidents of cheating, attempted cheating and plagiarism are taken very seriously at Murdoch University and Dubai Campus as we do not compromise on education standards. Any students found in cheating or plagiarism (e.g. copy/cut and paste the answers without paraphrase) during the course of the examination will be considered as committed the academic misconduct, and penalties for examination misconduct will apply accordingly.
We wish you all the best for your exams.
