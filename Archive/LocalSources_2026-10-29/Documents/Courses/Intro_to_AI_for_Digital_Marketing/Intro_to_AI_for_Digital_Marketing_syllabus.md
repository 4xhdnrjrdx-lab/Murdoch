## About this course

Welcome to a joint course offered by Galileo University and Davidson College. In this course, we delve into the dynamic synergy between Artificial Intelligence (AI) and digital marketing. Over the span of two modules, we embark on a journey through the evolution of AI and its pivotal role in reshaping modern marketing strategies.

Beginning with an overview of how AI has revolutionized digital marketing, we will cover its applications in retail, entertainment, and customer engagement. Topics include fundamental AI concepts, considerations for successful integration, and ethical implications, highlighting the importance of aligning AI with business goals to drive growth and enhance customer experiences.

Additionally, we explore the exciting realm of generative AI and its potential in enhancing marketing efforts. From generating engaging social media posts to crafting compelling product descriptions, you'll discover the endless possibilities offered by generative AI. However, with great power comes great responsibility, and we'll address the potential risks associated with combining generative AI and digital marketing, including ethical considerations, brand consistency, and misinformation.

By the end of this course, you'll emerge with a comprehensive understanding of how to leverage generative AI and master prompt engineering to craft compelling marketing messages that resonate with your audience. You'll also gain practical insights and strategies to navigate the evolving landscape of AI-driven digital marketing with confidence.

---

## What you'll learn

- Understand the fundamental concepts of AI and its role in modern marketing practices
- Explore real-life applications of AI in marketing
- Evaluate the key factors for successful integration of AI into marketing strategies
- Introduce generative AI and its applications in digital marketing.
- Showcase real-world applications and case studies demonstrating AI's transformative impact on marketing.
- Discuss risks and challenges associated with combining generative AI and digital marketing.
- Emphasize the importance of effective prompts in guiding AI-generated content and enhancing marketing outcomes.
- Master advanced prompt engineering techniques for optimizing AI-generated content for specific marketing objectives.

## Grading Policy

The final grade for this course is given by a series of short quizzes that represent **60% of the total course grade**, and a **final proctored exam that represents the remaining 40%**. The course is approved with 70 pts.

The verified certificate is obtained with a **grade equal to or greater than 70 points.**

**Quizzes** are at the end of each lesson; these DO NOT provide the correct answers, only the grade obtained. The proctored final exam evaluates all the content; the aim of this exam is to demonstrate what you learned in this course.

**Grade breakdown:**

- Quiz 1 + Quiz 2 → 60% of overall course grade
- Final proctored exam → 40%
- Total → 100% (pass = 70 pts)
