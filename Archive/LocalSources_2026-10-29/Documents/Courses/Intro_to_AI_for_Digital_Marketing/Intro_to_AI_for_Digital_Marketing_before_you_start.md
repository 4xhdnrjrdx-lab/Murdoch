## How to follow screencast (demo) videos?

Why use "videos" to give the instruction? Well, here are some reasons:

- Students can watch a lesson anytime, anywhere.
- Students have complete control of the lesson, which means they can repeat material or skip ahead as needed.
- Students learn by example, seeing every step in great detail. Learning by watching the professor move through the steps enables them to take note.

### So, how do we take advantage of this instruction method?

- Watching a video requires a commitment of time.
- You can pause or rewind the video, so take advantage of this characteristic and move to your own pace. Something a classroom can not always offer.
- When it is about programming, open the code file and do the example by watching the professor move through the steps.
- Take notes.

Basically, when it is about programming, we recommend you to do these four steps:

**Watch the video → Pause the video, Think! Reflect! → Open the code files → Do it in your own development environment. Practice! Practice! — LEARNING BY DOING.**

*(The above four steps are shown as a process diagram on the course page.)*

## To be successful in this course the participant is expected to:

- Study all the lecture videos that make up each lesson; in them, the expert develops all the topics that were described on the syllabus.
- Review the complementary readings or external references related to the topic.
- Perform all the activities in each lesson (questionnaires, activities, final project).
- You will learn by doing — *"the more you practice, the more you can, the more you want to, the more you enjoy it."* Practice means to perform over and over again. Each lesson comes with its sample code files, and concise instructions for how to build that code by yourself.
- Think! Reflect! Do! and Practice! about what you are learning.
- Participate in forums; your participation creates an active and dynamic learning community, in which you will find support and at the same time you will have the opportunity to contribute with your knowledge, experience and help your classmates.

> *Making your own planning and setting intermediate deadlines in advance of the deadline will allow you to achieve your goals.*

## Using Forums (Guidelines)

The forums that are part of this course are a unique opportunity to interact with students from all over the world. The community is diverse in experience, knowledge, language and culture. Forums are an excellent means of communication to post your doubts, comments or concerns. But remember, you are the one who enriches the forums with your participation and you make the community of the course stay active.

If you know the answer to a question posted by one of your course mates, answer it. Even if you do not know the exact answer or you only have some idea, you can contribute to build a solution with the other participants.

### Some very important rules to keep in mind:

- Respect the established categories. If you have any doubts about lesson 1, put your doubt in that section; this will help those who are studying that lesson help you to solve your doubt.
- Do not create new threads. This makes it difficult for your message to be displayed and consequently you may not receive support from your course mates and/or team members. Always try to place your doubts at forums created by the course team and which are marked as fixed.
- Be as detailed as possible. If you're having problems, it's very helpful if you post your code and the errors you're having.
- If you solved a problem, post what you did — someone else might be having the same problem as you.
- Be always courteous and concise in your messages; remember the rules of [netiquette](http://www.albion.com/netiquette/corerules.html).

In this course, we hope that you will learn many new things and, by the end of the course, you can take advantage of what you learned in your daily life.

**What do we expect from you?** Participate actively and support your peers, participate at least once a week, and always keep in mind the rules of netiquette.

**What can you expect from the course team?** A member of our team will review the forums periodically to answer your questions and concerns.
