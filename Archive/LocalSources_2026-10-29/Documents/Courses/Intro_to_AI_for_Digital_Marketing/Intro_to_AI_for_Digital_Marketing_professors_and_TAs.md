## Miguel A. Morales-Chan, Ph.D.

**Role:** Director Digital Education – GES. Vice-principal Von Neumann Institute. Project Manager GalileoX and ProfXXI at Universidad Galileo.

Miguel Morales holds a Ph.D. in Information and Knowledge Engineering from the University of Alcalá de Henares, Spain. He also has a Master's degree in Educational Planning and Management from Universidad Galileo, and a Master's in Production and Management of e-Learning Projects from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Spain.

Currently, he serves as the Director of the Digital Education Area and Coordinator of MOOC production at Universidad Galileo for edX. He is the author of the book *'Discover the Power of ChatGPT in the Classroom: 90 Templates for Creating Effective Prompts for Teaching'* and a Senior Researcher specializing in MOOCs, Artificial Intelligence in Education, STEAM, and Micro-Credentials.

Miguel has led the production of more than 50 successful MOOCs at edX with more than 1,000,000 students enrolled in the different cohorts. He has been responsible for the design and production of each MOOC, as well as the development of educational innovations.

As a consultant in innovation and educational technology projects, he is recognized as an expert in digital education.

## Rocael Hernández Rizzardini, Ph.D.

**Role:** Director GES – Digital University. Director Von Neumann Institute. CEO Corporation IT Industry at Universidad Galileo.

Rocael Hernández-Rizzardini combines academic excellence with real-world business insights.

At Galileo University, Rocael Hernández-Rizzardini serves as the Director of the GES department, steering its focus towards the concept of the Digital University. Under his leadership, diverse teams specialize in various facets of the digital domain: from learning and marketing to communications, technology, and research. Each team collaborates to redefine and reshape how education is approached in the digital age.

In addition to his role in the GES department, Dr. Hernández-Rizzardini is also the Director of the Von Neumann Institute. There, crafting innovative online degrees, ensuring that education remains both accessible and cutting-edge for students globally.

He envisions a "Future University" that aligns with today's technological strides, bridging the gap between traditional education and the digital realm.

As the CEO of a dynamic enterprise with extensive project experience spanning the USA, Canada, Europe, and Latam, Dr. Hernández-Rizzardini synergizes his academic and research background to champion purposeful innovation. Drawing from diverse customer engagements across these regions, he harnesses his deep-rooted passion for digital transformation, viewing it as an instrumental solution to contemporary challenges.
