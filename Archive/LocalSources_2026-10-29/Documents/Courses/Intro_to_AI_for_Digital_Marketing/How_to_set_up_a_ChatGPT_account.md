# How to Set Up a ChatGPT Account

> **Source / origin note.** External course material — *not* Murdoch. Screenshot UI states are summarised in italics in brackets.
>
> *Note: product details (free GPT-3.5, GPT-4 subscription) reflect the original 2024 version of the guide and have since changed on OpenAI's side — the signup flow is the durable part.*

---

This tutorial will guide you through the steps to create a ChatGPT account. Prior to beginning, ensure that you possess a valid email address.

**This is what we'll address:**
- Initiating the Signup Process
- Email Verification
- Optional Subscription Plans
- Testing ChatGPT

---

## Step 1: Initiating the Signup Process

Click here to access ChatGPT: https://chat.openai.com/

*[Screenshot: ChatGPT landing page with a "Get started" box containing "Log in" and "Sign up" buttons.]*

## Step 2

Select the "Sign Up" button. This action will lead you to the "Create your account" page, where you'll need to input your email address and generate a password.

Please note that you have the option to either enter an email or log in conveniently using existing Microsoft, Google, or Apple accounts.

## Step 3

In the "Email address" field, enter your email address, then click the **Continue** button.

*[Screenshot: "Create your account" page with the Email address field, a Continue button, and "Continue with Google / Microsoft Account / Apple" options.]*

If OpenAI recognizes the email as valid, a "Password" text field will appear. Generate a robust password that hasn't been used elsewhere in the provided space. Afterward, click the "Continue" button to move forward.

> **Security-Check!** Remember to create a strong password. Use 15–20 characters, and at least: two uppercase characters, two lowercase characters, two special characters, and two numbers in the password generated.

## Step 4: Email Verification

Once you've filled out the signup form, please review your email inbox for a verification message from OpenAI or ChatGPT. Open the email and select the "Verify Email" button or link. This action will redirect you to the ChatGPT website, where you can confirm your email address.

*[Screenshot: "Verify your email" dialog — "We sent an email to… Click the link inside to get started," with a "Resend email" option.]*

## Step 5: Subscription Plans (Optional)

ChatGPT using GPT model 3.5 is free to use. However, OpenAI offers a subscription plan to access GPT-4 and added features for the average user, as well as special pricing for Enterprise users. Browse through the available plans and choose one that best suits your needs. Enter your payment details if needed and complete the purchase. A list of available plans can be found here: https://openai.com/enterprise

## Step 6: Testing ChatGPT

Once your account setup is complete, you'll typically land on the ChatGPT interface. You can type a message in the input box (usually at the bottom of the interface) to interact with ChatGPT.

For example, you can type into the message box:

> **You:** Hello ChatGPT. Can you show me the structure of a good prompt?

Press "Enter" to send your message and wait for ChatGPT to reply.

### Example reply — tips for creating an effective prompt

> **ChatGPT:** Certainly! A well-structured prompt is crucial for getting the desired output from a language model like ChatGPT. Here are some tips for creating an effective prompt:

1. **Be Clear and Specific:**
   - Clearly articulate what you want the model to do. Provide details about the task or information you are seeking.
   - Instead of vague requests, provide specific instructions.
2. **Use Contextual Information:**
   - Include relevant context to help the model understand the context of your query or task.
   - If applicable, mention any background information or constraints that should be considered.
3. **Ask Direct Questions:**
   - Frame your prompt as a direct question when seeking specific information or answers.
   - Clearly outline the type of response you're looking for.
4. **Provide Examples:**
   - Offer examples or specify the format you want the answer in.
   - This can help guide the model in generating responses that meet your expectations.
5. **Use Keywords:**
   - Incorporate key terms or keywords related to your query to help guide the model's understanding.
   - If there are specific terms or concepts crucial to your prompt, make sure to include them.
6. **Break Down Complex Tasks:**
   - If your prompt involves multiple steps or subtasks, break them down into simpler components.
   - This can improve the model's ability to comprehend and respond accurately.
7. **Specify the Tone or Style:**
   - Indicate the desired tone or style of the response. For example, if you want a formal or casual reply, mention it in the prompt.
