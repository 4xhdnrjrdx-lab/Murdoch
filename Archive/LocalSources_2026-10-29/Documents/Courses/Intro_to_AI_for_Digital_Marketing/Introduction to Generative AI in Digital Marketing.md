facilitating a wide range of applications. Conversational agents, such as ChatGPT,
Google Bard, Bing, exemplify the practical applications of LLMs. These agents, whether
text-based or voice-activated, utilize natural language processing to interact with users,

from being enigmatic 'black boxes,' LLMs are intricate yet accessible tools that marketers
can utilize to push the boundaries of digital innovation and engagement.
