## Program structure

The Professional Certificate program consists of **three courses**, each focused on an aspect of integrating AI into digital marketing:

### Course 1: Intro to AI for Digital Marketing

This initial course explores how AI is transforming digital marketing. Participants will learn:

- Fundamental concepts of AI and its applications in marketing.
- Ethical considerations and challenges in implementing AI.
- Strategies for integrating AI into digital marketing campaigns.
- Use of generative AI to enhance content creation and marketing.

Upon completion, students will master AI tools that will allow them to optimize their digital marketing strategies, aligning their business objectives with the potential of AI.

### Course 2: AI Technology for Marketing Optimization

In this second course, participants will learn to apply AI principles and technologies to optimize their marketing campaigns:

- Predictive analytics to anticipate consumer behaviors.
- Real-time optimization of advertising campaigns.
- Advanced personalization of customer interactions.
- Advanced segmentation methods and market analysis using AI.

From market research to social media scheduling, advanced AI-driven methods will be taught to enable students to implement innovative solutions to improve customer interaction and increase conversion rates.

### Course 3: AI for Marketing Strategy and Creativity

This practical course focuses on the strategic and creative use of AI in digital marketing. Participants will learn to:

- Use AI for competitor analysis and customer segmentation.
- Automate marketing campaigns throughout the consumer journey.
- Create effective marketing strategies and personalize content using AI tools.
- Optimize graphic design, video, and landing pages to improve interaction and conversion rates.

It empowers professionals to integrate creativity and technology, providing the necessary tools to stand out in the competitive current market and offering a significant job advantage.
