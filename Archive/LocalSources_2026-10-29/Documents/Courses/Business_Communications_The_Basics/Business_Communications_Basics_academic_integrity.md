# Business Communications – The Basics — Learner Academic Integrity

> **Source / origin note.** External course material — *not* Murdoch. From the edX course **DoaneX CMS-3161x — "Business Communications: The Basics"** (Business Communications collection). Academic integrity policy page. Text reproduced verbatim for study reference.

---

Learner academic integrity is the commitment from learners to demonstrate honest, moral behaviors in their academic careers. You are expected to maintain the highest level of integrity by submitting honest academic work. Therefore, you are required to read the following policies and acknowledge that you will abide by them.

## Policies you must comply with

You must ensure that you are familiar and will comply with the following policies:

- Submit only work that is your own original work, or properly acknowledge the ideas, interpretations, words, and works of others.
- Avoid submitting work that was previously submitted for another course.
- Avoid working with other learners on course work, unless the instructor has allowed it.
- Comply with discussion policies and procedures by not posting inappropriate or illegal content.
- Include appropriate citations as needed in all submitted coursework.
- Avoid lending your original work to others for any reason.
- Comply with assessment policies and procedures for proctored exams.

## Plagiarism policy

DoaneX takes learner academic integrity seriously. In order to protect the integrity of this course, we actively check for plagiarism in all submitted work. We are not lenient about plagiarism. If you are caught submitting plagiarized work, you will receive a zero for the assignment and a note stating that your work was plagiarized — and this is your one and only warning to not do it again. If you are caught a second time, you will receive a zero for the course.

If you have any questions regarding learner academic integrity, you are encouraged to reach out to the team at doanex@doane.edu.
