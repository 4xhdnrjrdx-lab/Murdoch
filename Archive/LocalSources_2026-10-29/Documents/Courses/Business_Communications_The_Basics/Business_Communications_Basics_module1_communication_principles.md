To begin to understand communication, we first need to realize that there are certain **principles of communication**.

**Communication is unavoidable.** No matter what you do, you are communicating in some fashion. As we will learn later, our facial expression, posture, clothing, and many other factors communicate our attitude. We can even send messages when we are not present — so not showing up to an event or leaving the room can communicate meaning to others. So it is always important to think of the unintentional messages that we may be sending.

**Communication is also strategic.** Almost all of our communication is done to achieve a goal.

In a business setting we have what some call **instrumental goals** — these are messages that are aimed at accomplishing a task at hand. An example of this could be your boss telling you that they need a report done by 5pm, and your instrumental response is to ask how long the report needs to be. This is pretty straightforward. Not everyone will put their goals outright for you; think about a time when someone says "would you look at the time" or checks their watch — they didn't state their goal, but you probably have a good idea that they want to wrap things up or leave, depending on the situation.

There are also **relational communication goals.** These are messages that help shape relationships. Almost all messages contain both instrumental and relational communication, so it is not only the context of your message but how you present your message that will help in your communication.

**Communication is irreversible.** Yes, we have all had that moment where we said something and wished almost instantly that we could take it back — and as we know, that is not possible.

**Communication is a process.** Just because you receive a message does not mean that the communication stops there. So much will depend on the message that you received and how you respond.

**Communication is not a panacea.** It cannot and does not always solve all problems or get you what you want.
