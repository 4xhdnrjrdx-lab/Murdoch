## Welcome to Business Communication – The Basics Course!

Learners in this course will engage with the fundamentals of business communication. Learners will discover different communication styles and how to address them in a business setting. Learners will also assess their listening styles and emotional intelligence, and how both can affect their communication skills.

## Learning outcomes

At the end of this course learners will be able to:

- Define communication.
- Examine the communication principles.
- Identify the communication model.
- Identify ways to effectively listen.
- Identify their communication style.
- Examine emotional intelligence and its use in communication.

## Program context

This is one course in the three-course **Business and Professional Communications for Success** MicroBachelors Program. It is recommended to complete them in order unless you have a background in these areas already and feel comfortable skipping ahead. However, all three courses are required to obtain the program certificate and the college credits.

1. Business Communications – The Basics
2. Business Writing Techniques
3. Professional Business Presentations (includes a comprehensive program exam)
