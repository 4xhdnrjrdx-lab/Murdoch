## Course Description

Learners in this course will engage with the fundamentals of business communication. Learners will discover different communication styles and how to address them in a business setting. Learners will also assess their listening styles and emotional intelligence, and both can affect their communication skills.

## Learning Outcomes

- Define communication.
- Examine the communication principles.
- Identify the communication model.
- Identify ways to listen effectively.
- Identify their communication style.
- Examine emotional intelligence and its use in communication.
