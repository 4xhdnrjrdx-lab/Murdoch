# Business Communications – The Basics — Module 1 Overview

> **Source / origin note.** External course material — *not* Murdoch. From the edX course **DoaneX CMS-3161x — "Business Communications: The Basics"** (Business Communications collection). **Module 1 — Communication, What is it?** Text reproduced verbatim for study reference.

---

Communication is embedded in our everyday interactions and part of "home" life and "work" life. All interactions communicate something. Communication can be broken down into a simple formula that aids us in examining communication with a new set of lenses. Culture is just one thing that colors how we communicate. In this module, we are beginning to examine the components of communication and how culture impacts them.

Communication can look simple and effortless, especially when it goes well. But every communication exchange is affected by principles that we can't always see or may not even know about. Understanding the communication process better can help us make strategic choices that help achieve both personal and organizational goals.

## Learning Objectives

- Discuss the role of communication in the learners' work environment.
- Identify and describe the communication model.
- Explain how the hidden dimensions of culture affect communication (including yours) in a culturally diverse workforce.
