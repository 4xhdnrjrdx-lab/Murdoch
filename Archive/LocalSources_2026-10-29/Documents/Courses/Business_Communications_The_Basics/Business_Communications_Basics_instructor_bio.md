## Debora Sepich, EdD, MBA

Debora Sepich is an entrepreneur turned educator who has spent the last 15 years blurring the lines between the world of work and the halls of education. Debora founded Dolphin Software, an environmental health and safety software company focused on supporting pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, and other highly regulated industries focus on protecting people, and the planet while also making sustainable profits. Debora is delighted to be marrying her years of applied business experience with online education.
