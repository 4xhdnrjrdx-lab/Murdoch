# Business Communications – The Basics — Getting Started as the Self-Paced Learner

> **Source / origin note.** External course material — *not* Murdoch. From the edX course **DoaneX CMS-3161x — "Business Communications: The Basics"** (Business Communications collection). Onboarding / how-to-navigate page. Text reproduced verbatim for study reference.

---

By taking this course, you have entered into an exciting world of self-paced, online learning! If you're new to edX, we recommend that you view the [edX DemoX](https://www.edx.org/course/demox-edx-demox-1-0) course. Here is some additional information to get you started.

This course is structured into **modules, sections, and units**. Each module contains a combination of video content and interactive activities that cover the topic areas for that module. Each module contains all of the material that you will need to learn the topics. We have also included links to additional sources of information if you are interested in digging even deeper into these topics.

To navigate through the sections of a module, use the navigation bar at the top of the display window. You can use the left and right arrows to move through the units, and you can hover your mouse pointer over the unit icon to see the unit title.

As noted above, each module in this course will have some combination of video content, activities, and assessments. The modules are meant to be taken in order, but you can skip around in the resource document sections, or even skip over a topic if you have already mastered the material in a particular unit.

## Graded Assignments

It will be up to you to complete the graded assignments in each of the modules. If you get stuck, don't give up! There is enough material in the modules to help you complete them and your hard work will pay off.

## Other Resources

The [edX Student FAQ](https://www.edx.org/about/student-faq) will answer many questions about the edX student experience. Review the questions, so if you encounter any issues during the course, you can refer back to this document for help.

## Stick with it!

We encourage you to take the course from beginning to end to get the full learning experience. Some modules may be very easy for you and others may be harder. But each module should offer something of value. There is nothing like that feeling of accomplishment when you complete something from beginning to end, so hang in there and enjoy the course!

## Navigate the Course

The navigation bar along the top will take you anywhere in the course. To access the complete course breakdown, select the "Course" tab at the top of the page. From here you can select a specific unit to start.

Since this is a self-paced course, instructors are not able to provide active feedback to individual students as they would in a smaller course. If you need help with the course, select the "Discussion" tab and post a question. To get help with a technical problem, select "Help" to send a message to edX Student Support.

Please remember to be polite and respectful in all discussion boards. Posting inappropriate or illegal content will not be tolerated.
