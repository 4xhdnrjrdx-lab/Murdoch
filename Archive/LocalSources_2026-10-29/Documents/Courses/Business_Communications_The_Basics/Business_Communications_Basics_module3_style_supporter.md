# Business Communications – The Basics — Communication Styles: Supporter (video transcript)

> **Source / origin note.** External course material — *not* Murdoch. From the edX course **DoaneX CMS-3161x — "Business Communications: The Basics"** (Module 3, "Communication Styles"). Transcript reproduced verbatim for study reference; player line numbers omitted as non-content.

---

Someone with a **supporter** communication style is going to be loyal, cooperative, and sensitive. They are consistent, action-oriented, and honest. They are good listeners and are patient and friendly. However, a supporter may be thin-skinned, or may be hurt easily. They typically do not talk too much, and they try hard to please everyone.

## How to communicate with a Supporter

When you communicate with a supporter, you will want to be sincere and offer personal comments. Ask them questions and listen attentively. You do not want to be overpowering or very aggressive. Supporters also do not like one-way communication. A supporter is always going to worry about how people feel.

## Example

Let's say that a member from the corporate office is giving a presentation on recent sales trends. The supporter would be paying attention to their co-workers' reactions to what the speaker is saying — just as much if not more so than to what the speaker is actually saying. So how can the speaker tailor their speech to better resonate with this listener? They simply need to add in some emotional language to their speech.

Instead of saying "Hey, sales are positive this quarter" and leaving it at that, they should smile and say something like:

> "Hey guys, you've really made me proud — sales are positive this quarter, and that's the first time in three years that we've done that, and it's all thanks to you guys' hard work. And I honestly don't really have any critiques that I can give you; you all did a fantastic job. So instead of me lecturing you for the next 20 minutes, I was wondering, could we make this presentation a little more interactive? I was wondering if any of you had any ideas for the next quarter that you'd like to try out, or if there's anything that's been hindering you here at work that I can help you with?"

This will provide the supporter with pride not only in their work but also their coworkers' work as well.
