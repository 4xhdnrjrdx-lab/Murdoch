## Meet Your Instructor: Michael Trusov (video transcript)

Hello, my name is Michael Trusov. I'm a marketing professor at the Smith School of Business, University of Maryland. I'm also an academic director of the Master's in Marketing Analytics Program. At the University of Maryland, I teach courses on digital marketing and digital analytics in undergraduate and graduate programs.

Prior to joining academia, I worked in the software development field, specializing in marketing automation and internet applications. I'm an active academic researcher and do work in the domain of digital marketing. In particular, I'm interested in search engines, web analytics, social media, and social networks, as well as recommendation systems. My research has been published in top academic journals and won several major research awards. I also have ongoing collaboration with a number of companies doing business online.

I very much look forward to the opportunity of sharing my academic and industry experience with you in our courses.

## Meet Your Instructor: Liye Ma (video transcript)

Hi. My name is Liye Ma. I'm an Associate Professor of Marketing at the Robert H. Smith School of Business at the University of Maryland.

My research focuses on the dynamic interactions of consumers and firms on the internet, social media, and mobile platforms. I develop statistical, econometric, and machine learning methods to analyze the digital economy and use the findings to help companies develop digital marketing strategies. I have published articles in leading academic journals, such as Marketing Science, Journal of Marketing Research, Journal of Marketing, International Journal of Research in Marketing, Management Science, and Information Systems Research.

I obtained my PhD degree at Carnegie Mellon University. Prior to that, I worked for six years in the technology industry. At the Smith School, I have taught classes such as Marketing Research Methods, Data Science, Big Data and Artificial Intelligence, Action Learning Projects, and Structural Models in various programs.

Here, I'll be co-teaching, of course, *Digital Marketing Analytics: Tools and Techniques*.
