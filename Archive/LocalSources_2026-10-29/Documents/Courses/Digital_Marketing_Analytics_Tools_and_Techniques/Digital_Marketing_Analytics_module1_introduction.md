## Getting Started

In this module, we're going to introduce search engine marketing and analytical approaches and tools used in search engine optimization and paid search. We will also look at how marketers use website analytic tools to support business decision making.

*(Module 1 covers: Analytics of SEO · Analytics of Paid Search · Web Analytics.)*

## Learning Objectives

### Module 1 workload for verified learners

You should expect to spend at least 30 minutes in total watching seven short videos. In addition to the videos and readings, you will have practice questions, a few activities, and a scenario at the end of the module. These activities and scenarios are essential to helping you learn and apply the skills you will need to demonstrate and master Digital Marketing Analytics.

### Module 1 objectives

At the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Describe the importance of Search Engine Optimization (SEO) in digital environments.
- Identify the differences between on-page and off-page factors.
- Determine the Key Performance Indicators (KPI) to guide analysis and enable search campaign result comparisons.
- Describe the importance of Paid Search Marketing (PPC) in digital environments. (PPC Analytics decision rules)
- Identify the key factors affecting Paid Search ad placement on search engine results pages, including: Keywords, Bids, Ad Copy, and Landing Page.
- Describe the importance of website analytic tools in digital environments.
