Dear Students,
This is a quick reminder regarding your BUS293 Organizational Behaviour Team Contract Form.
You are required to upload the Team Contract Form on the LMS under your Private/Team Discussion Forum, not the main discussion forum.
The purpose of this is to maintain a proper record of:

  *
Team contracts
  *
Division of labour
  *
Team meetings and attendance
  *
Drafts
  *
Individual submissions and contributions to the team

Uploading the form in the Private/Team Discussion Forum will help ensure that each team’s records are kept together and can be reviewed when required.
If you have uploaded your Team Contract Form in the wrong place, kindly delete it and re-upload it under the correct Private/Team Discussion Forum.
