# Job Satisfaction & Employee Behaviour
BUS293 Organisational Theory and Behaviour
Topic: Job Satisfaction, Emotional Intelligence, Stress & Job Design

<!-- Slide number: 2 -->
# Job Satisfaction
How employees evaluate their jobs.
Dissatisfaction can lead to: Exit, Voice, Loyalty, or Neglect.
Example: A cashier unhappy with workload may resign (Exit) or suggest improvements (Voice).

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
# Emotional Intelligence (EI)
Self-awareness
Self-management
Social awareness
Relationship management
Example: A supervisor calming an upset customer while supporting staff.

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
# True Story: Satya Nadella (Microsoft)
After becoming CEO, Nadella emphasized empathy and emotional intelligence.
He transformed Microsoft's culture from internal competition to collaboration.
Result: Improved innovation and company performance.

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
# Stress at Work
Distress = harmful stress; Eustress = positive, motivating stress.
Common stressors: work overload, low control, workplace conflict.
 Example: Student balancing assignments and part-time work.

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->
# Managing Stress
Remove the stressor
Change perceptions
Seek social support
Flexible work arrangements and work-life balance help reduce stress.

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->
# Job Design Approaches
Job Specialisation – one repetitive task
Job Enlargement – more tasks added
Job Rotation – move between roles
Job Enrichment – more responsibility and autonomy

<!-- Slide number: 8 -->
# True Story: Toyota Job Rotation
Toyota rotates employees across functions.
Employees gain broader skills and understanding.
Result: Higher flexibility, teamwork, and problem-solving capability.

<!-- Slide number: 9 -->
# Job Enrichment Example
Instead of only entering customer data, an employee also follows up with customers and resolves issues.
Benefits: Meaningful work, autonomy, motivation, and satisfaction.

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->
# Key Takeaways
Emotions, stress, rewards, and job design affect satisfaction.
Emotional intelligence can be developed.
Well-designed jobs improve motivation and performance.
Organizations should support employee wellbeing.
