With regard to Assessment 2 (A2) – Team Research Report (30%), please find below a simplified explanation of the assessment requirements to assist you in understanding the task and planning your group work effectively.
The report requires you to work in teams of up to four members,
select one topic from the prescribed list,
and apply it to a suitable organisation listed on either the ASX200 or SGX.
Your report should critically analyse the challenges faced by the chosen organisation in relation to the selected topic, provide evidence-based recommendations for improvement, and demonstrate alignment with at least one of the United Nations Global Compact Principles.
The assessment also requires you to:

  *
Apply relevant organisational behaviour theories and concepts.
  *
Support your arguments with scholarly and credible sources.
  *
Use real-world organisational examples.
  *
Demonstrate teamwork, collaboration, and professional communication skills.
  *
Record all formal group communications through the LMS Team Discussion Forum.

Please note that the information provided is intended as a simplified guide only.
You are strongly recommended to carefully read the official Unit Outline (Pages 10–12) and the Detailed Report Guidelines available on the LMS, as these documents contain the complete assessment requirements, marking criteria, submission instructions, and expectations that must be followed.
It is your responsibility to ensure that your report fully complies with the requirements outlined in the Unit Outline and LMS resources.
