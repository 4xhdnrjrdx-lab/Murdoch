# Student Presentation Structure (5 Minutes per Group)

## Slide 1: Introduction to the Theory

* Name of the theory
* Founder(s)
* Year developed
* Main purpose of the theory
* Example: Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs developed by Abraham Maslow (1943) explains how human needs motivate behaviour.

## Slide 2: Key Concepts of the Theory

* Define the theory
* Explain the main concepts/components
* Use a diagram or model
* Questions to Answer:
* What does the theory explain?
* What are its main elements?

## Slide 3: How the Theory Works

* Explain the process step-by-step
* Describe how motivation or behaviour occurs according to the theory
* Example for Expectancy Theory: Effort → Performance → Reward → Motivation

## Slide 4: Workplace Example

* Provide a real-life organisational example.
* Example: A company offers performance bonuses to employees who achieve monthly sales targets.

## Slide 5: Application in Organisations

* Discuss how managers can use the theory.
* Questions:
* How can managers apply this theory?
* How can it improve employee performance?
* How can it improve engagement?

## Slide 6: Advantages/Strengths

* Discuss 3–5 strengths.
* Example:
* Easy to understand
* Helps managers identify employee needs
* Useful for employee engagement

## Slide 7: Limitations/Criticisms

* Discuss 3–5 weaknesses.
* Example:
* Not all employees are motivated in the same way
* Difficult to measure some concepts
* Cultural differences may affect applicability

## Slide 8: Modern-Day Example

* Find a company that uses similar principles.
* Examples:
* Google
* Microsoft
* Emirates Airlines
* Amazon
* Apple
* Explain how the company applies the theory.
