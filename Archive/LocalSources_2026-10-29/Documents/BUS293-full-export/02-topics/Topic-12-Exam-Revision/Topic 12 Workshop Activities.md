Topic 12 Workshop Activities

Activity 1:
Discuss exam information as a class and individually attempt the below sample short answer question.

a) Explain the differences between the interpersonal conflict-handling styles (4 marks).
b) Use one (1) interpersonal conflict-handling style to explain how a conflict situation was, or was not, effectively resolved during your teamwork experience in BUS293 (6 marks).

Activity 2:
In groups, compile answers to ALL the critical thinking questions from the focus topics respective chapters of the textbook.

Activity 3:
In groups, discuss and report to the class your answers to the following questions for the Additional case studies:

Case Study 2: ‘Bayou Life Assurance and Waltham Finance’ p569

1. Diagnose this case in terms of merging two organizational cultures.
2. Diagnose this case in terms of organizational conflict.
3. Diagnose this case in terms of Employee attitudes
4. Diagnose this case in terms of Perceptions.

Case Study 5: The Regency Grand Hotel’.p574

1. Identify the symptoms indicating that problems exist in this case.
2. Diagnose the problems in this case using organizational behavior concepts.
3. Recommend solutions that overcome or minimize the problems and symptoms in this case.
