**Topic 8 Workshop Activities**

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.

Each team must report to the class on each of the first two activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

**Activity 1:**
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 2, 5, 6, and 8 from chapter 10 of the textbook.

**Activity 2:**
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the questions for the case study 'Resonus Corporation' from chapter 10 of the textbook.

**Activity 3:**
Team exercise. 'Deciphering the Network.'

**Activity 4:**
Team meeting. The team is to review the completed team report meets the requirements, confirm all members met final milestones in their plan for report completion, and approve the final report for submission. **This should be recorded on the team discussion forum.**

---

**Key points:**

- В начале занятия каждая команда отмечает на доске, какой critical thinking question и какой case study question будет докладывать; нужно быть готовым отвечать на вопросы одногруппников.
- По первым двум активностям докладывает каждая команда, но т.к. распределение вопросов между командами заранее не известно — нужно подготовить **все**.
- **Activity 1:** critical thinking questions **2, 5, 6, 8** из Chapter 10.
- **Activity 2:** вопросы к case study **'Resonus Corporation'** (Chapter 10).
- **Activity 3:** командное упражнение **'Deciphering the Network'**.
- **Activity 4:** team meeting — проверить, что готовый отчёт соответствует требованиям, подтвердить, что все участники выполнили финальные milestones, и одобрить отчёт к сдаче; **зафиксировать на team discussion forum.**
