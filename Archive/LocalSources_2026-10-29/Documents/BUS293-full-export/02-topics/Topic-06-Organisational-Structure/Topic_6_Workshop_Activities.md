Topic 6 Workshop Activities

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.
Each team must report to the class on each of the first two activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

Activity 1:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 3, 4, 5, and 7, from chapter 13 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the questions for the case study ‘Sloan & Wu Inc’ from chapter 13 of the textbook.

Activity 3:
Team exercise. The Club Ed Exercise

Activity 4:
Team meeting. The team is to report on progress towards milestones in their plan for report completion, make any needed adjustments to their plan for report completion, and record this on the team discussion forum.
