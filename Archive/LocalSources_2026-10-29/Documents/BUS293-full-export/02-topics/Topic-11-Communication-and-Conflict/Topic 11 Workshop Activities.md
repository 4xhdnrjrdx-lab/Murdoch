Topic 11 Workshop Activities

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.
Each team must report to the class on the first two activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

Activity 1:
In groups, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 5 and 6 from chapter 9 of the textbook, and critical thinking questions 2, 6, and 9 from chapter 11 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In groups, discuss and report to the class your answers to the questions for the case study ‘Difficult Connections’ from chapter 9 of the textbook.

Activity 3:
Team exercise. Kumquat Conflict Role Play.
