Topic 9 Workshop Activities

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.
Each team must report to the class on each of the three activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

Activity 1:
In groups, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7 from chapter 12 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In groups, discuss and report to the class your answers to the questions for the case study ‘IbexTel’ from chapter 12 of the textbook.

Activity 3:
Team exercise. Leadership Diagnostic Analysis.
