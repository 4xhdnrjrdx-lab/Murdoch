Topic 3 Workshop Activities

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question and team exercise case they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.
Each team must report to the class on each of the first three activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

Activity 1:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 2, 6, and 8 from chapter 2 of the textbook, and critical thinking questions 4, 6, and 8 from chapter 3 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the three questions for the case study ‘Bridging the two worlds’ from chapter 3 of the textbook.

Activity 3:Team exercise. Bonus team exercise.
In each of the four cases, within your teams discuss and report to the class the extent to which the company’s action in each case was ethical.

Activity 4:
Team meeting. The team is to review team report requirements, report on progress towards milestones in their plan for report completion and seek any needed clarification from the workshop tutor. This should be recorded on the team discussion forum.
