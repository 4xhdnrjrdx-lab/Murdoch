# BUS293 Team Contract

Team Number: ********\_\_******** Team Name: **************\_**************

It is a normal business practice to put into writing all agreements that are binding on the parties involved. It is suggested that you now take the time to establish the acceptable team norms and behaviours that you will enforce. It is further suggested that you decide on a conflict and dispute resolution procedure, so this is established before conflicts arise. Below is a 'team contract' that will bind all team members to what the team decides, until the team is dissolved when the final assessment is concluded.

**TEAM CONTRACT**

It is agreed that the members of this team will:

- Upload a copy of the completed and signed contract to the LMS team discussion forum.
- Accept that ****************\_**************** is the team leader and all agree to follow their directions in completing the required tasks.
- Give written warnings via the LMS team discussion forum to team members who do not meet the agreed obligations and responsibilities.
- Give a written warning to any team member who does not attend the workshop, or other scheduled team meeting, without prior notice to the team for a reason acceptable to the team.
- Give a written warning to any team member who does not submit required work to the group by the deadlines set by the team.
- Consult with the lecturer if any team member has received two written warnings.
- Remove any member from the team who receives three written warnings.
- The agreed team dispute mechanism is as follows: [пустые линии для заполнения — несколько строк]

It is freely agreed to by the parties who have **SIGNED** below, that all team members listed as signatories, are bound by their signatures to carry out all duties and obligations and in return, they bind all others to give the same assurance.

I (name)……………………………(signature)……………………… [×4 строки для подписей]

…have read and signed this understanding that the ultimate sanction of dismissal from the team will require me to do all the assignments in the outline on my own to the same standard as a full team would.

Dated: ************\_\_\_\_************

---

**Key points (что фиксирует контракт):**

- Поля: номер и название команды; имя team leader; место для прописанного dispute mechanism; до 4 подписей участников; дата.
- Обязательства команды: загрузить подписанный контракт в LMS team discussion forum; письменные предупреждения через форум за невыполнение обязательств, пропуск воркшопа/встречи без уважительной причины и заранее, несдачу работы к дедлайну команды.
- Эскалация: **2 письменных предупреждения** → консультация с лектором; **3 предупреждения** → исключение из команды.
- Санкция за исключение: исключённый участник должен выполнить **все задания из outline самостоятельно** на том же уровне, что и полная команда.
