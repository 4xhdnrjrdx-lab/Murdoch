It  is  a  normal  business  practice  to  put  into  writing  all  agreements  that  are  binding  on  the  parties
involved.  It  is  suggested  that  you  now  take  the  time  to  establish  the  acceptable  team  norms  and
behaviours  that  you  will  enforce.  It  is  further  suggested  that  you  decide  on  a  conflict  and  dispute
resolution procedure, so this is established before conflicts arise. Below is a ‘team contract’ that will
bind all team members to what the team decides, until the team is dissolved when the final assessment
is concluded.

scheduled team meeting, without prior notice to the team for a reason acceptable to the team.

by the deadlines set by the team.
