Dear Students,
I hope you are all doing well.
As we approach the submission of Assessment 3 – Individual Reflection and Review (15%) for BUS293 Organisational Behaviour and Management, I would like to remind you of the key assessment requirements.
Assessment 3 consists of two parts:
Part 1 – Individual Video Presentation (10%)
You are required to record a 3.5-minute video reflecting on your teamwork experience.
Your presentation should cover:

  *
Your individual contribution to the team report, including the key findings from your section (approximately 1.5 minutes).
  *
A reflection on how the organisational behaviour concepts studied in this unit were, or were not, applied by your team (approximately 2 minutes).

Please note the following:

  *
Your face must be visible throughout the recording.
  *
The audio should be clear and easy to hear.
  *
Do not use PowerPoint slides or any other visual aids. This is a face-to-camera presentation.
  *
The 3.5-minute time limit is strict, and penalties apply if you exceed it.
  *
Upload your video in .mp4 or .mov format only.
  *
Ensure your filename follows the naming convention provided in the Unit Outline.

Part 2 – Self and Peer Review (5%)
You are also required to complete the Self and Peer Review on the LMS.
When completing the review, evaluate both your own contribution and those of your team members objectively. Your evaluation should be based on evidence such as:

  *
Research completed and contributed
  *
Quality and quantity of work submitted
  *
Timeliness of contributions
  *
Communication within the LMS discussion forum
  *
Attendance at agreed team meetings
  *
Participation in discussions and idea sharing
  *
Cooperation with team decisions
  *
Overall contribution to the team's effectiveness

Please ensure that your evaluations are fair, professional, and free from personal bias. They should reflect each member's actual contribution to the team project.
Assessment Criteria
Your video presentation will be assessed on:

  *
Timing
  *
Contribution to the report
  *
Reflection
  *
Presentation style
  *
Professionalism

Please remember that failure to submit the Self and Peer Review will result in zero marks for that component.
I strongly encourage you to carefully read pages 12–14 of your Unit Outline, where the assessment requirements, submission instructions, and marking criteria are explained in detail. You should also review the assessment rubric available on the LMS before submitting your work.
I wish you all the best with your preparation and submission.
Kind regards,
Dr. Esther
