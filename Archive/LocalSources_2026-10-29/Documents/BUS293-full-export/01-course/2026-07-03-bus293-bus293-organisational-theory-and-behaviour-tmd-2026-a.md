Assessment 3 Part 2: Self and Peer Review – How to Complete It on LMS
by Farheen Javed - Friday, 3 July 2026, 1:52 PM
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Dear students,

Assessment 3 Part 2 requires you to complete the SELF AND PEER
REVIEW activity on LMS.

This means you must review:

* YOUR OWN CONTRIBUTION to the Team Report; and
* EACH TEAM MEMBER’S CONTRIBUTION to the Team Report.

To complete this activity:

* Go to BUS293 ON LMS.
* Open the ASSESSMENT 3 section.
* Click on the SELF AND PEER REVIEW activity.
* You will see your group members listed.
* For each person, including yourself, provide a score based on their
contribution to the Team Report.
* Where comments are required, provide a brief and professional
explanation for the score.
* When you have reviewed yourself and all group members, click SUBMIT.

When giving scores, please base your evaluation on evidence of
contribution, not personal opinion. You should consider whether each
person:

* completed research and contributed useful material;
* contributed an appropriate amount and quality of written work;
* submitted agreed work on time;
* communicated through the LMS team discussion forum;
* attended agreed team meetings;
* contributed ideas or solutions;
* cooperated with agreed team decisions; and
* supported the overall effectiveness of the team.

Your Self and Peer Review is CONFIDENTIAL. It is visible only to the
marker and will not be shared with your team members.

Students who do not complete the Self and Peer Review will receive ZERO
MARKS FOR THIS PART OF THE ASSESSMENT.

Kind regards,
Dr Farheen Javed
