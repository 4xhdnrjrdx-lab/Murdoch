Final Examination Syllabus and Preparation
by Farheen Javed - Thursday, 23 July 2026, 5:03 PM
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Dear Students,

Please note that the following topics are included in the final examination
syllabus:

* TOPIC 1: INTRODUCTION
* TOPIC 2: TEAM DYNAMICS
* TOPIC 7: ORGANISATIONAL CULTURE
* TOPIC 9: LEADERSHIP
* TOPIC 11: COMMUNICATION AND CONFLICT

Please expect a few questions from TOPICS 7, 9, AND 11, so I encourage you
to pay particular attention to these areas. Please revise the relevant
concepts, theories, and required readings associated with each topic,
including material that may not have been discussed in detail during class.
Students are encouraged to complete the required weekly readings and seek
clarification from their class lecturer where needed.

I am referring to topic numbers rather than chapter numbers because chapter
numbers may vary between different editions of the textbook. Please use the
topic names above to identify the relevant chapters in your edition.

Please note that the case study and short-answer questions will not be
taken directly from the textbook. When answering examination questions,
please ensure that you respond directly to the question and clearly apply
the relevant textbook theories and concepts.

As this is an OPEN-BOOK EXAMINATION, you can bring the permitted textbook
and notes. Preparing clear and well-organised notes in advance will help
you locate relevant information more efficiently during the examination.

If you require any further support with the topics or exam preparation,
please do not hesitate to contact your class lecturer.

Kind regards,

Dr Farheen Javed
