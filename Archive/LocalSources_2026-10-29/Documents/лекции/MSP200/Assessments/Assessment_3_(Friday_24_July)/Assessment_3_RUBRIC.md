Three criteria, weighted: **Task 1 – Employability skills self-analysis (worksheet) (5%)**, **Task 2 – Strategy for professional development (20%)**, **Written component (5%)** — total 30%.

---

## Task 1 — Employability skills self-analysis (worksheet) (5%)

**High Distinction (80–100%):** Student has completed the post course skills self-assessment table. A highly developed analysis and clear rationale for notable change is provided.

**Distinction (70–79%):** Student has completed the post course skills self-assessment table. Effective analysis and rationale for notable change is evident.

**Credit (60–69%):** Student has completed the post course skills self-assessment table. Any notable change has been adequately discussed.

**Pass (50–59%):** Student has completed the post course skills self-assessment. Minimal discussion relating to notable change provided.

**Fail (0–49%):** Student has either not completed the post course skills self-assessment table or noted change in skill confidence.

---

## Task 2 — Strategy for professional development (20%)

**High Distinction (80–100%):** Student identifies three employability skills for advanced development and provides a detailed rationale for their choice reflecting on their confidence, gaps in capability, and industry preferences. Produces a highly developed actionable SMART goal for each PD opportunity with all components correct. Start dates and timeframes are fully detailed. Ongoing PD reflection demonstrates strong link to life-long learning, skill acquisition and career success. World of work unit theme is innovatively applied and highly relevant to student's future industry or role.

**Distinction (70–79%):** Student identifies three employability skills for advanced development and provides a summary of reasons for their choice reflecting on their confidence, gaps in capability, and industry preferences. Produces an actionable SMART goal for each PD opportunity with all components correct. Start dates & timeframes are included. Ongoing PD reflection identifies pertinent link in life-long skill building and career advancement. World of work unit theme is meaningfully discussed and appropriate to student's future industry or role.

**Credit (60–69%):** Student identifies three employability skills for advanced development and provides a detailed rationale for their choice reflecting on their confidence, gaps in capability, and industry preferences. Produces an actionable SMART goal with components mostly correct. Ongoing PD reflection adequately discusses life-long learning and career benefits. World of work unit theme is mentioned with satisfactory link to industry/role.

**Pass (50–59%):** Student identifies at least one employability skill for advanced development and provides a rationale for their choice reflecting on one or more factors such as their confidence, gaps in capability, and industry preferences. Produces the basis of a SMART goal that could be actioned with further development. Ongoing PD reflection incorporates aspects of life-long learning and career benefits. World of work unit theme is referred to.

**Fail (0–49%):** Student either does not identify employability skills for advanced development or provide a rationale for their choice that is reflective of their confidence, gaps in capability, and industry preferences. Does not produce a completed SMART goal and/or it cannot be actioned. Ongoing PD reflection, life-long learning, and career benefits discussion is limited or not complete. World of work discussion is not addressed and/or does not apply a unit theme.

## Written component (5%)

**High Distinction (80–100%):** Highly effective written skills are deployed to demonstrate advanced skill development plans.

**Distinction (70–79%):** Effective written skills are deployed to demonstrate effective skill development plans.

**Credit (60–69%):** Good written communication skills are used to show skill development planning.

**Pass (50–59%):** Basic written communication skills are used to show some skill development planning.

**Fail (0–49%):** Undeveloped written communication to show skill development planning.

## Quick takeaways (for your submission)

- **Weights:** Task 1 (5%) + Task 2 (20%) + Written component (5%) = **30%** total. (ULO 3 & 4.)
- **Task 1:** complete the _post-course_ self-assessment table AND provide strong **analysis + rationale for any notable change** (don't just fill the table — discuss what changed and why).
- **Task 2** is where most marks sit. Top band needs all of: **three** skills with a **detailed rationale** (confidence, capability gaps, industry preferences); an **actionable SMART goal for each PD opportunity with all components correct + start dates and timeframes**; an **ongoing-PD reflection** strongly linked to **life-long learning and career success**; and a **World of Work theme** applied innovatively and highly relevant to your industry/role.
- **Written component:** clear, effective writing throughout (this is a distinct 5% — proofread and structure well).
