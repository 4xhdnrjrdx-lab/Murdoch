## Slide 2 — IMPORTANT

- Initial registration with the MOOC platform is required for enrolment in a short course (MOOC)
- You will have free access to course content for a specified time, e.g., for FutureLearn the duration of the course plus 14 days.
- You will need to ensure the next available course start date and expected duration will correspond with your timeframe for completing assessment 2 by the due date nominated in the Unit Guide.
- For courses needed payment for completion certificates, you DO NOT NEED TO PAY – you just need to collect evidence of engagement prior to completion for the MOOC, as skill development, not the MOOC content is the focus of the assessment.
- You need to complete a MINIMUM of six (6) hours for each MOOC you have selected as a career learning experience and provide evidence of your participation.

## Slide 3 — Evidence of course progress/completion

- Each of the three approved MOOC platforms (FutureLearn; EdX & Courseera) offers a different way of showing your course progress.
- You need to explore the platform _course progress_ display options
- Once you have completed your six hours or have you completed the course (whichever comes first) you view your course progress and take a screenshot to provide as evidence of your participation.
- The screenshot is then uploaded as part of Assessment two.

## Quick reference (summary)

- **Approved MOOC platforms:** FutureLearn, Coursera, edX.
- **Minimum engagement:** at least **6 hours per MOOC** (you choose three).
- **No payment needed:** don't pay for completion certificates — collect **evidence of engagement** before completion (skill development, not MOOC content, is the assessment focus).
- **Evidence = a screenshot of your course Progress** that clearly shows (1) the course name and (2) an appropriate % completion for six hours (a six-hour course would show 100% of steps).
- **Where:** each platform shows progress via a "Progress" tab/overview (FutureLearn Progress tab; Coursera progress timeline/green line; edX Progress tab).
- **Submit:** upload the screenshot for **Assessment 2 (located in Module 2)**; ensure your **name/initials are visible** (per the A2 instructions).
- **Timing:** make sure the course's start date + duration fit before the Assessment 2 due date (FutureLearn gives free access for the course duration + 14 days).
