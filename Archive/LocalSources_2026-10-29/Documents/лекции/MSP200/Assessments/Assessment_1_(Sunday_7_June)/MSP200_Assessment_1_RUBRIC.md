Task 2 — INDUSTRY ANALYSIS REPORT (10%)

HD (80-100): Student selects three advertised jobs that are well suited to their selected industry, graduate career or studies. Job Information Package is thorough with relevant information included for the positions. Analysis is superbly structured; introduction describes each position; well detailed rationale links the job selection to the career/study path. Employability skills, both implicit and explicit within the text are identified and their significance within the role explained with pertinent use of examples. Student provides a summary of commonalities accentuating the prioritised skills and justifying their industry value.
D (70-79): Three advertised jobs well suited to industry/career/studies. Job Information Package includes all relevant information. Analysis well structured with introduction describing each position; good rationale linking job selection to career/study path. Implicit and explicit skills identified, significance within each role explained with the use of example. Summary of commonalities identifying prioritised skills and supporting their industry value.
C (60-69): Three advertised jobs relevant to graduate career or studies. Job Information Package includes adequate information. Analysis explains each position with a satisfactory link to career/study path. Skills identified and their significance within the role mentioned. Summary of prioritised skills and their industry value is satisfactory.
P (50-59): Selects three advertised jobs and provides most of the relevant information (Job Information Package). Analysis provides a general description and explanation that somewhat supports the job selections and link to career/study path. Some mention of the Employability Skills preferred by employers. Summary of prioritised skills is adequate.
F (0-49): Selects less than three advertised jobs. Little to no information for the positions. Analysis limited with little to no description or link to career/study path. Little to no mention of Employability Skills.

Task 3 — PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT EXPERIENCES (Selection and Rationale, 7%)

HD (80-100): Completed the strengths/areas-to-improve and industry essential skills overview. Three PD experiences nominated, each of appropriate length, with URLs or registration evidence. Choice of PD experiences and associated skills well aligned with current personal capabilities and highly relevant to industry and future career path. Written English highly developed, with articulate narrative connections across Tasks 1, 2 & 3.
D (70-79): Overview completed. Three PD experiences nominated, appropriate length, URLs/registration evidence provided. Choice aligned with current capabilities and relevant to career/industry. Written English well developed with strong narrative connections between Tasks 1, 2 & 3.
C (60-69): Overview completed. Three PD experiences nominated, appropriate length, evidence provided. Sound connection between choice of PD experiences, industry analysis, self-rating and skills to develop. Written English coherent with substantial narrative connections across Tasks 1, 2 & 3.
P (50-59): Overview completed. Three PD experiences nominated, appropriate length, evidence provided. Basic connection between PD experiences, industry analysis, self-rating and skills. Written English understandable with some narrative connections across Tasks 1, 2 & 3.
F (0-49): Overview not attempted/completed. Three appropriate PD experiences not nominated and/or URLs and registration evidence not included. Written English unclear with little or no narrative connections across Tasks 1, 2 & 3.
