Task 2 — Industry Analysis (стр. 1–2)

"Source three job advertisements in your field.

Analyse each one to determine which EMPLOYABILITY skills and work attributes are prioritised by employers in the career, industry or role from your discipline area. In your analysis you will interpret and match the terminology in the advertisements to the nine employability skills covered in the unit. To help you identify the employability skills, download the Quick Guide to Employability Skills (LMS)."
Industry Analysis report format:

Briefly introduce the jobs you have sourced and explain why you have chosen each of them. The introduction should provide context for the analysis that will follow.
Analysis component (note this is not a summary of the advertisement):

o Identify the employability skills required to undertake the work (what skills are being asked for). Some skills may appear explicitly and match the terminology used in the unit; and some may be referred to implicitly thus needing interpretation (e.g., work collaboratively = teamwork).

o Examine how the employability skills are applied in the context of each job (how will they be used)

o Determine how important the identified employability skills are in the context of the advertisement (which skill(s) appear to be prioritised and why).

o Consider all three jobs together and summarise the commonalities, highlighting the employability skills most valued. Make sure you comment on your industry and why you think these skills are preferred.

JOB INFORMATION - must be provided for verification and grading purposes:

A live URL to the job advertisements (note: these links expire, so you must also include a legible screenshot/s of the whole advertisement), any available job selection criteria, or job description forms (JDF)
Any relevant information about the organisation you will use in your analysis

Note: The job information package can be pasted in at the end of your document or uploaded as a clearly labelled separate document/s (e.g., Job Information Package A/B/C).

Suggested links (если не знаешь, где искать вакансии):

Murdoch University Careers portal - MyMurdoch Career. External job boards e.g. Indeed, Seek, Jobs.WA (W.A. state government), aps.gov.au (federal government), GradAustralia, Gradconnection, Ethical Jobs, My Careers Future (Singapore), Dubai Careers or individual employer websites – i.e., specific organisations you are interested in or LinkedIn.

You can include: Jobs open to all job seekers; Graduate programs open to final year students and recent graduates; entry level and/or more senior roles; local or more global professional opportunities. The jobs can be formally advertised roles for different positions or the same role with different employers aligned with your area of study.
Note: Industry is used here in the broadest sense to mean the economic and social structures providing various forms of employment (e.g., health, finance, publishing, agricultural, commercial, mining, research, tourism and hospitality, creative, arts, manufacturing, IT, robotics, space, higher education, and so forth).
