# MSP200 Assessment 2 — PD Workshop Journaling Guidelines (full text)

> Your own Murdoch course material (guideline for students). Reproduced verbatim for reference.

**Title:** Assessment 2 – guideline for students — Journaling your Professional Development (PD) activities
**Unit:** MSP200 Building Employability Skills

---

## 1. Planning Stage

- **Identify the Activity:** Name of the course or workshop
- **Define the Goal:** Which employability skill are you addressing?
- **Expectation:** Are you certain about your choice of PD activity. What makes you think you will develop the employability skill you applied to this activity?
- **Emotional:** How do you feel about attending - excited, looking forward to, worried, uncertain

## 2. Participation Stage

During or immediately after the activity, record the details while they are fresh.

- **Key Takeaways:** Summarise the main points, insights, or new concepts.
- **Data Collection:** Take photos of key slides if you are allowed to, and take any handouts that might be provided
- **Initial Reactions:** Did you find the session useful? Was it well-planned? Were there inter-active activities and how did you feel about these? Has this activity changed your perspective or approach? How does this new information or experience connect to your current study or past experiences? Did the employability skill you set out develop happen (jot this down to use as an example/s for your reflection task)
- **Other thoughts** that you would like to record for possible use in the 'Critical Reflection' task.

---

## How this fits Assessment 2

These journal entries are the raw material for your **Critical Reflection video** (the 5R's framework). Capturing planning-stage expectations/emotions and participation-stage takeaways/reactions for each of your three PD activities gives you the concrete examples the rubric rewards (e.g., 3+ examples of skill development per PD experience, and the "Did the skill develop?" note that feeds the REASONING step). Do this **during/after each PD activity**, while it's fresh.
