# MSP200 Assessment 2 — Rubric (full text)

> Your own Murdoch course material. Reproduced verbatim for reference; write your own submission.

**Assessment:** MSP200 Assessment 2 — Professional Learning and World of Work Reflection
**Unit Learning Outcomes:** 2 & 3
**Fields:** Student Name: **_ | Grade: _** %
**Grade bands (columns):** High Distinction (80–100) · Distinction (70–79) · Credit (60–69) · Pass (50–59) · Fail (0–49)

Three criteria, weighted: **Evidence of Engagement (5%)**, **Critical Reflection (20%)**, **Presentation and slides (15%)** — total 40%.

---

## Criterion 1 — Evidence of Engagement in three Professional Development (PD) experiences (5%)

**High Distinction (80–100):** Student has provided outstanding evidence demonstrating full completion (and/or maximum allowable completion) for all three (3) PD Experiences showing an exemplary level of engagement.

**Distinction (70–79):** Student has provided evidence demonstrating full completion and/or maximum allowable completion of, and a high level of engagement with three (3) PD Experiences.

**Credit (60–69):** Student has provided evidence demonstrating majority completion and considerable engagement with three (3) PD Experiences.

**Pass (50–59):** Student has provided progress evidence only demonstrating a minimum level of engagement with (3) three PD Experiences.

**Fail (0–49):** Student has provided some evidence for one or two PD experiences and/or not provided adequate evidence to demonstrate engagement with (3) three PD Experiences.

---

## Criterion 2 — Critical Reflection (20%)

**High Distinction (80–100):** Student fully utilises the 5R reflective framework and meaningfully synthesizes connections between all three PD experiences, with effective reflection and evaluation on the relevance of employability skills to their career. Skill development is well evidenced using 3 or more examples drawn from each of the PD experiences. Synthesizes PD experience with theory, making detailed, realistic plans for further skill development.

**Distinction (70–79):** Student applies the 5R reflective framework and makes an effective connection comparing all three PD experiences, with strong reflection and evaluation on the relevance of employability skills to their career. Skill development is efficiently demonstrated using 3 examples from the PD experiences. Recognises the value in professional development outlining relevant and actionable ideas for skill development.

**Credit (60–69):** Student refers to the 5R reflective framework and outlines a functional connection between all three PD experiences, with adequate reflection and evaluation on the relevance of employability skills to their career. Skill development is adequately demonstrated with the use of 3 PD experience examples. Connects PD experiences to concepts, outlining general steps for future development.

**Pass (50–59):** Student inconsistently uses the 5R reflective framework and attempts to form a connection between all three learning experiences, begins to reflect on the relevance of employability skills to their career. Skill development is beginning to be demonstrated with an example. Mentions future goals but connection to employability skills relevance is limited.

**Fail (0–49):** Student does not use the 5R reflective framework. Does not consider all three learning experiences, nor reflects on or evaluates the relevance of employability skills to their career. Little to no skill development is demonstrated through example. No evidence or connection to future actions or employability skill development.

---

## Criterion 3 — Presentation and slides (15%)

**High Distinction (80–100):** The delivery and overall tone is dynamic and professional, with excellent eye contact. Time limit is met. The text and images on the slides are extremely clear and make a very strong contribution to the effectiveness of the presentation.

**Distinction (70–79):** Much of the delivery and overall tone is dynamic and professional, with regular contact. Time limit is met. The text and images on the slides make a strong contribution to the effectiveness of the presentation.

**Credit (60–69):** The tone of the presentation is confident and appropriate for professional contexts, with adequate eye contact. Time limit is close to being met. The text and images on the slides make a good contribution to the effectiveness of the presentation.

**Pass (50–59):** Delivery is mostly understandable and attempts to present as professional and engaged are evident with some minor room for improvement. Time limit not met. The text and images on the slides need to be more relevant and/or more appropriate, and/or more attractive, and/or more easily readable.

**Fail (0–49):** Delivery tends towards being unclear, too fast, inaudible or to 'read'. Eye contact with the camera could be more direct. Presentation is too casual in tone and approach. Time limit not met. The text and/or images on the slides do not contribute to the effectiveness of the presentation / too much text in slides.

## Quick takeaways (for your submission)

- **Weights:** Evidence 5% + Critical Reflection 20% + Presentation/slides 15% = **40%** total.
- To hit the top band, the reflection must **fully use the 5R framework**, **synthesize across all three PD experiences**, **link employability skills to your career**, evidence skill development with **3+ examples per PD experience**, and connect PD to **theory** with **detailed, realistic future plans**.
- Presentation: **professional, dynamic tone, strong eye contact, meet the time limit**, and slides that are **clear and mostly visual** (recall the secure-assessment slide rules — headings/subheadings only, minimal text; "too much text in slides" is explicitly a Fail-band marker).
- Evidence: provide proof of **full/maximum completion for all three PD experiences** (certificates / Forage / MOOC progress screenshots with your name/initials and hours visible).
