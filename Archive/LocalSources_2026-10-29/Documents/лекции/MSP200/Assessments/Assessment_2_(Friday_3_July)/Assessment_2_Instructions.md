**Part 2 — Critical Reflection (видео) — введение (стр. 1)**
"Using the journal entries you completed during and after each PD workshop/course, you now plan and record a 10-minute video critically reflecting on the PD experience.
• The journal entries or basic reflection covered the 'what happened' component, whereas the critical reflection will delve deeper. This is more than a description or a summary – it is an insight into what the activity meant to you, how it connects to career readiness, and what you will do next to further your skill development.
A useful way to think through and reflect on an experience is to use a framework that prompts deep critical thought about what happened."

**Подготовка и фреймворк (стр. 2)**
"Start by reviewing your journal entries – draw out significant events and key themes, aspects that evoke strong feelings, or moments of change or new understanding.
Utilise the 5R's to reflective framework to structure your video narrative with a clear beginning, middle and end."

**Схема: 5 R's to Reflective Practice Framework** (изображение на стр. 2 — «A PIECE OF REFLECTIVE WRITING:»). Пять колонок-этапов с описаниями:

- **(REPORTING)** — Is personal account of an experience, describing key objectives and/or activities.
- **(RESPONDING)** — Examines your emotional reaction to the experience and requires openness about any anxieties, errors and successes.
- **(RELATING)** — Requires analysis of the experience in terms of personal relevance or meaning (e.g. how does it relate to "me and my career").
- **(REASONING)** — Includes what you learned or developed and recognising how it may have influenced your perceptions (e.g. "my employability.")
- **(RECONSTRUCTING)** — Draws conclusions and discusses what you plan to do with this new knowledge.
  Источник схемы (цитата на изображении): "Bain, J., Ballantyne, R., Packer, J., and Mills, C. (1999). Using journal writing to enhance student teachers' reflectivity during field experience placements, Teachers and Teaching 5(1): 51-73."

**Guideline по длительности секций (рамка на стр. 2)**
"A guideline for how long each section should be is provided below.
▪ The introduction should introduce the content and structure of your presentation (approximately 1 minute).
▪ The body will have dedicated sections briefly describing the events, interpreting their significance, and evaluating their impact (approximately 8 minutes; a little under 3 minutes per course).
▪ The conclusion summarises your key learnings and how these will inform your future actions or personal growth (approximately 1 minute)."

**Идеи для нарратива видео — этапы 5R (стр. 2–3)**
"The ideas below will help you develop a cohesive and compelling narrative for your video:
• REPORTING: Set the scene by explaining the purpose behind the PD workshops/courses and give a brief overview of each PD activity
• RESPONDING: What were your initial thoughts and feelings about choosing this PD activity and how it would help develop the employability skill you highlighted. Were there aspects that were difficult to understand or apply?
• RELATING: How is the PD activity and its associated employability skill relevant to you and your career
• REASONING: What have you learned - give a clear example of how your skill development occurred in each PD experience. Note - If the skill you selected was not (as you expected) the primary one being developed - expand on this and discuss any other skills that were developed or enhanced in the process
• RECONSTRUCTING: What do you plan to do with this new knowledge. For example, thinking about all three PD experiences and the skill development that occurred - how has your confidence in demonstrating each skill been impacted and what plan do you have for further skill development"

## Сводка — термины / форматы / требования

- Фреймворк: **5R's to Reflective Practice Framework** (Reporting → Responding → Relating → Reasoning → Reconstructing); источник — Bain, Ballantyne, Packer & Mills (1999).
- Две части: Part 1 — Evidence (3 PD-опыта), Part 2 — Critical Reflection (видео + слайды).
- Видео: **10 минут** (intro ~1 мин / body ~8 мин, чуть меньше 3 мин на курс / conclusion ~1 мин); загрузка через **Echo 360**.
- Доказательства: сертификаты завершения, Forage certificate, скриншоты прогресса MOOC (FutureLearn, Coursera, edX) с видимыми инициалами/именем и часами; маркировать как Part 1 PD Evidence A/B/C.
- Слайды (secure assessment): только заголовки/подзаголовки, без предложений/буллетов/абзацев, в основном визуалы с цитированием; исключение — Introduction и Conclusion (можно несколько подзаголовков в виде пунктов). **Важно: лишний текст → работа не оценивается.**
- Акцент: больше анализа и оценки, чем пересказа фактов; честность и личный тон.
