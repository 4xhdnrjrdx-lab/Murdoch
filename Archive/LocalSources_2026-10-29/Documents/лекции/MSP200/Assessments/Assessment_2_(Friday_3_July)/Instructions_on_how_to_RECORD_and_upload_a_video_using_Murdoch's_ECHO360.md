## 1 — Submit an Echo360 video assignment (Student)

Initial steps:

- Click on the assignment link in your myMurdoch Learning unit. _(Screenshot: a button labelled "Video assignment".)_
- Click **'add submission'**. _(Screenshot: "Add submission" button.)_
- Click to accept the assignment declaration. _(Screenshot: a checked "Assignment Declaration" checkbox.)_
- Select the **Echo360 icon** in the 'online text' dialog box. _(Screenshot: the text-editor toolbar with the Echo360 icon highlighted in the second toolbar row.)_

From the **'embed media'** dialog box, there are **3 different options**:

### Option 1 — Select a video previously uploaded to your library

- Select a video previously uploaded to your **library**, and click **'Next'**. _(Screenshot: "Embed Media" dialog, "Library" tab active, with a search box, a "Sort by Name" control, "9 item(s)", and a list of videos such as "Creating Assignments.mp4" (Created May 31, 2022 3:09 PM), "Gradebook Setup & Grades Transfer_JR_270522.mp4" (Created May 27, 2022 3:04 PM), "Gradebook Setup & Transfer (Resources & Support).mp4" (Created May 13, 2022 1:23 PM), "How students can engage in class (Copy)"; the chosen video is ticked; "Next" button bottom-right.)_
- Click **'save changes'**. _(Screenshot: the online-text editor now showing a link "Creating+Assignments.mp4", with "Save changes" and "Cancel" buttons below.)_
- View the **Submission Status: 'Submitted for grading'** when complete. _(Screenshot: "Submission status" panel — Submission status: "Submitted for grading"; Grading status: "Not graded"; Last modified: "Thursday, 4 August 2022, 3:11 PM".)_

### Option 2 — Select 'Create New Media' and click 'Launch Universal Capture'

_(Use this method if you do not have a pre-recorded video uploaded to your Echo360 library and wish to record immediately for the assignment, recording with Echo360 Universal Capture.)_

- _(Screenshot: "Embed Media" dialog, "Create New Media" tab active; "UNIVERSAL CAPTURE" graphic; text "Echo360 Universal Capture is the simplest video application for education that provides identical capabilities, and a consistent user experience across Mac and PC."; "Launch Universal Capture" button.)_
- Launch **'Universal Capture'** if the application is already installed on your computer, or **download** the application (appropriate for your computer) if not installed. _(Screenshot: "UNIVERSAL CAPTURE" panel — "Universal Capture will launch automatically if installed"; "Launch Universal Capture" button; "Don't have Universal Capture installed?" with links: "Mac Download (Intel) | Mac Download (Apple Silicon) | Windows Download" ("After installation, return here to launch Universal Capture"); "See system requirements".)_
- Open **Echo360 Universal Capture**. _(Screenshot: browser prompt "Open Echo360 Capture?" — "https://echo360.net.au wants to open this application."; checkbox "Always allow echo360.net.au to open links of this type in the associated app"; buttons "Cancel" / "Open Echo360 Capture".)_
- **Start your video recording, and stop when finished**, and the video will automatically upload to your library. _(Screenshot: Echo360 Universal Capture interface — top bar "Untitled → Library", "Title recording" callout, user "John Rogers", "Preview Frame Rate Reduced", "ON DEMAND"; input/display panes; a red record button labelled "On Demand Capture".)_
- **TIP:** give the recording a Title and Description before uploading to your library. _(Screenshot: "Capture Details" form — fields Title ("Untitled"), Description, "Tags (separated by commas)"; right side Quality ("High"), "Publish To…" ("Library"), Class ("Select…"), a "Live Stream" checkbox; buttons "CANCEL" / "SAVE".)_
- **Video automatically uploads to your library.** _(Screenshot: message "Capture complete. Click to view" with a "CLOSE" button.)_
- You can close Universal Capture when the upload is complete.
- Select **Library**, select your video that has just uploaded, click **'Next'** and **repeat the steps in Option a** (i.e., Option 1 — save changes, then confirm submission status).

### Option 3 — Select 'Upload Existing Media' to upload existing media (e.g., from your computer or mobile device)

- _(Screenshot: "Embed Media" dialog, "Library" tab — search box, "Sort by Name", "37 item(s)", list including "SampleVideo_1280x720_1mb.mp4" (Created Mar 7, 2022 11:13 AM), "Show Tell New Features in Teams_MH_280622.mp4" (Created Jul 11, 2022 12:50 PM), "Video Assignment Test" (Created Aug 4, 2022 3:54 PM, ticked); "Next" button.)_
- Select **'Upload Existing Media'**. _(Screenshot: "Embed Media" dialog, "Upload Existing Media" tab active; text "Create new media via upload, or select existing media from Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business, or Box."; "Select Media" button.)_
- _(Screenshot: upload panel with online-source icons (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business, Box) on the left; centre drop zone "Select Files to Upload — or Drag and Drop, Copy and Paste Files"; caption "Drag file, or choose from online source (Dropbox, Google etc.)".)_
- Click **'upload'**. _(Screenshot: "Selected Files" view showing "FILES — Creating Assignments.mp4 (56MB)"; buttons "Deselect All" / "Upload".)_
- Click **'Close'**. _(Screenshot: Library view, message "Files uploaded successfully" with a "Close" button; list shows e.g. "Creating Assignments_JR.mp4" (Created May 31, 2022 3:09 PM), "Creating Video Assignments_JR.mp4".)_
- Click **'Next'**. _(Screenshot: "Embed Media" Library tab, "1 item(s)", showing "Creating Assignments.mp4" (Created Aug 5, 2022 9:20 AM, ticked); "Next" button.)_
- Click **'Save changes'**. _(Screenshot: online-text editor showing link "Creating+Assignments.mp4"; "Save changes" / "Cancel" buttons.)_

## Quick reference (summary)

- **Plugin/tool:** Echo360 plugin embedded in MyMurdoch Learning (LMS); recording app = **Echo360 Universal Capture** (Mac/PC).
- **Three ways to submit a video:** (1) embed an existing video from your Echo360 **Library**; (2) **Create New Media** → record now with Universal Capture; (3) **Upload Existing Media** from your computer/mobile or a cloud source (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business, Box).
- **Core flow:** assignment link → Add submission → accept Assignment Declaration → Echo360 icon → embed/record/upload video → Next → **Save changes** → confirm **Submission status: "Submitted for grading"**.
- **Tip:** name the recording (Title + Description) before it uploads to your library.
