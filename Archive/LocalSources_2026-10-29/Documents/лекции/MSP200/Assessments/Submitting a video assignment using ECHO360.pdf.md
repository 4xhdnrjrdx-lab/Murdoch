Submitting a video assignment (Student Guide)

The Echo360 Plugin is installed in MyMurdoch Learning to allow students to submit embedded videos from Echo360
into an assignment. This quick guide will show students how to submit a video through the Echo360 plugin for an
assignment.

Submit an Echo360 video assignment (Student)

Click on the assignment link in your myMurdoch Learning unit

Click ‘add submission’

Click to accept the assignment declaration

Select the Echo360 icon in the ‘online text’ dialog box
