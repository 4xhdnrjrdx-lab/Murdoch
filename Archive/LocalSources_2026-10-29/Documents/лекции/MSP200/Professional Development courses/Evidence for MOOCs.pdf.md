# How to Evidence MOOCs

Providing Evidence of Participation
for a MOOC

IMPORTANT

•  Initial registration with the MOOC platform is required for enrolment in a short

course (MOOC)

•  You will have free access to course content for a specified time, e.g., for

FutureLearn the duration of the course plus 14 days.

•  You will need to ensure the next available course start date and expected

duration will correspond with your timeframe for completing assessment 2 by the
due date nominated in the Unit Guide.

•  For courses needed payment for completion certificates, you DO NOT NEED

TO PAY – you just need to collect evidence of engagement prior to completion
for the MOOC, as skill development, not the MOOC content is the focus of the
assessment.

•  You need to complete a MINIMUM of six (6) hours for each MOOC you have

selected as a career learning experience and provide evidence of your
participation.

•  Evidence of course progress/completion

Each of the three approved MOOC platforms (FutureLearn; EdX &
Courseera) offers a different way of showing your course progress.

You need to explore the platform course progress display options

Once you have completed your six hours or have you completed the course
(whichever comes first) you view your course progress and take a
screenshot to provide as evidence of your participation.

The screenshot is then uploaded as part of Assessment two.
