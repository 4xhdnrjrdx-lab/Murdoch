# MOOCs - FutureLearn, Coursera and EdX

**There are three MOOCs platforms you can select from:**

* [FutureLearn](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses)
* [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/)
* [edX](https://www.edx.org/)

Courses selected from these platforms must be a **minimum of 6 hours duration.**

The Good Universities Guide defines a MOOC as:

*‘[A] Massive Online Course, which is a free online course available to anyone that wants to take the course. They are similar to online courses in terms of teaching and learning methods videos, group chats, assignments and tests but they do not generally provide academic credit for use in other traditional courses, nor will you have much (if any) interaction with the lecturer’*

When selecting a MOOC please ensure that you choose courses that are offered with **free enrolment**.  We do not require you to pay for these courses, we only require that you have access to the material which is free.  The platforms may refer to the free access option as *free/ audit enrolment option or limited access.*

With the MOOCs you do not need to pay for a completion certificate – all we require is ‘evidence of engagement’ in the minimum 6 hours we stipulate.  You can take progress screenshots of a progress wheel (FutureLearn) or progress screenshots of the modules/weeks you complete.

Note:  If your course is exactly 6 hours, we understand that your completion will not be 100% since some sections will sit behind a paywall.  If your course is in excess of 6 hours you must complete a percentage that supports the minimum 6 hours (e.g., 12 hour course would require a 50% completion progress wheel or screenshots of completed sections that support a 6 hour duration).
