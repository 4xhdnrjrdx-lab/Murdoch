**СТР. 4 — 1 Unit Details**

Welcome to: MSP200 — Building Employability Skills

**1.1 Unit Description**
Get ready to shape your future and build the skills, confidence, and mindset you need to thrive in a rapidly evolving, technology driven, job market.
In this unit, we explore contemporary issues in the world of work including digital technologies (AI), industry recruitment preferences and practices, employability skills and the importance of a lifelong learning approach.
As part of your professional and reflective development, you will complete a series of professional development activities in the form of workshops and online courses, that offer you personalised opportunities to develop industry relevant skills.
You will plan for the next stage of your professional development and establish actionable steps to bridge skill gaps and define long term career goals.

**1.2 Learning Outcomes** (таблица: Unit Learning Outcomes ↔ Graduate Attributes)

Unit Learning Outcomes (ULOs):

1. Examine contemporary local and global developments influencing the world of work.
2. Evaluate your career needs, identifying preferred attributes and skills for professional development.
3. Engage in skill development activities, and critically reflect on, your professional development.
4. Demonstrate effective oral, written and interpersonal skills appropriate to the workplace.

Graduate Attributes (правая колонка таблицы — «This unit will contribute to the development of the following Graduate Attributes»):
• Communication
• Critical and creative thinking
• Social interaction
• Independent and lifelong learning
• Ethics
• Social justice
• Global perspective
• Interdisciplinarity

**1.3 Murdoch Strategic themes**
As an institution, the strategic themes of Sustainability; Equity, Diversity & Inclusion; and First Nations guide and shape much of our activity (see Our Strategy 2023-2030 Ngala Kwop Biddi).
In this unit, we support students in understanding the necessity and value in implementing Equity, Diversity & Inclusion processes in a professional setting. This occurs as part of the focus topic discussions and through the Professional Development workshops students will engage in.

**3.1 Approach to learning and teaching**
MSP200 is delivered as a series of world of work and employability skills topics delivered across the teaching period in the form of in-class lectures, group discussions and collaborative learning activities. Students also engage in three professional development workshops designed to help them develop specific skills relevant to their future career. This integrative approach provides a holistic understanding of real-world skill development for the workplace.
The four assessments include a participation component and three distinct but interrelated written assessments.
The underpinning learning approach is built around evaluative engagement, critical thinking and critical self-reflection. By cultivating these attributes students effectively steer their career paths as agile self-directed individuals.

**3.2 Expected time commitment for studying this unit**
As this is a 3-credit point unit, we expect you to spend on average 12.5 hours per week for the total weeks of this teaching period (or 150 hours overall) working on this unit.

**5.2.1 Assessment 1 – Industry and Skills Analysis**

ASSESSMENT OVERVIEW
This assessment has THREE tasks (detailed instructions available in the myMurdoch learning assessment tab):

1. Employability skills self-analysis and rationale (in-class completion) – you will rate and comment on your current understanding and confidence in demonstrating specific employability skills.
2. Industry analysis report (800 to 1000 words) – you will explore employment opportunities (job advertisements) to determine the work attributes and employability skills preferred by employers and recruiters in your chosen field (this must be an industry or field related to your university studies). Your analysis will require interpreting and matching the terminology in the advertisements to the nine employability skills covered in the MSP200 unit.
3. Provide details, registration/enrolment evidence and a rationale for each of the three Professional Development (PD) workshops/courses you will engage in to help you develop the three different employability skills you identified as required and valued in your industry or field.

❖ As per Point 5.3.1 in the Academic Integrity component in this Unit Outline - AI is not applicable in completing the three tasks in Assessment 1 as you need to demonstrate your core understanding and knowledge. The first and last task require personal self-analysis, subjective judgement and reasoning. The Industry Analysis requires in-depth unit content knowledge to support authenticity of the response.
❖ AI may assist in simple editing using Murdoch University editing tools and referencing functions that require MU log in credentials.

**5.2.2 Assessment 2 – Professional Learning Reflection**

ASSESSMENT OVERVIEW
This assessment has TWO parts (detailed instructions available in the myMurdoch learning assessment tab):
a. Evidence - provide appropriate evidence supporting your participation or completion in the three professional development experiences you selected for specific skill development. The type of evidence is dependent on the professional development experienced selected. More information is available in myMurdoch learning.
b. Critical Reflection – Video presentation and slides. Using the journal entries you completed during and after each PD activity, you will plan and record a 10-minute video critically reflecting on the PD experiences. To help you structure the critical reflection you will utilise the 5R’s to reflective practice framework. Instructions on how to prepare and upload your video are available in myMurdoch learning.

PowerPoint slides. As this is a secure assessment, your slides must contain minimal text:
• Text in slides is limited to headings and subheadings only
• Slides must not contain written sentences, dot points or paragraphs
• Slides are to contain mostly visuals (e.g. graphics, images, and so on, to support your presentation)
• Citations for visuals (graphics and images must be cited)
• Only the Introduction and Conclusion slides may contain multiple sub-headings in dot points

**5.2.3 Assessment 3 - Strategy for Professional Development**

ASSESSMENT OVERVIEW
This assessment has TWO parts (detailed instructions available in the myMurdoch learning assessment tab):

1. Employability skills self-analysis (in-class completion) – you will review and complete the self-analysis table you partially completed for Assessment 1. You will rate your current employability skill level since engaging with your Professional Development experiences, comparing your pre and post PD experience skill self-assessments, and discussing any notable changes in the three employability skills your PD experiences focused on, as well as other skill areas of note.
2. Strategy for professional development:
   a. Based on your updated ratings identify THREE employability skills you need to further develop. Note: The employability skills selected for further development can be a combination of your stronger and more challenging skills.
   b. Provide a brief rationale supporting your choice of employability skills. Reflect on your confidence and current capability in each skill, and how valued it is in the industry or role you aspire to.
   c. Using the SMART goal method – clearly define three specific and realistic professional development opportunities you will undertake to achieve each of your advanced skill development goals (e.g., online courses/workshops - provide the name/platform URL, if it is a volunteer gig or work placement - name the organisation you plan to approach).
   • Specific: What will you do (include URL/Title details/Organisation name etc.)
   • Measurable: How will you measure your progress
   • Achievable: How doable and realistic is this goal for you to achieve (does it fit in with your schedule, is it affordable and accessible)
   • Relevant: How is this relevant to your future career and/or personal growth
   • Time-bound: When will you start and complete the PD opportunity
   d. Conclude with a short reflection on how ongoing professional development and adopting a life-long learning mindset will benefit your career growth. Discuss with reference to ONE World of Work theme from the unit (e.g., globalisation, casualisation, AI ethics, automation, alienation, work as identity, or big data analytics).

❖ As per Point 5.3.1 in the Academic Integrity component in this Unit Outline - AI is not applicable in completing the tasks in Assessment 3 as you need to demonstrate your core understanding and knowledge - (the content is informed by the industry research, PD experience and World of Work knowledge from assessments 1 and 2. The action plan requires personal self-analysis and individual reasoning for further skill development in your industry/role). AI may assist in simple editing using Murdoch University editing tools and referencing functions that require MU log in credentials.
