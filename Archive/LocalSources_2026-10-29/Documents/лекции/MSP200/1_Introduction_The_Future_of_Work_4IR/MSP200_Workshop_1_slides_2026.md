In this topic, we introduce and begin to explore the major shifts and rapid change currently taking place in the world of work and the employability skills you will need to navigate your career.

We will explore the positive and negative implications of new technologies, globalisation, casualisation and the gig economy for your career trajectory. In this time of rapid change, uncertainty, and disruption, MPS200 prepares you comprehensively for the future of work.

To do that we focus on the notion of employability and support you to identify and develop the employability skills sought by employers in your field or industry.
