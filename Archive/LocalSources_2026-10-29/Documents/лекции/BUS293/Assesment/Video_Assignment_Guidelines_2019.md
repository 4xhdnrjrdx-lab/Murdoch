**Murdoch University — Video Assignment Guidelines**

The following guidelines will assist you with producing and submitting your video assignment. The document is divided into two parts:

1. Producing your video
2. Submitting your video to your assignment drop-box in LMS

---

**1. Producing your video** (p.1)
There are a number of options to produce your video, depending on the devices and equipment you have available.

Table — Device → Recommendation:

- Mac laptop/desktop computer → iMovie or Photobooth
- Windows PC laptop/desktop computer → Windows Movie Maker
- iPhone → iMovie App
- Android → Devices' Camera Video Recorder

---

**2. Submitting your video to your assignment drop-box in LMS** (p.2)
After you have recorded your video, you can submit directly into the assignment portal. Before you submit, please follow these steps:

1. Restart the PC
2. Use only Chrome or Firefox (only use one **updated** browser)
3. Access the LMS unit and submission point

Step-by-step (with screenshots):

- Click the **Video Assignment submission point**. (Screenshot: "Submission status" panel — Submission status: No attempt; Grading status: Not graded; Last modified: –; Submission comments: Comments (0); "You have not made a submission yet.")
- Click the **"Add submission"** button.
- In the text editor (Online text), click on the **Echo360 video link** (the circular icon in the editor toolbar).
- Click on the **"Upload Existing Media"** tab. (Screenshot: "Embed Media" dialog from echo, with tabs "Choose From My Home" and "Upload Existing Media"; caption "Create new media via upload, or select existing media from Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business, or Box.")
- Click on the **"Select Media"** button.

**(p.3)**

- After drag and drop the video into the upload box, or select the file to upload from your machine. (Screenshot: "Selected Files" dialog showing file "Assignment Video.mp4", 117MB; cloud source icons for Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box; buttons "Deselect All", "Upload more", "Upload".)
- Click on the **"Upload"** button.
- Once the video is uploaded you will get a prompt and you can close the prompt. (Screenshot: "Files uploaded successfully" with a "Close" button.)
- Click on the **"Insert"** button to select the video. (Screenshot: echo "Choose From My Home" tab — search content, file "Assignment Video.mp4", date "Aug 15, 2019 12:57pm", "Insert" button.)

**(p.4)**

- You will see the video title in the text box. (Screenshot: Online text editor showing "Assignment+Video.mp4"; buttons "Save changes" and "Cancel".)
- Click on **"Save Changes"**.
- _Congratulations! you have now submitted your video assignment._ (Screenshot: "Video Assignment Submission Point" with "Return to: Section 2" button — Submission status: Submitted for grading; Grading status: Not graded; Last modified: Thursday, 15 August 2019, 3:40 PM; Online text shows the embedded video; Submission comments: Comments (0).)

---

**Key points at a glance:**

- **Recording tools by device:** Mac → iMovie/Photobooth; Windows PC → Windows Movie Maker; iPhone → iMovie App; Android → built-in Camera Video Recorder.
- **Before submitting:** restart the PC; use only one updated browser (Chrome or Firefox).
- **Submission flow in LMS:** Video Assignment submission point → Add submission → Echo360 video link in the text editor → Upload Existing Media → Select Media → drag/drop or select file → Upload → close the success prompt → Insert → Save Changes.
- Media platform is **Echo360**; can upload from machine or pull from Dropbox/Google Drive/OneDrive/Box.
- Submission confirmed when status shows **"Submitted for grading"**.
