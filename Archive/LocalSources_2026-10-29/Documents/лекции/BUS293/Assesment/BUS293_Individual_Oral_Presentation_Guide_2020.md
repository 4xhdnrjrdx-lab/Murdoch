**BUS293 Organisational Theory and Behaviour**
**Individual oral presentation – recording video – content guide**

Each student is required to make a video recording of their oral presentation.

The quality of the recorded video will not be graded therefore there is no requirement to have the oral presentation professionally filmed. Students may record using a laptop or a mobile phone or the web cam on a computer. Students are required to make sure the video is clear enough for the marker to view and mark the presentation.

This BUS293 assessment develops the skill of making a presentation in front of a camera (recording) which gives Murdoch University graduates an introduction to this form of communication. Some Murdoch graduates have reported that they are being asked to submit a recorded interview as part of the job application process. Therefore the development of this skill may benefit students during their career advancement.

**Guide to presentation:**

Students should dress and groom to a professional business standard for the recording of the video. During the presentation, facial expressions will be important, remember to smile, and remember _to try to make contact with the audience via the camera_.

The recording can be done in any location and the background/backdrop will be the student's choice. When choosing the environment consider the background noise that may be a distraction to the audience/listeners. Some background noise can be good as it breaks the silence, however, try to avoid loud or distracting background noise.

**Guide to content:**

The video cannot be longer than 3.5 minutes and there is a penalty for exceeding this time. Therefore it is important to be concise. Considering how to structure the presentation, make it engaging, logical, relevant and interesting. The oral presentation content is divided into two parts:

**Part 1 – a summary of your section of the team research report = 1.5 minutes**

Students are required to present on the section of the team research report that they participated in completing. Each student's content will vary depending on what section of the team assignment they completed. Consider introducing the topic and the organisation to your listener, then outline a summary of your section of the team research report. What did you learn? What did your research uncover? What are your findings?

**Part 2 – reflection on the teamwork experiences or processes = 2 minutes**

Students are required to reflect on the teamwork experiences while working on the team research report. The reflections should be applied to theory on 'team dynamics' (Chapter 8). Consider what OTB issues were evident in your team or in yourself, during this process? Students may draw on any of the OTB concepts during their reflection to compile their presentation. For example, consider using one or some of these areas: teamwork, leadership, management, culture, power, communication, motivation, empowerment, individual attributes, behaviour, decision-making. Students are not expected to use all these areas, but are expected to at least apply their reflection to concepts in chapter 8.

(Students should consider reviewing the marking rubric on LMS.)

---

**Key points / structure:**

- Video assessment, max **3.5 minutes** (penalty for exceeding), recorded on laptop/phone/webcam; video quality not graded but must be clear; professional business dress/grooming; face visible, smile, make eye contact with the camera; mind background noise.
- **Part 1 (1.5 min):** summary of your own section of the team research report — introduce topic + organisation, then your findings ("What did you learn / uncover / what are your findings?").
- **Part 2 (2 min):** reflection on the teamwork experience, applied to **team dynamics theory (Chapter 8)**; may draw on OTB concepts (teamwork, leadership, management, culture, power, communication, motivation, empowerment, individual attributes, behaviour, decision-making) — Chapter 8 application is the minimum requirement.
