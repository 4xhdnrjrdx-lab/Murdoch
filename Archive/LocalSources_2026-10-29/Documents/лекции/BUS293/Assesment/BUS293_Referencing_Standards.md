**BUS293 Referencing Standards**

All assessment for BUS293 must be referenced in full using **Chicago Style Referencing**.

A full detailed guide of the style accepted for BUS293 assessment work is outlined on the Murdoch Library web site at:
http://libguides.murdoch.edu.au/Chicago

Chicago referencing is based on the convention that in the text a student needs to cite:
(Author[s] family name[s], year) and page number for all direct quotes.

Please note that a professional and consistent presentation of the format used will avoid penalties during marking. One area where students often lose marks is by presenting the page number in some in-text referencing and not in others. The in-text references do not need the page numbers unless it is a direct quote in which case the page number must always be presented and the quote placed in inverted commas. However equally acceptable is for the page numbers to be presented in all cases. A consistent approach is required.

In the Reference List students can lose marks when using brackets around the years in some references and not in others. Chicago referencing does not use brackets around the years.

A sample of the required presentation of the 'List of References' is available in the Murdoch Library web site. Take note of the required indentation of second and subsequent lines of each reference (formatted using a hanging indent).

**Group Research Report:**

Minimum requirement is 20 credible references.

The requirement is that all ideas, statements and facts are acknowledged by referencing the source.

One reference at the end of a paragraph will not be an acceptable degree of referencing.

A consistent presentation of all in-text and the reference list should be checked during the editing process.

---

**Key points:**

- Referencing style: **Chicago Style** (Murdoch Library guide: http://libguides.murdoch.edu.au/Chicago).
- In-text format: (Author family name[s], year); page number required for **direct quotes** (and quote in inverted commas). Page numbers optional otherwise — but be **consistent** (either page numbers only for quotes, or page numbers in all cases).
- Reference List: **no brackets** around years; use a **hanging indent** for second and subsequent lines.
- **Group Research Report:** minimum **20 credible references**; every idea, statement and fact must be referenced; one reference at the end of a paragraph is not acceptable; check consistency during editing.
