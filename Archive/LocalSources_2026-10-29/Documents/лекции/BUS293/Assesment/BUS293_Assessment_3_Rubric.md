**BUS293 – Assessment 3: Video Presentation Marking Rubric**

**Note for students:** Part 1 of Assessment 3 is the video presentation. The rubric below explains the detailed grading criteria on a scale of 50 marks. The final mark for Part 1 will be converted and recorded out of 10 marks. Part 2 is the Self and Peer Review, which is worth 5 marks. Together, Part 1 and Part 2 make up Assessment 3: Individual Reflection and Review, worth 15% of the unit grade.

Grade bands across the top: **HD / D / C / P / N**.

---

**TIMING — /5**

- **HD (4.0–5.0):** The presentation was 3.5 minutes (within 5 seconds)
- **D (3.5–3.9):** The presentation was very close to 3.5 minutes (within 10 secs)
- **C (3.0–3.4):** The presentation was slightly under time (within 20 secs)
- **P (2.5–2.9):** The presentation was under time (within 30 secs)
- **N (0–2.4):** The presentation was well under or over time (more than 30 secs)

**CONTRIBUTION TO THE REPORT — /10**

- **HD (8.0–10.0):** The presentation includes an excellent explanation of the key findings, issues, and recommendations developed in the report. The contribution to these is very clear.
- **D (7.0–7.9):** The presentation includes a clear explanation of the key findings, issues, and recommendations developed in the report. The contribution to these is clear.
- **C (6.0–6.9):** The presentation explains the contribution to the key findings and recommendations in the report.
- **P (5.0–5.9):** The presentation does not fully explain the findings of the report, or the contribution towards these, but enough to understand the key ideas.
- **N (0–4.9):** The contribution is unclear. The presentation does not fully explain the findings of the report.

**REFLECTION — /20**

- **HD (16.0–20.0):** Integrated complex knowledge of frameworks and experience. Presented an excellent application of OB concepts, with critical analysis of the team's performance based in these concepts.
- **D (14.0–15.9):** OB concepts are discussed and applied in depth. Intelligent organisation and expansion of concepts and ideas, with sound analysis and discussion of implications involved. Effective integration of theories with teamwork experience.
- **C (12.0–13.9):** OB concepts are discussed and applied. Analysis of the issues/concepts/theories involved with clear presentation of components. Ideas generally organised and logically presented.
- **P (10.0–11.9):** OB concepts are discussed with some minor omissions. Awareness and understanding at a basic level of the major concepts, theories and frameworks involved. Flaws in thinking may be present.
- **N (0–9.9):** Discussion does not relate to the report and/or contains significant flaws such as large gaps in knowledge of key issues and/or errors in application of concepts/theories.

**PRESENTATION STYLE — /10**

- **HD (8.0–10.0):** Well-rehearsed, smooth delivery in a conversational style. Very well-paced. Expression and rhythm keep the audience listening. Correct grammar is used.
- **D (7.0–7.9):** Rehearsed smooth delivery. Expression and pace are good throughout the presentation. Correct grammar is used.
- **C (6.0–6.9):** Some sections appear rehearsed; some sections are uneven in delivery. Some distracting pacing and/or expression during the presentation.
- **P (5.0–5.9):** Appears unrehearsed, with uneven delivery. Occasional incorrect grammar is used through the presentation.
- **N (0–4.9):** The presentation is inconsistent and/or sounds like the presenter is reading. Speech is muddled and unclear. Poor grammar is used throughout.
- _Note:_ Correct grammar should reflect what is acceptable for professional/business conversations in the local country.

**PROFESSIONALISM — /5**

- **HD (4.0–5.0):** The presentation is highly professional in appearance and structure. Considered suitable for a meeting with senior managers.
- **D (3.5–3.9):** The presentation is professional in appearance and structure.
- **C (3.0–3.4):** Student's appearance is semi-professional, but presentation is well structured and delivered.
- **P (2.5–2.9):** Student's appearance is semi-professional but presentation casual. Would be considered enough for a minor business meeting.
- **N (0–2.4):** Student's appearance and delivery is unprofessional and does not meet suitable business requirements.

---

**Summary of weighting:**

- Five criteria, total **/50**: Timing (/5), Contribution to the report (/10), Reflection (/20 — the heaviest), Presentation style (/10), Professionalism (/5).
- Part 1 (video) raw /50 → **converted to /10**. Part 2 (Self and Peer Review) = **/5**. Combined = Assessment 3 (Individual Reflection and Review) = **15% of unit grade**.
- Grade band abbreviations: HD (High Distinction), D (Distinction), C (Credit), P (Pass), N (Fail/Non-pass).
