Topic 4 Workshop Activities

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.
Each team must report to the class on each of the first two activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

Activity 1:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 3, 5, 7, 8, and 9 from chapter 5 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In your teams, discuss and report your answers to the questions for the case study ‘Cincinnati Super Subs’ from chapter 5 of the textbook.

Activity 3:
Class exercise. The learning exercise.

Activity 4:
Team meeting. The team is to report to the workshop facilitator on progress towards set milestones in their plan for report completion, (e.g. final draft date, discussion section draft date, discussion section dot-point date, initial research completion date). Use of SMARTER goals criteria (see page 180 of text) is recommended. Make any needed adjustments to their plan for report completion, and record this on the team discussion forum.
