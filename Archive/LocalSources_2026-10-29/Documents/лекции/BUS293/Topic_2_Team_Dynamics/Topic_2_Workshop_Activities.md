Topic 2 Workshop Activities

Activity 1:
In groups, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 2, 4, 5, and 8 from chapter 8 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In DIFFERENT groups from activity 1, discuss and report to the class your answers to the questions for the case study ‘The Shipping Industry Accounting Team’ from chapter 8 of the textbook.

Activity 3:
Team formation and group report assessment requirement discussion.
• Completion of the team contract which is to be uploaded to the LMS Team discussion Forum.
• Each student is responsible for registering online into the correct workshop team via LMS.

Activity 4:
Team meeting.
• The team is to select a topic and decide upon an organisation.
• The team should also produce a comprehensive plan for report completion detailing ‘division of labour’ for each team member and show this to the workshop lecturer.
• This plan must also be uploaded onto the team discussion forum.
