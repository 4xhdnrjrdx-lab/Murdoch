Topic 7 Workshop Activities

At the start of class, each team needs to indicate on the whiteboard which critical thinking question and case study question they wish to report to the class. Be prepared to respond to questions regarding your answers from other members of the class.
Each team must report to the class on each of the first two activities, however, selection of which team reports on which question means that it is important that you are prepared to answer ALL.

Activity 1:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the critical thinking questions 2, 3, 6, and 7 from chapter 14 of the textbook.

Activity 2:
In your teams, discuss and report to the class your answers to the questions for the case study ‘ComsOne Bank’ from Chapter 14 of the textbook.

Activity 3:
Team exercise. Organisational culture metaphors.

Activity 4:
Team meeting. The team is to report to the workshop facilitator on progress towards milestones in their plan for report completion. The team will review the final report draft, and allocate any final tasks for report completion. This should all be recorded on the team discussion forum.
