## Grade Bands

- **HD (85+):** The Strategist
- **D (75):** The Practitioner
- **C (65):** The Analyst
- **P (55):** The Entrant
- **N (45 и ниже):** Lost in the Noise

## The Driver of Success: Strategic Depth & Effort

Success is never a "fact" — it's a hypothesis tested by the market. Grade is driven by **Strategic Effort**, measured by **depth, volume, and thoroughness** of documentation. In this "War Room" environment, document detail acts as a **Strategic Signal**:

- **High-Volume Overviews:** comprehensive, exhaustive answers show the agility and critical thinking to build a **Strategic Fortress**.
- **The Depth Requirement:** if a complex challenge requires an exhaustive deep-dive, a brief/superficial response signals unwillingness to find the "right answer."

Суть: ты должен **«sell off the risk»** своей стратегии — дать достаточно детальных доказательств и рассуждений, чтобы логика выглядела как **Safe Bet**.
