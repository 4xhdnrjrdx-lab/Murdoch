# Presentation 1: Sprint 1 Overview & Defence

**Assessment Type:** Individual Video Presentation
**Weighting:** 10% of your total grade (10 points out of a potential 20% for all presentations)
**Submission Method:** Video file upload via the LMS

## Assessment Overview

For this individual assignment, you are required to create a video presentation providing a high-level overview of your progress in Sprint 1. You will outline the activities you have completed in the sprint and critically defend the reasoning behind your decisions. The core focus is on your ability to clearly articulate what you have done thus far and strongly justify why you chose to do it.

## Assessment Criteria

Evaluated on four core areas:

- **Content Defence:** Your ability to clearly explain the logic and rationale behind your chosen marketing strategies.
- **Strategic Accuracy:** Your ability to correctly reference marketing principles and theory to back up your decisions under questioning.
- **Delivery Style:** Your professional presence, tone, and spoken pacing throughout the video.
- **Body Language:** Your effective use of gestures, posture, and eye contact within the virtual recording space.

## Guidelines for Success

- **Know the "Why":** The most successful students will be those who can confidently express their work and clearly articulate the rationale behind their strategic decisions.
- **Be Consistent:** Completing your weekly preparation sessions is your best method of preparation. This builds the theoretical "muscle memory" required to discuss marketing concepts naturally and accurately.
- **Focus on Delivery:** Practice your speaking points to ensure a confident, professional, and engaging virtual presence.

## Essential Additional Information

- **Feedback for Learning:** Brief, constructive feedback will be provided directly via the LMS after grading.
- **Academic Hurdle:** There is no academic hurdle in place for this specific assessment.
- **Citations:** You do not need to provide formal academic citations for this presentation.
- **Further Details:** Please refer to myMurdoch Learning for any additional information or specific technical submission requirements.
