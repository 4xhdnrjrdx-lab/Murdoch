# Presentation 2 — Online Flex

For your **7-minute video presentation**, you are the **"Strategic Architect"** pitching an immersive AR event to the CEO of Velocity Fit. This is an **exploratory assessment**. You aren't being marked on a single "correct" answer, but on your ability to research the future of augmented reality and envision how these emerging technologies can be leveraged to drive business conversions, then synthesising this into a cohesive event.

## The Velocity Vortex Strategic Pitch

- **The Experience Aesthetic:** Define the visual "vibe" of your retreat, such as the high-energy **"The Dream"** or a **"Relatable Reality."** Based on your research into AR marketing trends, explain how this aesthetic minimises "Cognitive Friction" and makes the user feel like a "Safe Bet."
- **The Three Pillars (Stations):** Outline three distinct virtual stations. You must include an **Elite Performance** station (to create a "Dopamine" hook), a **Daily Habit Support** station (focused on "Oxytocin" and trust), and a **Real-Time Coaching** area for discussions.
- **Breaking the Friction Wall:** Address the "AI Camera Assessment," a major friction point that causes **45% of users to drop off**. Use your research to envision a way to make this camera setup feel like a natural, "fun" part of the retreat rather than an "awkward" or "exposed" check.
- **The Community Spark:** Describe a "Shareable Moment" or digital trophy area designed to encourage users to share their experience across social groups. Explain how this advocacy turns a marketing "Itch" into a subscription "Scratch."
- **Strategic Vision & Success:** Conclude by educating the CEO on why this is the right move for the launch. Explain how your vision avoids "Analysis Paralysis" and how you will measure success through user growth and subscriptions. This section is a brief overview of everything.

## Submission Focus

This activity is designed to move you from passive entertainment into a strategic role. However, as much as you might read that AR/VR/Meta are the future, there are still a lot of questions here. This assignment is meant to be investigative into what 'might be' instead of what is. So, be a bit creative about this. Think about what would get you to attend an AR event like this.
