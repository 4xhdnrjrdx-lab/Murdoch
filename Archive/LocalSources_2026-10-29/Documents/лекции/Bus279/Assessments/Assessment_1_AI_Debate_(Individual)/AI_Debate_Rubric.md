# Debate Rubric

| Grade                           | Criteria & Student Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **85% (HD) — The Strategist**   | Demonstrates exceptional critical synthesis and active processing. Engages in a conversational back-and-forth, frequently summarising complex concepts in their own words to check understanding, and challenges the logic. **Crucial Requirement:** Must introduce **original examples** (real-world or hypothetical scenarios) and actively apply unit frameworks to test theories. Must be thorough and cover most of the weekly material. |
| **75% (D) — The Practitioner**  | Demonstrates good active processing and uses own words to check understanding. Asks highly sophisticated, complex, or ethical questions. **Shortfall:** Discusses concepts entirely in the abstract or relies on generated examples (e.g., "Give me an example of Status"). Alternatively, provides own examples but uses a rigid, static Q&A format with no conversational follow-up. Coverage of the work is incomplete.                    |
| **65% (C) — The Analyst**       | Demonstrates moderate engagement and some active processing. The student asks clarifying or follow-up questions but fails to summarise points in their own words. They rely entirely on generated examples and do not challenge the logic. While they cover a reasonable amount of the material, they lack the sophisticated questioning of a Distinction student and miss several core concepts.                                             |
| **55% (P) — The Entrant**       | Engagement is entirely passive and surface-level. Relies almost exclusively on repetitive, generic, or copy-pasted prompts (e.g., "Discuss what these sources say"). Submits static lists of basic "What is" or "Explain" questions, accepts first answers without follow-up, shows no personal voice, and provides no original examples.                                                                                                     |
| **25% (N) — Lost in the Noise** | The chat log is extremely short or fragmented (e.g., only 3 to 5 basic prompts). Fails to cover the breadth of core learning outcomes and provides no active processing, summaries, or examples.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Как это ложится в твой комплект: эта рубрика — критерии для **Assessment 1 (Debate, 20%)** из Unit Outline (8 еженедельных разборов теории). Грейд-бэнды совпадают с общим неймингом курса (Strategist / Practitioner / Analyst / Entrant / Lost in the Noise), как и в Sprint Criteria. Ключевые сигналы для верхней оценки одинаковы по всему юниту: **свои примеры**, пересказ концепций **своими словами**, оспаривание логики, применение фреймворков и полное покрытие недельного материала — а не пассивные «What is…/Explain…» вопросы.

📋 Instructions

STEPS REQUIRED

Each week, before class, you will log into NotebookLM. The link for each week will be given.

You will go through the work and have a discussion with the AI Agent.

You will then select the entire chat (this works better on a desktop/laptop), copy it, and then paste it into a Word document.

You will name this document StudentNumber Debate number, for example, 75001154 Debate 1,2,3.

On 4 June you will submit the first 3 debates and follow the schedule from thereon.

You will receive a grade based on your output.

Each debate contributes up to 2.5% of your final grade, for a total of 8x2.5=20%

You can submit as one long document or as a max of 3 documents.

NotebookLM has been set to block extremely long chats. Please use examples and don't simply click through the answers. Not giving your own examples will lead to a low grade.
