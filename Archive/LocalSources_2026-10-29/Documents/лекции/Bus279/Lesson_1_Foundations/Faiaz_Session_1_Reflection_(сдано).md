Session 1 Reflection: Introduction to Operations and Project Management
Faiaz Dzhafarov  |  Student ID: 35553299  |  Online Student
In Session 1, I learned what this unit is about and how it is structured. BUS363 Scaling Agile Projects focuses on building real project management skills, so we can turn a plan into reality. It also asks us to think about scale, ethics and sustainability from the start.
One key thing I understood is the five learning outcomes. We will learn to explain operations and project management, develop a business case, define the scope and goals of a project, apply project management tools, and build a plan to scale and internationalise a business venture. This showed me where the unit is going.
I also found the “Can Do” learning journey interesting. It shows how the major moves from the self, to others, to groups, and then to systems. I liked that the unit is linked to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), such as Zero Hunger, Reduced Inequalities, and Life Below Water. This made me realise that good projects should also create a positive impact, not just make money.
I also learned about the four assessments: an individual video (20%), a project plan (35%), a group presentation (30%), and participation (15%). Knowing the weight of each one helps me plan my time.
We looked at what makes a project succeed too. Factors like a clear goal and scope, a realistic schedule, enough resources, good communication, and strong risk management all help a project reach its outcome.
For the pre and post-workshop tasks, I watched the introduction videos and reviewed the slides and the Echo recording. They helped me see that the course is practical and based on real standards such as the PMBOK Guide. This is a useful start, and I will use these ideas in my first assessment.
