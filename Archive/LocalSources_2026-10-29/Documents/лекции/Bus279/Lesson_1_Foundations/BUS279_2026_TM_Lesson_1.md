Раздел-заголовок. _(пропускаю)_

---

**СЛАЙД 17 — «Porter's 5 Forces»** _(боковая метка: «What?»)_

- **Threat of New Entrants:** How easy is it for new companies to enter the market? High barriers to entry (e.g., high capital requirements, strong brand loyalty) reduce the threat of new competitors, while low barriers increase competition.
- **Bargaining Power of Suppliers:** How much power do suppliers have to dictate prices and terms? If there are few suppliers or they offer unique resources, their bargaining power is high, potentially squeezing industry profits.
- **Bargaining Power of Buyers:** How much power do customers have to negotiate prices? If buyers have many choices or purchase in large volumes, their bargaining power is high, putting pressure on industry prices.
- **Threat of Substitute Products or Services:** Are there readily available substitutes for your products or services? The availability of substitutes increases competition and can limit pricing power.
- **Rivalry Among Existing Competitors:** How intense is the competition among existing firms in the industry? Factors like the number of competitors, industry growth rate, and product differentiation influence the level of rivalry.

**СЛАЙД 20 — «SWOT SYNTHESIS»** _(диаграмма — текстом не извлекалась, описание ниже)_

Схема из синих блоков в два ряда, соединённых линиями (показывает, как синтезировать SWOT через картирование сред). Содержимое блоков:

_Верхний ряд (слева направо):_

- Блок 1: Strength / Weakness / Neutral points / Blind spots
- Блок 2: Strength / Weakness / In connection to the three environments
- Блок 3: Threats / Opportunities
- Блок 4: Threats / Opportunities / How do they relate to the / Three environments and the SW
- Блок 5: Threats / Opportunities / How do they relate to the / buyer

_Нижний ряд (слева направо):_

- **Map the internal environment:** Systems / Human capital / Financial resources / Internal culture / Departments / Specialised knowledge / Etc.
- **Map the micro environment:** Competitors (Primary, Secondary, Informal) / Suppliers / Community / Complementors/Contributors / Etc.
- **Map the external environment:** PESTLE
- **Synthesise** (большой пустой блок справа)

Связи: линии идут от блоков нижнего ряда (Map internal / micro / external) вверх к блокам Strength/Weakness и Threats/Opportunities, и сходятся к правым блокам — то есть три «среды» (internal, micro, external/PESTLE) питают SWOT-факторы, которые затем сводятся (Synthesise) и соотносятся с «тремя средами», SW и покупателем (buyer).

---

**СЛАЙД 21 — «Synthesising the "So What?"»**

- The goal of the SWOT Synthesis is to find the **Strategic Fit**. We use systems thinking to ask: **"How do our Strengths allow us to capture Opportunities while mitigating the Cortisol of our Threats?"**
- **Neutral Points & Blind Spots:** We acknowledge that every business has neutral facts that aren't yet strengths or weaknesses. The "Expert" monitors these to ensure they don't become blind spots that allow a **New Entrant** to slip into the market.
- **Friction Mapping:** We map the external **Threats** (External Friction) against our internal **Strengths** (Friction-reduction tools). If we can't use our strengths to lower the customer's Cortisol, we are not marketing; we are just existing.

**Термины:** Strategic Fit, systems thinking, Neutral Points, Blind Spots, Friction Mapping, External Friction, Cortisol (метафора для «угроз/трения»).

**СЛАЙД 23 — «PESTLE»** _(боковая метка: «What?»)_

PESTLE analysis is a framework used to analyse the macro-environmental factors that can affect an organisation or industry. It stands for:

- **Political:** Government policies, regulations, political stability, and trade agreements.
- **Economic:** Economic growth, inflation, interest rates, unemployment, and exchange rates.
- **Social:** Cultural trends, demographics, lifestyle changes, and consumer behaviour.
- **Technological:** Technological advancements, automation, innovation, and infrastructure.
- **Legal:** Laws, regulations, and legal frameworks related to the industry and business operations.
- **Environmental:** Environmental regulations, sustainability concerns, climate change, and resource availability.

---

**СЛАЙД 31 — «ROI, CTR, CLV, PPC»** _(боковая метка: «What?»)_

These are all important metrics used in digital marketing to measure the effectiveness and profitability of marketing campaigns.

- **ROI (Return on Investment):** A measure of the profitability of a marketing campaign. It's calculated as (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) \* 100.
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** The percentage of people who click on a link or advertisement after seeing it. It's calculated as (Clicks / Impressions) \* 100.
- **CLV (Customer Lifetime Value):** The predicted net profit attributed to the entire future relationship with a customer.
- **PPC (Pay-Per-Click):** An advertising model where you pay a fee each time one of your ads is clicked.
