# SPRINT 1B — Agency Briefing: Transitioning from Awareness to Action

**Client:** AuraFlow Energy · **Product:** Quasar Rush (250ml Slimline) · кейс «The AuraFlow Digital Launch»

## What You've Learned so far

- **The Neuro-Mix:** while **Dopamine** gets them to the site, only **Oxytocin (Trust)** makes them click "Buy."
- **The Friction Wall:** every extra second of load time and every unnecessary form field (like a mandatory SMS gate) triggers **Cortisol**, causing 95% of customers to walk away.
- **The Power of Positioning:** **Even Pricing** isn't just a number — it's a signal of quality and prestige that separates you from "supermarket bargains."

## Product Brief: Quasar Rush

- **Product Type:** Premium Functional Energy Beverage
- **Core Proposition:** "Clean Focus without the Crash"
- **The Problem:** Most energy drinks rely on high sugar and high caffeine → "jitters-and-crash" cycle: cortisol (stress) spike, then glucose drop, leaving the user more fatigued than before.
- **The Solution (formulation):**
  - **L-Theanine + Natural Caffeine** (green tea): energy of coffee with calm focus of meditation; L-Theanine "smooths out" the caffeine, neutralising jitters/anxiety.
  - **Zero Sugar:** prevents insulin spike and "brain fog" crash.
  - **B-Vitamin Complex:** supports sustained neurochemical synthesis (Dopamine and Acetylcholine).
- **Marketing Challenge:** market dominated by **Loyalty of Habit** (buy what's in the shop fridge) and **Loyalty of Identity** (extreme-sports branding). Disrupt via:
  - **Just-in-Time Marketing** using the **Recency Effect** (reach consumers at peak "decision window," e.g. mid-afternoon fatigue).
  - **Brand Salience** — high-impact digital assets that make Quasar Rush the most "available" thought when focus drops.
  - **Neuromarketing Triggers** — position the product as a **performance tool**, not a "drink."

## Task 1 — The Value Exchange (Capture)

Visitors are anonymous; convert them into "known" leads via a sign-up. **Mission:** design a "Lead Magnet" (irresistible offer) to capture their details.

- **The Component:** what exactly are you offering — physical reward (discount), informational reward (performance guide), or experiential reward (exclusive access)? _Elaborate._
- **The Path:** how will you frame the "ask"? A "Join the Flight Crew" community vibe, or a "Get 20% Off Your First Slump" value play? _Elaborate._
- **The Strategic Why:** explain how your path balances **Dopamine** (excitement of the reward) with **Oxytocin** (trust needed to share a phone number).
- **Mock-up:** quick mock-up of your 'Ask' (the sign-up pop-up box).

## Task 2 — The Manual 'Relationship' Sequence

Design the "Full Blueprint" for three emails. Each needs: a **Subject Line**, a **Hero Image Description**, **Body Copy (2–3 sentences)**, a **CTA Button**.

- **Email A — 'Welcome & Scratch' (Instant):** customer just gave their data; deliver the reward immediately in a high-interest state. _Strategy:_ how to move them from "interested" → "first-time buyer"?
- **Email B — 'We See You' (Cookie Response):** cookies show they visited the "Ingredients" page 3× but didn't buy — curious but hesitant. _Strategy:_ how to use copy to "sell off the risk"?
- **Email C — 'Rescue' (2-Week Inactivity):** no opens/visits in 14 days. _Strategy:_ a "Re-engagement" play.

## Task 3 — Transitioning to the 'Brain' (CRM & Automation)

Marketing Stack = **Mailchimp** (delivery) + **HubSpot/Salesforce** (data).

- **The Data "Warm Up":** CEO wants to buy a broker list of 5,000 "likely energy drink buyers" in your area — how do you feed it into the CRM to "warm them up" _before_ they visit your site?
- **Hint:** think "Custom Audiences" on social — use those emails to show them ads first, so your brand feels familiar when the first email arrives.

## Task 4 — The Loyalty Framework

Loyalty = a "Strategic Fortress" where it's too hard/unappealing to leave. Evaluate four **Loyalty Pillars**:

1. **Convenience (Frictionless)**
2. **Advocacy (Social)**
3. **Switching Costs (Difficulty)**
4. **Psychological (Prestige)**

**The Task:** (1) Which **two** pillars are most effective for a brand-new energy drink? (2) How would you use your CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce) to reinforce them?

> **CEO Strategic Nugget:** _"A website without a CRM is just a digital brochure. Once you have their email, you stop 'marketing' and start 'managing' a relationship. If you can predict when they are about to slump, you own the fridge."_
