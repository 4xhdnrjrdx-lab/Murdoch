## Tool descriptions (reference)

- **Salesforce:** The "Gold Standard" for enterprise data. Manages every single customer interaction across sales, marketing, and support. Powerful but complex; usually requires a dedicated team to manage.
- **HubSpot:** All-in-one growth platform. Connects your TikTok ads to your email marketing and CRM, so you can see exactly which ad creative resulted in a long-term paying subscriber.
- **Dragonfly AI:** A "Bio-Visual" tool that predicts human attention. Shows a heatmap of your ad _before_ you publish, ensuring your "Pattern Interrupt" is the first thing a user sees in the first 2 seconds of a scroll.
- **Hotjar:** A UX "eye." Records anonymous video sessions of people using the Velocity Fit app/website, helping you see exactly where they get stuck or frustrated during the AI Camera Assessment.
- **Sprout Social:** A high-level social media scalpel. Goes beyond "likes" to provide deep AI sentiment analysis — tells you if the conversation around our launch is positive, sceptical, or confused.
- **Hootsuite:** The central "Social Command Centre." Schedule posts across multiple platforms at once and monitor brand "mentions" in real-time to jump on trending conversations.
- **Mailchimp:** Email automation engine. Perfect for "Drip Campaigns" — automated emails to users who downloaded the app but haven't completed their first workout yet.
- **Brand24:** A digital "listening" tool. Crawls the entire web (news, forums, blogs) to find every mention of Velocity Fit, helping us see if we're actually building "Brand Equity" outside of social media.
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** Foundational sensor for web traffic. Tells you where visitors come from and which pages they stay on longest.
- **Canva Pro / Adobe:** The "Creative Engine." Quickly edit ad "Hooks," add captions, or tweak visual variables for A/B testing without a full production crew for every change.
- **Cometly:** Ad attribution specialist. Solves the "Data Gap" by proving exactly which specific TikTok ad led to a sale, even if the user clicked but didn't buy until three days later.
- **Klaviyo:** Advanced "Retention" marketing. Uses data to send personalised SMS/emails based on behaviour — e.g., texting a discount code the moment data shows a user is likely to quit the app.
- **Zapier / Make:** The "Digital Glue." Connects all your other tools (e.g., auto-moving a new lead from a TikTok form into HubSpot CRM) so you don't do it manually.
- **Amplitude:** Product-specific analytics. Looks "inside" the app to see which features (like AI Form Correction) are most popular and which are ignored.
- **Jasper / Copy.ai:** AI writing assistant. Generates dozens of ad headlines and captions in seconds, so the team can test many "desire triggers" quickly.
- **SEMrush:** Competitive "Intelligence." Lets us "spy" on other fitness apps — which keywords they buy and what their most successful ads look like.
- **Typeform:** Interactive data collection. Instead of a boring form, creates a "Fitness Discovery Quiz" that feels like a conversation, reducing the "Friction Wall" for new users.

## Hints (last page)

- **Platform & Positioning** — _Hint:_ Consider your target market and their daily habits. If targeting Gen Z, TikTok's high-dopamine, fast-cut style is a "sensor" that fits their behaviour better than a static Facebook post.
- **A/B Test Storyboards** — _Hint:_ A/B testing needs to test **one specific variable** (like a "How-to" hook vs. a "FOMO" hook). If you change music, actor, and headline all at once, you won't know what caused the change in performance.
- **Brand Equity** — _Hint:_ Think "Constellation of Variables." Your ad shouldn't be a one-off joke; it should add a "star" to the user's mental map, associating Velocity Fit with privacy, high-tech AI, or relatable peer success.
- **The Plan** — _Hint:_ The ad is the "Itch" and the app is the "Scratch." If users drop off at the AI Camera Assessment because they feel "awkward," your ad needs to build enough **Oxytocin** (Trust) or show a "No-Thinking" path so they feel it's worth continuing.
