Pre-work
Sprint 1 Pre-Work Questions before you start your sprint
The Target Persona: Why is it essential to give your persona a name and a specific "Pain Point" rather than just listing basic demographics?
Awareness vs. Attention: In the context of the "Attention Economy," how does a strategist break through the noise to move a customer into the Awareness stage of the funnel?
The Interest Stage: Once you have someone’s attention, what kind of "Value Proposition" is needed to keep them interested in the brand?
Friction in the Action Stage: What are some "Friction" points on a website or social media ad that might stop a customer from taking the final Action?
Expectation vs. Perception: Why is it a marketing failure if a customer's Perception of a product doesn't match the Expectation created by your advertising?
The "So What?" of SWOT: How do you use an internal Strength to combat an external Threat (like a competitor's lower price) to find a strategic fit?
STP - Positioning: In your own words, what is Positioning, and why does it rely so heavily on the specific Segment you have chosen?
Customer Journey Touchpoints: What is a "touchpoint," and can you identify one digital and one physical touchpoint a customer might experience with a brand?
The Loyalty Loop: Why is the journey not over once a customer makes a purchase, and what is the strategist's goal after the sale is made?
The Technician's Trap: If you spend your time making a "pretty" Instagram post that doesn't seem to gain any traction, what might you have missed, and what will the fix be? (This is a precursor to what we’ll be doing in the following classes, but it’s interesting to think about already.)

 
Goal: Create a "Targeting Map" for a local brand to ensure marketing spend isn't wasted on the "Marketing Mirage".

Step 1: Choose Your "Client"
Groups choose one well-known local business.
• Constraint: It must be a business where you can clearly identify a specific "Problem" they solve.

Step 2: The Persona Deep Dive
Don't just list demographics; build a Psychographic Profile. Who is the person most likely to pay a premium for this service?
• The Basics: Give them a name, age, and job.
• The "Why": What is their primary Pain Point? (e.g., "I have no time to cook healthy meals").
• The "Where": Which digital channel do they actually use when they are in "buying mode"? (LinkedIn vs. TikTok) .

Step 3: Mapping the Funnel
Map out how your Persona moves from being a stranger to a loyal customer. Be thorough here.

Step 4: The "So What?" Synthesis
• How does CAC/CLV/ROI fit into your target market?
• What do you think contributes to CAC for the company you chose? What do you think the length of time is that a person stays with them and what is the average spend per month? What would be the highest ROI marketing strategy for this company in your opinion?
