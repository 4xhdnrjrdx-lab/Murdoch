Task 4: The Behavioural Barriers (Tax vs. Friction)
As you audit the site, remember the psychological balance every user must face:
• The Tax: This is what a person is willing to pay (money, time, or energy) to get their "scratch".
• The Friction: This is the irritation or effort that stops action. If a website is slow or confusing, the friction becomes too high, and the user will not pay the "tax" .
• Your Goal: Think of a site you interacted with recently where you went through with your shopping.
o What was the ‘tax’ you paid on this site (this is not the governmental tax; this tax is specifically time, effort, and risk)? Your goal is to explain in more detail.
o Next, think of a site where you didn’t go through with your shopping; what was the ‘friction’ that was too high to go through with the purchase?
o What could the company have done to ‘downgrade’ the friction you experienced to a tax?
o Do this for each member of the group.
