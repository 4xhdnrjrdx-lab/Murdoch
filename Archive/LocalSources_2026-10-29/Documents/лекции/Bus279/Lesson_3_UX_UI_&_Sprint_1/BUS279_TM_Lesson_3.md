**СЛАЙД 8 — переходный**
Let's discuss each in turn

---

**СЛАЙД 9 — «Stylised Photo»**

- **Importance:** The primary photo is crucial for grabbing attention. It showcases the product in an appealing way, highlighting its key features and style. In this case, the jacket is shown as though on a model, allowing potential buyers to visualise it on themselves.
- **Temu's Approach:** Temu uses high-quality, well-lit images that often feature models or lifestyle settings. This helps create desire and makes the product more relatable.
- **Lessons:** Invest in professional-looking photos that clearly display your product from various angles. Consider lifestyle shots to show the product in context.

---

**СЛАЙД 10 — «Guides to the App»**

- **Importance:** Encourages app adoption, which can increase user engagement and repeat purchases. Apps often offer a smoother browsing and purchasing experience. People are much more likely to scroll Temu on a phone (in bed, on the train, in front of the TV, etc.)
- **Temu's Approach:** Prominently displays a link to download the app.
- **Lessons:** Make it easy for users to access your app if you have one. Highlight the benefits of using the app over the website.

---

**СЛАЙД 11 — «Peace of Mind»**

- **Importance:** Builds trust and reduces purchase anxiety. Assurances like secure payment gateways and easy returns are crucial, especially for new or less-known brands.
- **Temu's Approach:** Mentions "Free returns" and "Up to 90 days" return window, implying a hassle-free return process.
- **Lessons:** Clearly communicate your return policy, warranty information, and any guarantees you offer. Display security badges or certifications to build trust.
- This is part of the 'convenience' aspect. Peace of mind means a person doesn't have to worry. If there's something holding them back like fear of an item not working or not arriving, you need to address this.

---

**СЛАЙД 12 — «Free Shipping»**

- **Importance:** A major motivator for online purchases. Free shipping can often be the deciding factor for a customer.
- **Temu's Approach:** Boldly advertises "Free shipping" multiple times.
- **Lessons:** If possible, offer free shipping. If not, be transparent about shipping costs and offer various shipping options.
- Studies have shown that when a product costs, for example $99 or $89 with $10 shipping, people would rather pay $99. Try to avoid shipping fees where possible.

---

**СЛАЙД 13 — «Feels Personalised»**

- **Importance:** Creates a connection with the customer and makes them feel valued. Personalisation can increase engagement and loyalty.
- **Temu's Approach:** While not explicitly personalised in this view, Temu likely uses data to show similar items or offer recommendations based on browsing history.
- **Lessons:** Implement personalisation strategies like recommending related products, sending targeted emails, or offering exclusive discounts to loyal customers.
- People like things that are personalised to them. We want to humanise the company as much as possible wherever we can.

---

**СЛАЙД 14 — «Short Description»**

- **Importance:** Provides key information about the product quickly and concisely. Should highlight the most important features and benefits.
- **Temu's Approach:** Uses a brief description mentioning the material, style, and key features of the jacket.
- **Lessons:** Keep descriptions concise and focus on the most important selling points. Use keywords that customers are likely to search for.
- You can add 'specifications' etc., in a dropdown, for example, where you can add specifics for those who want it. Making this info readily available is important.

---

**СЛАЙД 15 — «Reviews»**

- **Importance:** Social proof is essential for building trust. Positive reviews from other customers can significantly influence purchasing decisions.
- **Temu's Approach:** Displays a star rating and the number of reviews.
- **Lessons:** Encourage customers to leave reviews. Display reviews prominently on product pages. Address negative reviews professionally and use them as an opportunity to improve.
- Showing that you address negative reviews and experiences also helps with peace of mind.
- Remember! When people don't leave reviews, the negative aspects don't go away, people simply leave.
- Try to encourage reviews as much as possible, even negative ones!

---

**СЛАЙД 16 — «How Many Sold»**

- **Importance:** Shows popularity and can create a sense of urgency (fear of missing out or FOMO).
- **Temu's Approach:** Displays "Almost sold out," suggesting limited availability.
- **Lessons:** If applicable, highlight popular items or limited-time offers. This can encourage impulse purchases.
- This also offers social proof in that others bought it and enjoyed it, so you can feel safe in doing the same.

---

**СЛАЙД 17 — «Reference Price»**

- **Importance:** Shows the perceived value of the product and makes the current price seem more appealing.
- **Temu's Approach:** Displays a discounted price alongside the original price (though it's crossed out).
- **Lessons:** If you offer discounts, clearly show the original price and the discounted price.
- People are bad at knowing what something should cost. So, we provide a reference price to show what the 'true' value is and what they could buy it for.

---

**СЛАЙД 18 — «Call to Action»**

- **Importance:** Guides the user towards the desired action, which is typically to purchase the product.
- **Temu's Approach:** Uses clear CTAs like "Add to Cart" and "Pay Now."
- **Lessons:** Use strong, action-oriented language in your CTAs. Make them visually prominent and easy to find.
- CTA is one of the most important aspects here. You need to provide something that pushes someone to make the purchase: Only a few left! / Limited time! / Limited sale! / Buy now and get a discount! / Etc.

---

**СЛАЙД 19 — «Psychological Pricing»**

- **Importance:** Uses pricing techniques to make the product seem more attractive to customers.
- **Temu's Approach:** Uses a price ending in ".12," which can make it seem more precise and therefore more believable. Also, below $30.
- **Lessons:** Experiment with different pricing strategies like charm pricing (ending in .99), odd-even pricing, or price anchoring.

---

**СЛАЙД 20 — «Variety»**

- **Importance:** Offering different options in terms of size, colour, or style caters to a wider audience.
- **Temu's Approach:** Shows different colour options for the jacket as well as sizes.
- **Lessons:** Offer a range of options whenever possible. This increases the chances of someone finding something they like.

---

**СЛАЙД 21 — «Fast Shipping»**

- **Importance:** Fast shipping is a major factor for many online shoppers.
- **Temu's Approach:** Highlights "Fast shipping" and provides an estimated delivery timeframe.
- **Lessons:** Offer fast and reliable shipping options. Be transparent about shipping times and costs.
- This is important as there's an idea that Temu products might take long to arrive. Understand what your 'unappealing' factors are and work towards remedying them or dispelling that myth.

---

**СЛАЙД 22 — «Overall Lessons from Temu's Layout»**

- **Focus on Visual Appeal:** Temu prioritises product imagery and uses a clean layout to avoid overwhelming the customer.
- **Mobile-First Approach:** Temu's layout is clearly designed with mobile users in mind, with a vertical scrolling format and clear, touch-friendly buttons.
- **Emphasis on Value:** Temu highlights discounts, free shipping, and other value propositions to make purchases more appealing.
- **Data-Driven Optimisation:** Temu likely A/B tests different layouts and elements to optimise for conversions.

**СЛАЙД 50** _(визуал-шкала)_
So, for simplification, UX is how it 'feels' when you use something. If you can use it easily and it accomplishes its goal, then it's good UX.
Схема: горизонтальная линия с узлом **UX** наверху и ответвлениями: UI, Ease of use, Performance, Functionality, Organisation, Accessibility, Emotion, Flow, Customer support.

---

**СЛАЙД 51**
User experience (UX) encompasses how a user interacts with and experiences a product, system, or service. It involves the user's perceptions of practical aspects such as utility, ease of use, and efficiency, but also more holistic elements like enjoyment and engagement.

---

**СЛАЙД 52 — «Here are some of the most important aspects of UX:»**

- **Usability:** This is arguably the most fundamental aspect. It refers to how easy and effective a product is to use.
- **Learnability:** How easy is it for users to accomplish basic tasks the first time they encounter the design?
- **Efficiency:** Once users have learned the design, how quickly can they perform tasks?
- **Memorability:** When users return to the design after a period of not using it, how easily can they reestablish proficiency?
- **Errors:** How many errors do users make, how severe are these errors, and how easily can they recover from the errors?
- **Satisfaction:** How pleasant is it to use the design?

**СЛАЙД 54 — «Usefulness»** _(фон — видео)_
Does the product or service actually do something people need or want? A beautiful, easy-to-use app is worthless if it doesn't provide value. This involves understanding user needs and goals and ensuring the product addresses them.

---

**СЛАЙД 55 — «Credibility»** _(фон — видео)_
Users must trust and believe what the product/service tells them. This is built through honest and transparent communication, providing accurate information, and demonstrating expertise. Design aesthetics and professionalism also play a role.

---

**СЛАЙД 56 — «Findability»**
Users should be able to easily find what they're looking for within the product or service. This includes clear navigation, intuitive information architecture, and effective search functionality.

---

**СЛАЙД 57 — «Desirability»**
This refers to the emotional aspect of the experience. Is the product enjoyable to use? Does it evoke positive emotions like delight, satisfaction, or even excitement? Visual aesthetics, branding, and tone of voice contribute significantly to desirability.

---

**СЛАЙД 58 — «Interaction Design (IxD)»** _(фон — видео)_
This focuses on designing interactive elements in a way that is intuitive and predictable. It involves considering how users will interact with the product and creating controls, feedback mechanisms, and workflows that support those interactions.

---

**СЛАЙД 59 — «Information Architecture (IA)»** _(фон — видео)_
This is the structure and organisation of content within the product. Good IA makes it easy for users to understand where they are, what they've found, what to expect, and what's around.

---

**СЛАЙД 60 — «Visual Design»** _(фон — видео)_
While not the sole focus of UX, visual design plays an important role in creating an engaging and aesthetically pleasing experience. It influences usability, desirability, and brand perception.

---

**СЛАЙД 61 — «Content Strategy»** _(фон — видео)_
This involves planning, creating, and managing useful, usable content. It ensures that the content presented to the user is relevant, well-written, and appropriately formatted for the context.

---

**СЛАЙД 62 — «Value»**
Ultimately, the product or service must provide value to the user. This value can be functional, emotional, social, or even financial. A good UX helps ensure the user perceives the product as worth their time and effort.

**СЛАЙД 64 — «User Research»**
This isn't a specific aspect of UX but an underlying process that informs all the others. It involves understanding users through methods like surveys, interviews, usability testing, and analytics. User research ensures that design decisions are based on actual user needs and behaviours rather than assumptions.

**СЛАЙД 65 — «Don't get confused!»**

**UX (User Experience) Design**

- **Focus:** The overall experience a user has with a product or service, from start to finish. It encompasses all aspects of the user's interaction, including their perceptions, emotions, and behaviours.
- **Scope:** Broad and holistic. It considers the entire user journey, from initial discovery and onboarding to ongoing use and support.
- **Goal:** To create a product that is useful, usable, enjoyable, and meets the user's needs and goals effectively.
- **Process:** Involves user research, information architecture, interaction design, usability testing, content strategy, and more. It's an iterative process focused on understanding and responding to user needs.
- **Deliverables:** User personas, user journey maps, wireframes, sitemaps, prototypes, user flow diagrams, and usability reports.
- **Analogy:** UX is like the skeleton and organs of the body. It provides the underlying structure and functionality that keeps everything working.

**UI (User Interface) Design**

- **Focus:** The specific look and feel of the product's interface - the visual and interactive elements that users interact with.
- **Scope:** Narrower than UX, focused on the visual design and interactivity of the product's screens or pages.
- **Goal:** To create an interface that is visually appealing, easy to understand, and intuitive to use.
- **Process:** Involves visual design, graphic design, typography, colour theory, layout design, and creating interactive elements like buttons, menus, and forms.
- **Deliverables:** Mock-ups, style guides, visual assets (icons, images), interactive prototypes.
- **Analogy:** UI is like the skin, senses, and appearance of the body. It's what makes the body visually appealing and allows for interaction with the outside world.
