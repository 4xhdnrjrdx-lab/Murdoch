# Sprint 2a: The 'Velocity Fit' Launch

**Agency:** \_\_\_ · **Client:** Velocity Fit (a new AI-driven personal training app)
**Objective:** Launch Velocity Fit using a data-first approach. Move through three rounds of A/B testing to refine creative, influencer selection, and chemical triggers.

**The 'Expert Marketer' Context:** A novice thinks they know which fitness influencer will work. An expert knows that they don't know. For Velocity Fit, we aren't just looking for "likes" — we're looking for the **Easy Path** to a subscription. We use A/B testing to find the patterns in the noise.

## Agency Dossier: Project 'Velocity Fit'

**Client:** Velocity Health Technologies · **Project:** Launch of 'Velocity Fit' (AI-Driven Personal Training App)

**1. The Product:** Velocity Fit is not just another library of workout videos. It is a **hyper-personalised AI coach** designed to remove the 'Choice Overload' (Friction) that causes 80% of people to quit their fitness routines within the first three weeks.

- **Adaptive Programming:** AI uses the smartphone camera to analyse form and 'Real-Time Fatigue' (biometrics); if the user struggles, it adjusts reps instantly.
- **The 'No-Thinking' UX:** eliminates planning — tells users what to do, for how long, at what intensity, based on goal (Weight loss, Muscle gain, or Longevity).
- **Bio-Feedback:** syncs with wearables to monitor **Cortisol** levels and sleep, suggesting 'Active Recovery' instead of a heavy workout if the user is physiologically stressed.

**2. The Company — Velocity Health Technologies:** VC-backed startup (Dubai and Singapore). Three years in R&D, a 'Strategic Fortress' of data from a closed UK Beta. Moving into your market; needs to establish **Oxytocin (Trust)** quickly in a crowded category.

**3. Preliminary Data — the 'Beta Test' Nuggets:** Company ran a 'Dark Mode' pilot (no branding) to see how personas reacted to the core value proposition.

## Round 2: The Influencer Audit & Selection

**Agency Context:** Round 1 gave you the first number: **5**. Dopamine-driven, high-energy visuals get the click. But bounce rate remains high because the "AI Camera Assessment" still feels like a **Friction Wall**. In Round 2 we look for the second number (**7**) — can a "Human Voice" bridge the trust gap? You don't intrinsically know which voice your audience will trust; you test it.

**Task 2 — The Influencer Talent Scout:** From a shortlist of 10, select **two** for a head-to-head A/B test: one **Dopamine/Aspiration**, one **Oxytocin/Relatability**. Budget covers two influencers.

**Things to Consider — The Influencer Pitfall:** "Audit the Audience." A high follower count can mask a dead audience.

- **The Curve of Truth (Retention):** A natural video has a **rounded decay curve** (gradual drop-off). A **sharp vertical cliff** then a flat line usually signals "Bought Views" (bots click and leave instantly).
- **The Engagement Gap:** Compare **Chloe V.** (850k followers, 15k likes) vs **Bec's Journey** (18k followers, 3.5k likes). Who actually has the audience's attention?
- **Watched vs. Liked Ratio:** **Urban Athlete** — 500k views, few likes = "Passive Entertainment"; people watch stunts but don't hear the message.

**The Task:** Select your two (one Aspiration, one Relatable). Justify with the numbers. Brief the content (two-sentence creative brief each — how should they introduce the "AI Camera"? as a tool for perfection (Elite) or a tool to stop feeling lost (Relatable)?). Platform selection (Insta, FB, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.). **Storyboard** both influencers. Note: same copy won't work — different target markets, and identical copy isn't A/B testing (wastes time/money).

## "Monday Morning Post-Mortem"

Monday morning is when you look at CRM data and realise your "gut feeling" was either genius or a $10,000 mistake.

**⚠ FIND YOUR INFLUENCERS TO SEE THE OUTCOME — ONLY GO TO THE NEXT PAGE IF YOU'VE FINISHED THE ABOVE.**

### The Velocity Fit "Post-Mortem" Intelligence Report

1. **Jaxson Fit — The "Ghost" Campaign:** Total Budget Loss. 200,000 "Views" but 0 clicks — bought followers from a bot farm. Lesson: **Reach ≠ Resonance.**
2. **Sarah-Jane — The "Trust" Architect:** Highest Conversion Rate. Her 12k are "Super-Fans"; laughing about being "uncoordinated" triggered massive **Oxytocin**. Lesson: she lowered the **Friction Wall** — audience didn't feel judged by the AI because they didn't feel judged by _her_.
3. **Tech-Tom — The "Analysis Paralysis" Trap:** High Traffic, Low Subscriptions. Too complex; people read whitepapers but never hit "Start Workout." Lesson: too much science = **Cognitive Friction**. Scratch the itch, don't give a lecture.
4. **Chloe V. — The "Double-Tap" Ghost:** Low ROI. "Passive Entertainment"; liked the photo, ignored the caption. **Sharp Decay Curve.** Lesson: high-quality aesthetics don't always drive high-intent action.
5. **Big Mike — The "Cultural Hero":** Breakthrough Success in the B2B/Tradie segment. Filmed at 5 AM on a construction site, tired and "normal." Lesson: Authenticity is the ultimate **Cortisol** reducer — "a tool for guys like us."
6. **Dr. Aris — The "Self-Sabotage" Specialist:** High Engagement, Zero Sales. So good at explaining form that followers fixed their own squat and left. Lesson: if your content solves the problem completely for free, there's no "Itch" left to scratch.
7. **Luna Sol — The "PR Liability":** Campaign Terminated / Legal Headache. Arrested for trespassing on a heritage site; brand logo visible; dragged into a news cycle. Lesson: when you hire an influencer, you marry their reputation — vet for **Brand Safety.**
8. **Coach Kev — The "Aggression" Backfire:** Massive Uninstalls. "No Excuses" yelling was a **Cortisol Spike** for new users; they felt bullied. Lesson: aggressive marketing works on a "Hot" lead; it scares a "Cold" lead.
9. **Bec's Journey — The "Organic Engine":** Elite ROI / Viral Growth. Followers advocate; post shared into 50+ "Weight Loss Support" Facebook groups — you paid for a post, got a community. Lesson: 18% engagement is a **Statistical Goldmine** — the "9" and "11" in your sequence.
10. **Urban Athlete — The "Darwin Award" Finalist:** Brand Boycott. Backflip off a moving train; Public Transport Authority condemned the "Velocity Fit stunt"; CEO had to apologise publicly. Lesson: high-Dopamine "Stunt" content is high-risk — if the "Aha!" moment is a safety violation, the brand loses all **Oxytocin** (Trust).

### Recovery or Acceleration Storyboard (the "Positioning Framework")

For each of your two chosen influencers, storyboard four frames:

- **The Position:** Where are we now? (Scandal, bot-fraud, or viral success.)
- **The Problem:** What happens if we stay here? (Brand death, wasted budget, or missed opportunity.)
- **The Action:** The specific pivot or scale-up move.
- **The Expected Effect:** How this fixes the "Sequence" and gets us back to the "11."

**Expert Summary:** Each storyboard frame is a data point. Negative influencer → Damage Control. Positive influencer → Optimisation. **Submission:** Present your two storyboards. Your Tutor will assess whether your "Action" actually addresses the specific "Problem" from the Post-Mortem Report.
