## Overview / context

- **Zalando** is a fashion platform founded in **2008**, headquartered in **Berlin**, and is described by IDEO as one of Europe's fastest-growing tech companies.
- Scale at the time of the case study: headcount had grown to **15,000** employees; revenue was growing **23% year over year**. IDEO states that **more than €1 of every €100 spent on clothing in Europe** is spent at Zalando.
- The fashion industry backdrop: digital platforms and tools are reinventing fashion — e-commerce reshaped the shopping experience and social media opened up a once-exclusive industry.
- **The trigger (2014):** Zalando co-founder and CEO **Robert Gentz** approached IDEO to think about the company's future amid increasing competition. Gentz wanted to build **design-thinking capabilities** across the company so Zalando could make systemic business shifts — better customer experiences, and balancing operational excellence/execution with innovation.
- The engagement created an innovation lab at Zalando's Berlin HQ called **The Studio**. Over the three years after launch, The Studio helped build new products, experiences and ways of working that drove a digital transformation across Zalando and the wider fashion world.

**Quoted positioning (IDEO/Zalando voice):**

- Philipp Erler (Senior Vice President of Digital Experience, Zalando) framed The Studio as focused on both elevating the role of design within Zalando and building bridges between strategy and product development.

## The strategic shift

- The core strategic move: shift Zalando from a pure **e-tailer (online retailer)** to a **full-blown fashion platform**.
- Robert Gentz's personal framing of the brief to IDEO: he wanted his daughter to be proud of the company he had created.

---

## Phase 1 — Finding purpose

- First task: lock in the new platform strategy and resurface the organisation's **purpose**.
- Method: a series of **interviews and workshops** across the company, from which a unifying intent emerged:
  - **"Zalando exists to reimagine fashion, for the good of all."**
- To spread that message, the team designed a **five-day "Festival of Purpose"** at the Berlin HQ and satellite locations, plus a long-term plan to help leaders and teams weave purpose into their everyday work.
- (Video referenced: how Zalando used purpose to mobilise its community.)

---

## Phase 2 — Building innovation capacity

- With a shared purpose, Zalando began designing and building quickly. The Studio experimented and prototyped, launching products such as:
  - **Seen At** — a mobile app letting members upload street-style photos.
  - **Collabary** — a digital tool connecting online fashion influencers with brands.
- These projects pushed cross-disciplinary teams to practise **aligning around joint goals, making fast decisions, and building with agility**.
- **The Studio as a hub (capability transfer):**
  - Zalando employees from across business units rotated into The Studio, teaching IDEO designers about their business and company culture, while IDEO helped them master the **design-thinking process** so they could lead future projects themselves.
  - **"Studio graduates"** ensure that whatever is prototyped there actually gets **implemented**, and they fold what they learned into their day jobs.
  - These graduates become **design ambassadors**, spreading experimentation, prototyping and storytelling across the organisation.
- (Video referenced: how Zalando pioneered a new innovation studio.)

---

## Phase 3 — Teaching leaders how to scale change

- The hard problem: it's one thing to work differently inside a small innovation lab, but how do you **scale change across the whole organisation** and account for its effect on each major business unit — **product, technology, commercial, and logistics**?
- IDEO interviewed Zalando employees to understand how to embed a method they called **Ways of Working (WoW)** — a method that **combines design thinking with lean and agile approaches**.
- **Deployment of WoW:**
  - Rolled out by a **centralised team** that ran **workshops and coaching sessions** to help leaders adopt it.
  - Impact measured through **OKRs (Objectives and Key Results), KPIs (Key Performance Indicators), and NPS (Net Promoter Score)**.
  - The team also helped leaders' teams understand the new processes.
- **Scale of rollout:** Ways of Working was being rolled out across the **entire organisation, reaching all 15,000 employees**.
- **Reported outcome:** the multi-year IDEO–Zalando change project is credited with spurring **double-digit growth**, while advancing the "reimagine fashion, for the good of all" vision.
- (Video referenced: how Zalando scaled new ways of working across the organisation.)
