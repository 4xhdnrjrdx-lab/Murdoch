## Part 1 — Introduction and Definition

- The lesson explores various approaches to optimising business operations.
- **Business optimisation** = the process of maximising desired outcomes/factors while simultaneously minimising undesired ones.
- Core purpose: construct new processes, develop new resources, and make the business as effective as possible.

## Part 2 — The Four-Step Optimisation Process

1. **Identify and define:** pick a problematic process that needs optimising and clearly define its purpose and specific goals.
2. **Analyse:** evaluate the current process to see whether it's meeting the goals set in step 1.
3. **Eliminate:** remove any unnecessary elements found within the process.
4. **Monitor:** continuously track the process until the desired results are achieved.

## Part 3 — Practical Examples

**Example 1 — Call centre operations:**

- Metric: customer satisfaction.
- Method: measure satisfaction by asking customers for direct feedback.
- Optimisation technique: pursue better ratings by offering **incentives for employees** who achieve high customer satisfaction.

**Example 2 — Order picking in distribution centres:**

- Metric: turnaround time for order picking.
- Optimisation technique: reorganise the physical layout of the distribution centre.
- Specific strategy: place items that are commonly ordered together in **close proximity** to reduce picking time.

## Part 4 — Conclusion and Further Learning

- Many different approaches to optimisation exist; it's up to each company to decide which best suits its circumstances.
