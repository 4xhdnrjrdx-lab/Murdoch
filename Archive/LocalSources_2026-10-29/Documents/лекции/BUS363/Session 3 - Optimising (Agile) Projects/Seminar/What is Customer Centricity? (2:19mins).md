## Lecture framing (Session 3 slide)

An organization is only truly customer-centric when the viewpoint of the customer plays a pivotal role in its decisions, processes and expected behaviours. Customer centricity is both a personal choice and a company requirement, as organizations understand that every action influences customer experience — positively or negatively.

## Part 1 — Definition and the Coffee-Shop Analogy

- Explained via a **coffee shop**:
  - **Customer service vs customer centricity:** coffee-shop employees provide customer service directly to the consumer; customer centricity is different — it happens far from the physical "brick and mortar" location.
  - **Focus:** customer centricity is about internal **processes, procedures, and the daily decisions** every employee makes.
  - **The "filter" concept:** it's a "filter" everyone working behind the scenes must have, ensuring all processes/procedures are focused specifically on the customer.

## Part 2 — Economic Context and Intentionality

- **Consumer-led economy:** today's economy is driven by consumers, with change happening faster than ever.
- **Feedback loops:** social media created a "frenzy of feedback" / "instant feedback," making customer centricity essential for survival and success.
- **Defining the experience:** customer centricity lets an organization define exactly what the consumer/customer will experience.
- **Intentionality:** it begins with intentionality — employees waking up each day thinking about the customer, so every decision is focused on the customer experience.

## Part 3 — The Three-Step Framework for Employees ("See, Own, Do")

1. **See the opportunities:** identify opportunities for improvement/customer focus within your function, your role, or the whole organization.
2. **Take ownership:** be accountable — treat it as your personal responsibility to see those opportunities through.
3. **Do something about it:** take action — whether performing the task yourself or leading a group to do it.

- Goal: make the resulting customer experience **intentional**, not accidental.

## Key terms & references

- **Customer centricity:** a mindset / organizational "filter" where internal processes, procedures and daily employee decisions focus specifically on the customer experience.
- **Customer service:** direct assistance to customers at the point of interaction (the "brick and mortar").
- **Consumer-led economy:** consumers drive market speed and feedback.
- **Intentionality:** deliberate, daily employee focus on prioritising the customer.
- **See, Own, Do framework:** identify opportunities → take accountability → take action.
