Net-Works is a social, environmental and commercial solution tackling the problem of discarded fishing nets, by Interface and ZSL.

The programme evolved from a need to find a better way of doing business. By working together in close partnership, Interface and ZSL are creating a solid business solution with a long-term positive impacts on marine and freshwater ecosystems while also providing financial opportunities to some of the poorest people in the world.

## Part 1 — The Net-Works Program and Social Enterprise

- Business can be done while contributing to serious environmental objectives — here, cleaning the oceans of marine debris.
- **Net-Works** is a **social enterprise**, not a charity.
- The cycle: discarded fishing nets are collected from coastal communities → recycled into **carpet fibre** → used in **modular flooring**. An "inclusive business model" showing a better way of doing business beyond philanthropic giving.

## Part 2 — The Operational Process and Value Chain

- **Collection:** local community groups collect nets from beaches and the sea.
- **Prevention:** they also collect "end-of-life" nets directly from fishers to stop them entering the environment in the first place.
- **Supply chain:** the community sells collected nets into a **global supply chain**.
- **Regeneration:** nets are processed and regenerated into **nylon yarn**.
- **Manufacturing:** the yarn is used to make Interface's carpet products.
- Core goal: retain as much of the nets' value as possible within the community.

## Part 3 — Community Impact and Financial Inclusion

- **Economic value:** community members now see discarded plastics as a source of income ("pesos and dollars") rather than just waste.
- **Financial habits:** the program introduced the concept of **saving money** — previously unfamiliar or deemed impossible for many.
- **Future planning:** saving lets community members plan ahead, especially for their **children's education**.

## Part 4 — Program Success, Partnerships, and Expansion

- Described as a "huge success"; the initial pilot is now a viable model.
- **Expansion goals:** scale further and influence the wider manufacturing community to adopt **inclusive business opportunities** in their own supply chains.
- **Partner:** **ZSL (Zoological Society of London)**.
- **Funding:** ZSL was awarded two three-year programs from the **Darwin Initiative** to fund marine conservation in remote areas, with Net-Works integral to those programs. [Detail beyond the lecture — verify.]

## Part 5 — Paradigm Shift in Waste Management

- The project aims to trigger a **paradigm shift** in how the world views plastics — by assigning a monetary value to plastic waste, creating a model replicable worldwide to tackle the global plastic problem.

## Key terms & references

- **Net-Works:** social-enterprise program recycling discarded fishing nets into carpet tiles.
- **Social enterprise:** a business pursuing social/environmental objectives alongside financial ones.
- **Inclusive business model:** includes low-income communities in the value chain (as suppliers, etc.).
- **Value chain:** ghost/end-of-life nets → nylon yarn → modular flooring (carpet tiles).
- **Partners/funding:** ZSL (Zoological Society of London); Darwin Initiative (two three-year grants).
- **Company:** Interface (initiative owner; carpet-tile manufacturer).
- **Target:** marine debris / discarded fishing nets.
