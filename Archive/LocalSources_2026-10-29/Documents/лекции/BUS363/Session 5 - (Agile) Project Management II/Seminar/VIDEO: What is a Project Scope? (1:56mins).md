## Part 1 — Defining Project Scope and the Scope Statement

- Opens with the idiom "don't bite off more than you can chew" to stress that defining project scope is an essential step in project planning.
- **Project scope** = determining and documenting a comprehensive list of specific project elements:
  - project goals, deliverables, tasks, costs, deadlines.
- Primary purpose: inform stakeholders while keeping the team focused and on task.
- The formal documentation produced is the **scope statement**, which establishes:
  - **Project boundaries** — what is and isn't part of the project.
  - **Responsibilities** — duties for each team member.
  - **Approval procedures** — how work will be officially approved.

## Part 2 — Role and Benefits of the Scope Statement

A well-articulated scope statement ensures all stakeholders understand exactly what's involved, and serves several functions:

- **Project roadmap** — for assigning tasks, scheduling work, and setting budgets.
- **Team alignment** — focuses team members on common objectives.
- **Vision preservation** — stops the project expanding beyond the established vision.
- It's a critical component of effective project management; a scope should be determined for **every** project, regardless of size or management methodology.

## Part 3 — Managing Evolution and Scope Creep

- A project is expected to evolve over its lifecycle, so better scoping at the start — including **guidelines for change requests** — lets the team manage necessary changes more effectively.
- Being as specific as possible when developing scope helps avoid **scope creep**: when parts of a project end up needing more work, time, or effort due to poor planning or miscommunication.

## Key terms & references

- **Project scope:** determining/documenting goals, deliverables, tasks, costs, deadlines.
- **Scope statement:** formal document establishing boundaries, responsibilities, and approval procedures.
- **Stakeholders:** those who must be informed and understand what the project entails.
- **Change requests:** guidelines included in initial scoping to manage evolution.
- **Scope creep:** project expansion requiring extra time/effort due to poor planning or miscommunication.
