## Part 1 — The "Wide Umbrella" Concept

- The **PMBOK® Guide 7th Edition** is a significant shift from previous versions, visualised as a **"wide umbrella"** sitting at the top of project-management practice — its principles and performance domains are designed to be universal.
- **Universal compatibility:** designed to work with any delivery method.
- **Methodologies it covers under the umbrella:** PRINCE2, Extreme Programming (XP), Citizen Developer, Critical Chain, SAFe, and all past editions of the PMBOK® Guide.
- **Integration:** can be used alongside the **PMBOK® 6th Edition** — the 7th provides the over-arching framework for behaviour and results.

## Part 2 — Structure of the Guide

When you get the PMBOK® Guide 7th Edition, it actually bundles **two distinct books**:

1. **The Standard for Project Management** — the inner ("purple") area containing the **principles**.
2. **The PMBOK® Guide** — the section covering the **performance domains**, the practical answer to the principles.

- Visual model: a circular diagram where the **12 Principles** form an inner circle and the **8 Performance Domains** form the outer ring. There are no "dotted lines" — all elements circulate and shift together simultaneously.

## Part 3 — The 12 Principles (behavioural guides)

Principles **guide behaviour**; they aren't "practised" through exercises but must be **felt and experienced**. Many seem obvious, yet most people fail to actually implement them.

1. **Stewardship** — being a "responsible adult": following rules, being ethical, guiding behaviour to deliver the best for the project.
2. **Team** — operating as a collaborative unit for the best results.
3. **Stakeholders** — engaging all connected parties (suppliers, providers, internal groups) to deliver the project.
4. **Value** — delivering value "above and beyond" the **Golden Triangle** (Scope, Cost, Time, Quality); delivering on time/budget is pointless if the result has no value.
5. **System thinking** — recognising, evaluating and responding to system interactions; a project isn't isolated and relies on many other factors.
6. **Leadership** — demonstrating leadership regardless of role (not exclusive to the PM or Scrum Master); everyone should be a role model.
7. **Tailoring** — not taking things for granted; customising methodologies (e.g. XP, FEL) to specific needs. The guide has a full chapter on this.
8. **Quality** — being **customer-centric**, knowing what the customer expects, producing deliverables that meet required quality.
9. **Complexity** — navigating complex environments without oversimplifying.
10. **Risk** — optimising risk responses; closely tied to adaptability.
11. **Adaptability & resilience** — the ability to adapt, cited as a "key competency" for modern PMs.
12. **Change** — embracing change as necessary evolution rather than something to avoid.

These principles are often applied in a **VUCA environment** (Volatility, Uncertainty, Complexity, Ambiguity).

## Part 4 — The 8 Performance Domains (activities and work)

Performance domains are groups of activities/work to exercise to deliver results (compared to "small booklets" within the guide):

1. **Stakeholder** — engaging stakeholders.
2. **Team** — deploying and developing a team, defining roles and responsibilities.
3. **Development Approach and Lifecycle** — selecting the best method: **Agile, Iterative, Predictive (Waterfall), or Hybrid**.
4. **Planning** — defining the work.
5. **Project Work** — execution and day-to-day activities (e.g. managing procurement).
6. **Delivery** — producing the expected deliverables to provide intended value.
7. **Measurement** — evaluating whether the project is reaching its objectives.
8. **Uncertainty** — addressing complexity, risks, and unknowns.

Vargas notes the **Planning, Project Work, and Delivery** domains are very closely connected to the content of the **PMBOK® 6th Edition**.

## Key terms & references

- **PMBOK® Guide 7th Edition:** PMI's latest standard, organised around principles + performance domains.
- **The Standard for Project Management:** the part containing the **12 principles**.
- **12 Principles:** Stewardship, Team, Stakeholders, Value, System Thinking, Leadership, Tailoring, Quality, Complexity, Risk, Adaptability/Resilience, Change.
- **8 Performance Domains:** Stakeholder, Team, Development Approach/Lifecycle, Planning, Project Work, Delivery, Measurement, Uncertainty.
- **Golden Triangle:** traditional constraints — Scope, Cost, Time (often including Quality).
- **VUCA:** Volatility, Uncertainty, Complexity, Ambiguity.
- **Methodologies under the umbrella:** PRINCE2, XP, SAFe, Citizen Developer, Critical Chain.
- **Tailoring:** adjusting the PM approach to the specific environment/requirements.
- **PMI:** the body that publishes the PMBOK Guide.
- **Presenter:** Ricardo Vargas.
