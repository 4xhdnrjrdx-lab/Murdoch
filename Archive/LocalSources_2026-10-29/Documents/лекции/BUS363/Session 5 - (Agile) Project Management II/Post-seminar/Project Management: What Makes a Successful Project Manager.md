## Part 1 — Communication and the Role of the Project Manager

- **Communication** is a key skill for project managers — they're estimated to spend up to **90% of their time** communicating. [Commonly-cited PM figure — verify before citing.]
- This communication happens through various channels: meetings, phone calls, emails, letters.
- Duties/responsibilities vary between projects and companies, but certain tasks are common to all project managers.

## Part 2 — Common Duties and Responsibilities

- **Project planning** — developing the initial roadmap for the project.
- **Stakeholder interaction** — communicating and interacting with key stakeholders.
- **Resource management** — securing necessary project resources.
- **External relations** — interacting with outside clients, vendors, and suppliers.
- **Implementation** — initiating the project implementation phase.
- **Monitoring and adjustment** — continually monitoring progress, reviewing interim objectives, adjusting project elements as needed.
- **Team management** — managing the team, including delegating tasks and providing feedback.
- **Problem solving** — identifying opportunities and problems and developing solutions/adjustments.
- **Conflict management** — managing and resolving conflicts.
- **Mentorship** — consulting with advisers, mentors, and coaches.

## Part 3 — Skills and Characteristics for Success

- **Leadership and self-confidence** — leading the team with assurance.
- **Pragmatism and empathy** — balancing practical needs with understanding of others.
- **Effective meetings** — conducting meetings efficiently.
- **Judgment** — sound judgment and the ability to resolve conflicts.
- **Negotiation** — negotiating and influencing others.
- **Team dynamics** — team building and motivation.
- **Analysis and decisions** — decision-making, analysis, and managing data and impact.
- **Strategic perspective** — detail-oriented while keeping the "big picture" in view.
- **Interpersonal traits** — listening, generosity, flexibility, humour, calmness.
- **Feedback** — giving and receiving criticism effectively.
- **Professional awareness** — awareness of PM tools, political and cultural awareness, general management skills.

## Key terms & references

- **Communication:** cited as ~90% of a PM's time.
- **Stakeholders:** key individuals/groups the PM interacts with throughout the project.
- **Project planning:** the foundational duty of defining the project's direction.
- **Resource management:** securing the tools and people needed for success.
- **Project monitoring:** ongoing checking of progress against objectives.
- **Big picture:** seeing the overarching goal while managing details.
- **PM tools:** professional software/methodologies the PM must be aware of.
