## Part 1 — The Importance of Planning and the Cost of Failure

- Planning is a critical skill; the phrase "I should have planned that better" usually signals a result that didn't meet expectations. Failing to plan has real personal and professional costs.
- **Consequences of poor planning — loss of:** time, money, energy, credibility, business opportunities, relationships.
- In a career context, a lack of planning can lead to a **failed project, a failed company, or even the loss of a job**.

## Part 2 — Project Basics and the Planning Framework

- A project has a **specific start date and end date**, delivering a product, service, or information by a certain date — distinct from **ongoing operational work**.
- **The planning process answers four fundamental questions:**
  1. **Why** — why are we doing this project? (the ultimate goal / big picture).
  2. **What** — what specific products/services are delivered? This is the **scope**.
  3. **Who** — who are the **people resources** doing the work?
  4. **How** — what's the **process** so everyone knows what to do, when, and where?
- Visually, planning can be seen as a timeline (e.g. a five-month span) with **deliverables** due at different points.

## Part 3 — The Deliverables Diagram Methodology

- A key planning tool is the **Deliverables Diagram**, which breaks a project into packages of deliverables. Example — **building a house:**
  - **Package 1:** foundation, framing, roof.
  - **Package 2:** plumbing and wiring (delivered together, inspected after package 1 is complete).
  - **Package 3:** inside appliances, landscaping, security systems.

## Part 4 — From Deliverables to Tasks and Resources

- Once high-level deliverables are set, subdivide them into **sub-work / tasks**:
  - **Task identification:** for each deliverable (e.g. the foundation), determine the specific tasks required.
  - **Duration estimation:** determine how long each task will take.
  - **Assignments:** turn tasks into assignments for the people resources.

## Part 5 — Finalizing the Project Plan

- Compile the gathered information into the project's foundation:
  - **Timeline:** from the start date, end date, and estimated task durations.
  - **Project cost:** compile all project **expenses**.
  - **Collaboration process:** establish the workflow so team members know how to work together to produce the result.
- Recommends planning software (projectmanager.com) to manage these steps.

## Key terms & references

- **Project planning:** defining the **why / what / who / how** of delivering a project.
- **Deliverables:** products, services, or information a project produces.
- **Scope:** the specific deliverables a project commits to producing.
- **Deliverables Diagram:** visual tool breaking a project into packages and sub-tasks.
- **People resources:** individuals assigned to perform tasks.
- **Process:** the established method for team coordination/execution.
- **Timeline / project cost:** schedule (start/end + durations) and compiled expenses.
- **Ongoing operational work:** continuous work with no defined project end date.
- **Source:** ProjectManager.
