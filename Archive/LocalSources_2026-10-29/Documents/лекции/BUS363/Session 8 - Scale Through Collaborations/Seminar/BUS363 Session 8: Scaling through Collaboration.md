## How to Grow & Scale Quickly? (core topic of session)

The body of the session covers the channels for scaling **through collaboration**: collaborating with other brands, working with influencers/guests, building publicity, using online collaboration tools, and managing in-person collaboration.

---

## Collaborate with Other Brands

- **Joint projects.**
- **Promoting each other on Social Media** — e.g. tags on Instagram, Instagram/Facebook stories, YouTube channels promoting products, etc.
  - Partner should be a **similar brand / associated / NOT a competitor**.
- **Sponsoring / Contributing to events** — e.g. sporting events, trade expos, conferences, etc.

_Example collaborations cited:_ KIA × Uber (free rides); Vans × Marvel; Uber × Spotify.
_Sources:_ kicksonfire.com/vans-x-marvel-collaboration/ ; brandessencenigeria.com/brand-collaboration-kia-partners-with-uber-to-provide-free-rides/

---

## What to Look for in a Collaboration Partner

Three criteria for choosing a brand collaboration partner:

1. **Shared target markets + similar buyer personas.**
2. **Complimentary [complementary] brand identity.**
3. **Similar marketing goals.**

_Example collaborations cited:_ Stüssy × Nike; Nike × Apple; MINI × The Woolmark Company.
_Sources:_ ampjar.com/blog/collaborative-marketing-guide/ ; marketing91.com/brand-collaboration/

---

## Influencers / Guests

- Influencers "come in different forms and shapes": consultants, analysts, journalists, academics, regulators, YouTube celebrities, homebased business owners, stay-at-home parents, etc.
- **Guest posting** might be an option if you have a blog-based business.
