## 1 Unit Details

### 1.1 Unit Description

This unit concentrates on developing the skills needed to transform a plan into reality. Students build practical project management skills and knowledge, to enable them to manage business strategy and incorporate scale and internationalisation while considering ethics and sustainability from ground up.

### 1.2 Learning Outcomes

This unit enables you to demonstrate your achievement of the following unit learning outcomes.

### 3.1 Approach to learning and teaching

Through different workshops, individual and collaborative exercises and sharing times, each student will learn the value and importance of creativity and innovation in an organization and how to develop it.

So that the course is the most profitable for everyone, consider the following points:

- Attendance and e-attendance is HIGHLY required for face-to-face and online students.

Please maintain a very fluid communication with the unit coordinator and peers to clarify any question and avoid any issue in your learning process.

### 3.2 Expected time commitment for studying this unit

As this is a 3 credit point unit, we expect you to spend on average 12.5 hours per week for the total weeks of this teaching period (or 150 hours overall) working on this unit. This includes the 12 x 2-hour face-to-face workshop sessions (delivered weekly), throughout the trimester.

Students are required to complete reading and activities before and after each workshop. Each workshop has three interconnected components: pre-workshop, workshop, and post-workshop. The workshop activities are designed to build on the knowledge gained through reading the required chapters and watching the lecture podcasts.

- **Pre-workshop** will include video podcasts, online and textbook readings, and other learning activities.
- **Workshops** will include discussion questions, case studies, and other applied learning activities.
- **Post-workshop** will include summarising, collaborating, and reflecting on the topic/s covered.

For details, please refer to LMS for the latest information regarding pre, post and in-class learning requirements.

Student will be participating in weekly (individual and group) learning activities, which students will need to prepare for by completing the pre and post workshop tasks. This will also inform and contribute towards part of the student's Assessment 4: Participation & Engagement marks, which will be assessed by the lecturer throughout the unit.

**Students are required to maintain at least 80% attendance across all learning and teaching sessions.** See Murdoch Dubai Attendance Policy.

### 5.2 Assessment information

#### 5.2.1 Assessment 1 — Project Idea, Individual Video Presentation (20%)

**Assessment Overview**
This assignment is designed to demonstrate your understanding and application of operational project management concepts and ability to scale projects.

**Task / Where to Submit**
Make a video of the process of producing / constructing / creating an item of your choice (e.g., assembling a bedside table, building a garden glasshouse, making a meal, installing shelving, creating a vertical garden etc.) and identify the processes that make up the operation. Then include a series of recommendations for improving the agility, scale, effectiveness, and efficiency of the process.

Make sure that you:

- Cover key concepts covered in the workshops on Operations Management
- Provide recommendations on how to improve the process.
- Clearly communicate and demonstrate the creativity of your ideas.

Other important guidelines:

- The duration of the video should be up to 5 minutes.
- Your face should be visible in the video throughout the entire video.
- Make sure you introduce yourself (state your name, surname, and a student ID) at the start of the video.
- Use your phone or camera to film the video.
- Speak clearly, be informative and engaging.

#### 5.2.2 Assessment 2 — Individual Project Plan (35%)

**Assessment Overview**
This assignment is designed to demonstrate your understanding and application of core (agile) project management concepts, provide evidence of project management skills developed throughout this unit and application of an agile project management approach.

**Scenario**
You are a Project Manager at an IT Development Company. You have been assigned to deliver a project that is worth **$500,000 fixed price over 6 months**. It is a software development project — your team is supposed to develop a new version of the **"Murdoch Student LMS App"**. The requirements are all understood and signed off.

Your task is to present an approach to starting the project up, proposed project timeline, how you would monitor/control the project, how you would manage stakeholders and your approach to commercial control. Keep it high level, aiming the presentation at management rather than technical.

Your project plan should clearly define the following:

- The project's objectives (i.e., vision, goals, objectives, deliverables etc.)
- The scope of work to be completed within the timeline (i.e., scope statement, time, budget, resources etc.)
- Proposed plan for the delivery (i.e., project approach, WBS, key deliverables, team, resources, quality etc.)
- Billing schedule (i.e., budget / cost allocations, distribution of payments etc.)
- Key milestones (i.e., Gantt Chart, Critical Milestone Timetable / Project Schedule, Billing schedule etc.)
- Examples of risk and issue logs (i.e., risk matrix, risk management / mitigation strategy etc.)
- Your approach to reporting and stakeholder management (stakeholder matrix, communication strategy etc.)

All the above-mentioned points should be presented in **PowerPoint** (please see example on LMS). Marking emphasis of this assignment will be on critical evaluation and demonstration of an understanding of the principles in project management.

**Where to Submit**
The Project Plan should be submitted via LMS by the assigned due date (see section 5.4 regarding penalties for late submissions). **No re-submission is available.**

#### 5.2.3 Assessment 3 — 'Scaling your Venture' Group Presentation (30%)

**Assessment Overview**
This assignment is designed to demonstrate your knowledge and skills in scaling one's business (project) venture. This presentation is to be recorded and submitted in a **10-minute video format**.

You should develop and present a feasible proposition on how to scale your agile business model. This assignment should be completed in **groups of 2–4 (max)**. You will need to form groups yourself; however, if you don't form a group by **Week 8**, you may be assigned to a group of students you don't know — so best, find a team you prefer to work with and who share your values and academic aspirations.

Your task is to present with your group a recorded 10-minute video presentation on how you are going to scale your agile business venture, based on the knowledge learnt throughout the unit i.e., scaling through technology, looking for collaborations, searching for funding options etc., as presented and discussed during workshop sessions.

You can choose the topic of your business venture; however, you should confirm your topic with your lecturer by **Session 9 (Week 9)** at the latest.

Examples of suitable topics ('businesses to scale'):

- Selling an Australian product, service and/or brand in a foreign market i.e., Asia, Europe, US or other regions / countries etc.
- Scaling your business venture / project to a broader community i.e., other organisations, across different national and/or global regions etc.
- Digitalising your business to be accessible from anywhere across the globe
- Offering your services to a new community, area, region, or country
- Making a Perth-based shop international using e-commerce
- And similar!

**Where to Submit**
The video should be submitted via LMS on the assigned due date. Please submit the link to a YouTube video (as unlisted) via LMS (same as in Assignment 1). See guidelines on LMS how to do that. **No re-submission is available after the due date.**
