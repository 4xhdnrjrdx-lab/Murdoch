## Key Indicators for a Resilient Ecosystem

- **6.5×** increase in ecosystem activity over the last 10 years.
- **3×** more organisations participating in 2017–2022 than in the preceding 5 years.
- **3.2×** increase in collaboration connections (people/orgs/government) from 2017 to 2022 — **3,570+** connections established across the ecosystem.

### Accelerator Programs

Definition used: fixed-term, cohort-based programs that accelerate commercialisation of an early-stage innovation, providing mentorship, education and often a small seed investment **in return for equity**.

Data:

- **29** accelerators have existed in WA; **54+ cohorts**; **450+ participants**.
- **First in WA:** RAC SeedSpark (2012). **Longest running:** Curtin Accelerate (since 2015). **Average accelerator lifespan:** 5.5 years.
- Yearly participation has **2×'d in the last 5 years**.
- Accelerator participant gender diversity has stayed **flat at ~36%** for the last 4 years.

Current/example WA accelerators (with focus where given — _mapping approximate_): Propel Program; ScaleUpWA; UWA Venture; We are Emersyn; Agristart Harvest; Biodesign; Bloom Launchpad; Core Start; Curtin Accelerate; Impact 2 Innovate; Plus Eight Sprint; Plus Eight Accelerator. Focus tags appearing in the table: XR/Sustainability/Ocean Tech; SMEs; Youth; Female; Agriculture; Health; Resources; Commercialisation; Social Impact; Idea-stage; Growth.

**Case studies:**

- **Curtin Accelerate** (Geremy Farr-Wharton, Manager – Curtinnovation Entrepreneurship Programs): grows entrepreneurial capability and go-to-market skills; founders receive a **$5,000 equity-free grant** plus Curtin infrastructure/resources/networks. **60+ businesses** have participated.
- **Bloom Launchpad** (Lisa Longman, Head of Programs, Bloom): 12-week course. In 2019, **Ashleigh Small & Chris Dickson** founded **Hello Initiative** (improving social/judicial outcomes for young people in WA) through Launchpad; later secured **$250,000** operational funding.
- **Plus Eight Accelerator** (Ophelie Cutier, COO, Spacecubed): **6-month** program; since launching **2016**: **6 cohorts**, **37 companies** accelerated, **$2m** invested via Plus Eight Partners, **$16m** follow-on investment, **$106m** total portfolio valuation.

## Ecosystem Initiatives

### MIT REAP (Regional Entrepreneurship Acceleration Program)

- Two-year program of the **MIT Sloan School of Management** (Boston): cohorts of regional/city reps run workshops/cases/analysis of their own ecosystems to find gaps/opportunities.
- WA is part of **Cohort 9**, alongside **Hungary, Brazil, the Dominican Republic**, and four US cities (**Kansas City, Omaha, Des Moines, St Louis**). WA won the right to host the **third (of four)** workshop series.
- **Team WA bid** supported by the WA Government, **funded by Rio Tinto and Woodside**; members include **DJTSI, City of Perth, Rio Tinto, Woodside, Spacecubed, West Tech Fest, CERI (Centre for Entrepreneurial Research and Innovation), Australian Venture Consultants, Malka Foundation, Curtin University**.
- Associated 2023 activity: launch of **Blak Angels** (First Nations angel-investing group); launch of the **City of Fremantle Innovation Strategy**; **MIT Perth Symposium**; visit to Rio Tinto's "mine of the future" at the **Gudai Darri** site near Newman. Tied to supporting resources-sector decarbonisation and economic diversification.

### Supercharge WA

- Led by **Nicole Lockwood** and **Professor Peter Klinken**; combines three **Meshpoints sub-constellations** to accelerate WA's energy transition. Delivered early findings to MIT REAP in **June 2023**; first key output of **WA+** (an informal association of Western Australians).
  - **Decarbonising Electricity** — "Gigazones" project: gigawatt-scale generation/load/storage with renewable electricity and heat, co-optimised with industrial loads in autonomous mini-grids.
  - **Critical Minerals** — common-use facility to optimise innovation and WA control across the critical-minerals value chain.
  - **Green Iron / Steel** — AI & innovation hub to make WA a globally significant green-iron producer (incl. owning the green-steel-industry AI model).
- (Meri Fatin associated with Supercharge WA communications.)

### Dealroom WA (Venture & Funding Data)

- Online data platform launched by **Hon. Minister Stephen Dawson, September 2022**; showcases WA startups, investors, funding deals, programs. **Funded by the New Industries Fund**, in partnership with local data gatherer **Techboard**. WA is the **4th Australian state** to launch Dealroom.
- **Dealroom** founded in **Amsterdam, 2013**; serves a global network of governments/ecosystems.
- Data shown: **665** verified startups; **263** funding rounds in WA in 2022; **11.9k+** WA employed by startups; VC investment over time. (wa.dealroom.co)

## New Industries Fund (NIF)

- WA State Government commitment: **$25.8m over four years**, plus a **$9.1m boost** in the 2023-24 Budget.
- Headline stats: **$25.8m** ecosystem support; **$5.3m+** booster grants; **276** ideas supported; **60+** award recipients.
- Programs/initiatives co-funded by the NIF:
  - **WA Innovator of the Year** — since **2006**; free to enter; finalists get free mentoring/workshops (IP, legal, marketing, boards, pitch training); runs May–December annually.
  - **Innovation Hubs** — co-funds **three** (Cyber, Data Science, Creative Tech); each acts as a **"connector," not a physical space**, running programs/conferences/summits.
  - **Innovation Booster Grant** — formerly the **Innovation Voucher Program**; competitive grant for WA startups/small businesses to access professional skills/services/knowledge.
  - **X-TEND WA Program** — competitive grants up to **$100,000** for entrepreneurial/investor education programs.
  - **WAVES (WA Venture Support) Pilot** — **$900,000** to subsidise operational costs of **three WA-owned early-stage VC funds**: **Purpose Ventures, Fund WA, Quokka Capital** (Purpose Ventures had already raised $38m).
  - **West Tech Fest** — sponsored at **$250,000/year** over three years; over 12 years grown to **~20 events** across Perth, Joondalup, Fremantle, Rottnest Island, plus a regional investor event and a **founder trip to Singapore (2022)**.

## The Innovative Society Initiative

- Launched at **Spacecubed's 10-year anniversary**; a **3-year, $5.9m** program supported by **Lotterywest**, to elevate the WA ecosystem's maturity.
- **First-year achievements:** funded **$1,100,000+** of initiatives; supported **87** early-stage innovations; attracted **90+ partners**; formed **13 sub-constellations**; developed the Index.
- Delivers its mission through **three pillars**:
  1. **Innovative Society Index** — measures ecosystem activity, impacts and outcomes over time.
  2. **Meshpoints** — collaborative network enabling community access, plus support to prioritise/endorse/progress projects via sub-constellations.
  3. **Innovative Society Fund** — funds initiatives/innovations across three streams (below).

### Sub-constellations

Collaborative partnerships around a shared mission, encouraging cross-sector collaboration and transparency. Active examples:

- **Innovation in Education** — connecting education-industry players.
- **Funders** — multi-stakeholder collaboration on funding methods/models.
- **Regional Hub Network** — connecting regional hubs to Perth's ecosystem (reducing isolation).
- **Fremantle Impact Hub** — impact-focused entrepreneurial hub.
- **Female Founders** — increasing female participation; addressing WA having the **country's highest gender gap**.
- **ESGI into Incubators** — embedding Ecological/Social/Governance/Impact into supported ventures.
- **SME Decarbonisation** — helping SMEs decarbonise, with system-level support.
- **Agile Business Modelling** — promoting agile business planning for founders.
- **Entrepreneurial Development Support** — cross-program collaboration between WA incubators/accelerators.

**Sub-constellation case studies:**

- **WA Regional Hub Network** (Alan Bradley, Strategic Lead, Pollinators): bridges regional areas and the Perth ecosystem from **Kununurra to Esperance**; monthly video links, news updates, semi-annual surveys, facilitated online workshops; driven by **Pollinators in Geraldton**, supported by **JTSI** and **Meshpoints**.
- **SME Decarbonisation – Canning Decarbonisation & Circular Economy Project** (Susan O'Byrne, Project Director, Business Foundations): net-zero/circularity transition for SMEs in the ageing (1960s-era) **Canning Vale/Welshpool** industrial areas; delivered with **Holonic** and **ClimateClever**, engaging **10 SMEs** across manufacturing, construction, transport/logistics and circular-economy sectors; supported by the Catalyst Fund (Lotterywest).

### Meshpoints Partners

- **90+ partners** (foundations, universities, government agencies, private enterprises).
