## What GoFundMe is

- Describes itself as the **#1 crowdfunding / social-fundraising platform** — the world's largest, on a mission to help people fundraise for **personal, business, and charitable** causes.
- **Donation-based** model (no rewards or equity): people give to causes they believe in. Common categories: personal cause, medical, emergency, business, animal, education, memorial/funeral, charity.
- **No fee to start** a fundraiser; funds are received via bank transfer by the organiser or named beneficiary; backed by a **Trust & Safety** team and the **GoFundMe Giving Guarantee**.

## Headline stats

- **Launched:** 2010 (site footer: © 2010–2026 GoFundMe).
- **Provided figures (your snapshot):** over **$9 billion** raised, from more than **120 million donations**; trusted by **100+ million people**.
- **Live-site activity figures (averaged 2023–2024 data):** more than **$50 million raised every week**; about **1 donation made every second**; **8,000+ fundraisers started daily**.

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Create your fundraiser** with guided prompts — add details, set a goal, update anytime.
2. **Reach donors by sharing** — share the link and use dashboard resources to build momentum.
3. **Securely receive funds** — add bank details (organiser or beneficiary) and start receiving donations.

## Key terms & references

- **GoFundMe:** donation-based crowdfunding platform for personal/business/charitable causes; "no fee to start."
- **Donation-based crowdfunding:** giving to a cause with no reward or equity in return (contrast with Kickstarter's reward-based "all or nothing," Indiegogo's flexible funding).
- **GoFundMe Giving Guarantee / Trust & Safety:** the platform's donor-protection assurances.
- **Snapshot stats:** launched 2010; $9B+ raised; 120M+ donations; 100M+ people; ~$50M/week; ~1 donation/second; 8K+ fundraisers/day (cumulative/averaged — verify).
