## Twenty Years in the Perth Startup Scene — Charlie Gunningham

Timeline narrative:

- **1999:** founded own tech startup. No coworking, no accelerators, no broadband, no social media, no smartphones. "Dotcoms," dial-up internet, ~30-second page loads, Y2K fears. Most dotcoms collapsed within months.
- **Mid-2000s:** internet-business survivors met ad hoc as **"eGroup"** (still meeting).
- **2012:** Spacecubed opened the first modern coworking space; first **Startup Weekend** within months.
- **~2013 (a year later):** first accelerator programs of their kind — **Curtin Accelerate, RAC SeedSpark, AMCOM (later VOCUS) Upstart** — invested in startups and provided mentoring.
- Around this time the **mining construction boom faded**: over next five years a **net 8,000 mining/oil & gas jobs** disappeared in Perth; **tech jobs** were among the largest growth areas (along with lawyers).
- **2019:** ecosystem bustling; mentors/programs/support plentiful; Perth positioned as a potential "regional tech city."

## 130+ Startup "Hubs" — Andrew Outhwaite (Director, We Are Rising; Former Chair, StartupWA)

Growth metrics:

- Ten years ago: only a handful of startup "hubs" (programs, networks, spaces, supporters).
- Now: **more than 130**.
- 2015–16 report showed **12 incubators + 12 coworking spaces**. Now: **26 programs, 16 recurring major annual events, 52 coworking spaces**.
- Numbers comparable to **Victoria** and **Queensland**, whose State governments historically invested an order of magnitude more in startup support.
- **2018–19 State + Federal government co-investment in WA hubs was 10× any previous year in the last decade.**

Strategic framing — **Startup Genome "Ecosystem Lifecycle" model**: next phase needs greater **activation, resource attraction, and globalisation** → more startups/scale-ups, more **$100m exits**, stronger sector specialisation, more globally connected entrepreneurs/hubs.

**Three recommendations:** (1) increase support for local startups and hubs; (2) be a great global ambassador; (3) support advocacy and data-collection efforts.

Citations referenced: Startup Ecosystem Report 2015-16 (StartupWA); 2018 Startup Ecosystem Mapping report (LaunchVic); Business Queensland (co-working spaces in QLD); Startup Genome Ecosystem Lifecycle Model.

## Startup Funding

### Overview — Rafael Kimberley-Bowen (Director, StartupWA)

VC landscape:

- **Stone Ridge Ventures** — multiple funds, exits like **Scanalyse**.
- **Yuuwa Capital** — **$40m fund**, ~a decade in, focused on managing existing investments (e.g., **iCetana**).
- Newer boutique funds: **808 Ventures** (Perth-based, not exclusively WA-focused); **Lateral Capital Ventures** (portfolio incl. **NCX, Spectur**); **RAC's Better Labs** corporate venture fund.
- Boutique funds tend to target **seed stage** → **funding gap at later stages**, filled largely by the **ASX**. The stock market is a disproportionately high source of funds for WA startups vs other states. **Fastbrick Robotics, Brainchip, AusCann** raised **>$100m combined** over the last couple of years.

Angel & other early-stage funding:

- WA now has **two angel groups** — **Southwest Angels** joining **Perth Angels** (three if you include **Innovation Bay**).
- **Perth Angels invested more in the past year than Melbourne Angels or Sydney Angels** (younger group, smaller membership, less sophisticated environment).
- **No exits by angel groups in WA yet**; larger angel cheques often written outside formal groups and are less visible.
- **Family offices** active in early-stage: **Larsen Ventures** (HealthEngine, Humm), the **Vukelic Group**, the **JJ Leach Group**.
- Seed accelerators multiplying, esp. **Plus Eight**. **Founder Institute** and **Vocus Upstart** not currently active.
- **Crowd-sourced funding** surge: **West Winds Gin** (~$1m, first off the mark), **Credi** ($250k), plus concurrent campaigns by **Rhinohide, Urbotanica, Tiller Rides**.
- Biggest funder of early-stage ventures likely remains the **R&D tax incentive**. Federal funding via **Accelerating Commercialisation grants** and **EMDG**.
- **Medical Research Commercialisation Fund** (managed by **Brandon Capital**) supported WA life-science companies, notably **OncoRes Medical**.
- Over four years, **Accelerating Commercialisation** provided **$12m to 23 WA businesses** with pre-revenue projects.
- **WA receives only ~1% of the national total of state startup funding.**

### Investor commentary — Derek Gerrard (Purpose Ventures)

New entrants supplementing Perth Angels, Larsen Ventures and Spacecubed (Plus Eight): **BetterLabs Venture Fund (RAC-backed)**, **808 Ventures**, and the newly announced **$15m Fogarty Foundation fund**. "No shortage of capital in Perth" — ongoing need to **educate investors**. Investment decision still revolves around the **founder relationship (character, competency, chemistry)**, plus belief in **problem, solution and market fit**, demonstrated via **genuine traction**.

## WestTechFest — Paula Taylor (Co-Founder)

- Initiated by **Curtin University** with co-founders **Bill Tai, Larry Lopez, Paula Taylor, Rohan McDougall** ~a decade ago.
- Evolution: **WAPP competition → OzAPP Awards → week-long annual festival** fostering dialogue between entrepreneurs, innovators, researchers, investors and industry.

- Leverages WA strengths (coastline, world-class kite-surfing) to attract high-profile VCs/entrepreneurs from **Silicon Valley, Israel**, Australia's East Coast and South-East Asia.
- Regional spin-off tours (**Abrolhos Islands → Margaret River**); hosted **WA's first major blockchain summit**.
- 2019 expansion: **social impact technology** aligned to the **UN Sustainable Development Goals**.

## Young Entrepreneurs in WA — Jasmin Ward (Bloom)

- **Launchpad** = a **12-week university-accredited entrepreneurship program** run out of Bloom; **tripled in size this year** due to demand.
- Entrepreneurial mindset is **degree-agnostic**: applicants now drawn equally from **Science, Engineering, Commerce, Law, Arts** (previously mainly Commerce/Engineering). Science students often from deep majors (genetics, neuroscience, biotechnology).
- Current cohort: **48% female / 51% male** (per source).

- Trend toward **purpose-driven / social-enterprise models** (revenue-generating) over traditional not-for-profit.

## Indigenous Entrepreneurship — facts + Leslie Delaforce (Director, StartupWA)

Key facts:

- Of **660 Indigenous students** who finished school last year, **10 enrolled** in software engineering or other tech-related subjects.
- **44% of jobs** projected to be automated in the next 10 years.
- Tech/STEM has potential as an **equalising effect** on opportunity for Aboriginal people.
- **60% of the Aboriginal population is under 35 and digitally connected.**
- **Most Indigenous startups have a social focus.**
- Aspiration: Aboriginal people transition **from consumer to creator** of technology.
- **Raise the Bar initiative** (Business Council of Australia): **20 of its largest members** to spend **>$3 billion with Indigenous suppliers over 5 years**.

Ecosystem narrative:
