## What Kickstarter is

- A crowdfunding platform whose stated mission is to help bring **creative projects to life** — a home for film, music, art, theatre, games, comics, design, photography and more.
- Uses a reward-based, **"all or nothing"** funding model (from the Session 6 lecture and the LaunchBoom video): a project must hit its funding goal by the deadline or no money changes hands.

## Headline stats (snapshot — provided figures)

- **Launched:** 28 April 2009.
- **Backers:** ~**22 million** people have backed a project.
- **Total pledged:** **$7,214,383,854** pledged to projects.
- **Successfully funded projects:** **236,880**.
- (These are cumulative since launch and grow continuously — re-check before quoting.)

## Company principles / structure

- Kickstarter became a **Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)** — a legal commitment to uphold its principles (its stated mission and values) alongside its profits, rather than prioritising profit alone.
- It launched **The Creative Independent** — a resource publishing advice and resources for all kinds of makers/creators.

## Key terms & references

- **Kickstarter:** reward-based crowdfunding platform for creative projects; "all or nothing" funding.
- **Backers:** people who pledge money to projects (often for rewards/early access).
- **Public Benefit Corporation (PBC):** a for-profit structure legally committed to a stated public benefit / mission, not just shareholder profit.
- **The Creative Independent:** Kickstarter's resource hub of advice for makers.
- **Snapshot stats:** launched 28 Apr 2009; ~22M backers; ~$7.21B pledged; 236,880 projects funded (cumulative, rising).
