### **1. Task Delegation: "Grunt Work" vs. Decision Making**

Students differentiate between boring, repetitive tasks and those requiring human judgment.

- **The "Grunt Work" Rule**: "I'm trying to give it as much grunt work as I can... If I have to do it for the class, I'm going to do it [myself]. But like... if you find a really long research article, throw it in, get a summary, then read it so you find the good nuggets."
- **Knowledge vs. Decision**: "To me, it's like the difference between knowledge and decision. I keep the decisions to myself, but if I need like knowledge about something or information, I would ask [AI] for it."
- **The Line for Critical Thinking**: "I don't want to get into a practice of delegating little tasks that require even minimal amounts of critical thinking... if I give it to ChatGPT, it's learning and I'm not."

### **2. AI as a Tutor: "Teaching vs. Solving"**

Students use AI to accelerate their learning process rather than just to get the final answer.

- **The Tutoring Framework**: "Using AI to teach you versus to solve for you... even if there's a problem that I didn't know how to do... I'll say 'how do you solve this problem, [give me] more problems like this'."
- **Immediate Feedback**: "You have an available tutor at all times... this is just immediate... you can get quiz questions, all of this material just on the fly. I think you can learn much faster through the use of AI."
- **Critical Thinking Requirement**: "There needs to be a critical thinking aspect to it... we should be using it as like a tool instead of like something that fully replaces our thinking."

### **3. Writing and Professional Polishing**

AI is used to refine human-generated ideas rather than write from scratch.

- **Drafting and Polishing**: "I would definitely keep the structure and inspiration for myself... but like rephrasing, polishing, and also elevating the style—these types of things, it’s definitely something that I won’t put that much time in."
- **Learning from AI Output**: "I realized that now I talk more professionally... it really phrased my goals well or phrased my purposes better... it learns my habits and I learn its habit and I improve myself based on what I learn from chat."

### **4. Impact on Majors and Future Skills**

The presence of AI is changing how students view their career paths and necessary skills.

- **Major Shifts**: One student switched from Computer Science (CS) to Electrical Engineering (EE) because: "Seeing CS being so good at coding... I start having like an existential crisis seeing that like doing a better job than I do."
- **The Rise of Soft Skills**: "In a world where anyone can be technical... what sets people apart is like the people's skills... being able to express yourself clearly... thinking on your feet."
- **Focus on AI Application**: "You don't need to know how to build a large language model yourself if you want to take advantage of all the benefits."

### **5. Institutional Changes: "AI-Proof" Education**

Stanford professors are beginning to integrate AI into the curriculum.

- **AI-Proof Initiatives**: "A lot of our assignments are now structured around how are we interacting with AI... you have to actually talk it out with your TA [to explain] what were the gaps in the research or the sources that AI gave you."
- **Mandatory AI Use**: "Some professors... actually require you to use ChatGPT... for specific tasks... because we're going to graduate to the world where we're going to have AI."

### **6. Emotional Perspective: Excited and Scared**

The general sentiment among students is a binary of excitement and fear.

- **The Duality**: "I feel both excited and scared... excited in the sense that I could design a flyer instantly... but then sometimes I also feel very scared because... if AI is able to do all these things... what am I going to do in the future?"
- **The Uniqueness of AI**: "AI specifically is different because it feels like it has like wisdom, it has like something that we thought that only humans have."
- **The Goal**: "This is more efficiency so therefore we should be able to push that end goal further and do so much more and tackle those big problems."
