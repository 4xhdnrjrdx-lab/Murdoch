# BUS353: Assignment 3 — Video Presentation Marking Guide (Content Dump)

Source: "BUS353: Video Presentation (30%)" marking guide.

The document is a **marking rubric (grading matrix)** for a **Video Presentation**, worth **30%**, marked out of **30 marks total**. The underlying topic of the assessed presentation is **scaling one's business** and linking to **BUS353 unit topics**.

Five assessed **Elements** (with mark allocations) are graded across five grade bands:

- **Fail** (below 50%)
- **Pass** (50–59%)
- **Credit** (60–69%)
- **Distinction** (70–79%)
- **High Distinction** (80–100%)

Each element is transcribed verbatim below with the descriptor for every grade band.

---

## Element 1 — Presentation Style [7 marks — marked individually]

- **Fail (below 50%):** The presenter does not look professional. Delivery is hesitant and choppy and sounds like the presenter is reading. Spoken work is muddled and unclear. Poor grammar is used throughout.
- **Pass (50–59%):** The presenter does not look professional. Presentation appears unrehearsed, with uneven delivery. Occasional incorrect grammar is used throughout.
- **Credit (60–69%):** The presenter looks professional. Some sections appear rehearsed; some sections are uneven in delivery, with some distracting rhythm during the presentation.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** The presenter looks professional. Rehearsed smooth delivery. Expression and pace are good throughout the podcast. Correct grammar is used.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** The presenter looks professional. Well-rehearsed, smooth delivery in a conversational style. The presentation is well paced. Expression and rhythm keep the audience listening. Correct grammar is used.

---

## Element 2 — Quality [6 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** Student was not able to demonstrate that they had an understanding on scaling one's business. No use of the right terminology and concepts.
- **Pass (50–59%):** Student was not clearly able to demonstrate that they had an understanding on scaling one's business. Very limited use of terminology and concepts.
- **Credit (60–69%):** Student was able to demonstrate that they had some understanding on scaling one's business by correctly using some terminology and concepts.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** Student was able to demonstrate that they had a good understanding on scaling one's business by correctly using right terminology and concepts.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** Student was able to demonstrate that they had a well-developed understanding on scaling one's business by correctly using terminology and concepts.

---

## Element 3 — Business Model [6 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** The proposal to scale the business is not clear or usable.
- **Pass (50–59%):** The proposal to scale the business is not fully clear and only partially usable.
- **Credit (60–69%):** The proposal to scale the business is clear and usable.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** The proposal to scale the business is very well planned and developed to a very good standard.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** The proposal to scale the business is very well planned and developed to an excellent standard.

---

## Element 4 — Creativity [6 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** The overall presentation quality is very poor. There is no expression of creativity or innovative thinking.
- **Pass (50–59%):** The overall presentation quality is average. Expression of creativity and innovative thinking is poor.
- **Credit (60–69%):** The overall presentation quality is good. Expression of creativity and innovative thinking is good.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** The overall quality of the presentation is very good. Expression of creativity and innovative thinking is very good.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** The overall quality of the presentation is excellent. Expression of creativity and innovative thinking is excellent.

---

## Element 5 — Unit Learning [5 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** No learning from BUS353 unit (i.e., linking to the topics covered) have been used in the presentation.
- **Pass (50–59%):** Learnings from BUS353 unit (i.e., linking to the topics covered) have been used in the presentation to a minimum degree.
- **Credit (60–69%):** Learnings from BUS353 unit (i.e., linking to the topics covered) have been used in the presentation well.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** Learnings from BUS353 unit (i.e., linking to the topics covered) have been well integrated in the presentation.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** Learnings from BUS353 unit (i.e., linking to the topics covered) have been integrated in the presentation to an excellent degree.

## Quick takeaways for the assignment

- The single most heavily weighted element is **Presentation Style (7 marks)** — and it is **marked individually** (i.e., assessed per presenter, not as a group). Top marks require a well-rehearsed, smooth, conversational delivery, good pace, and correct grammar.
- The assessment is fundamentally about **scaling a business** — both **Quality (6)** and **Business Model (6)** reward correct **scaling terminology/concepts** and a clear, well-developed proposal to scale.
- **Creativity (6)** rewards innovative thinking and overall presentation quality.
- **Unit Learning (5)** rewards explicitly **linking the presentation to BUS353 topics** — integrate unit concepts, don't just mention them.
- The presentation is referred to in places as a "podcast" / conversational style — aim for a natural, audience-holding delivery rather than reading from a script.
