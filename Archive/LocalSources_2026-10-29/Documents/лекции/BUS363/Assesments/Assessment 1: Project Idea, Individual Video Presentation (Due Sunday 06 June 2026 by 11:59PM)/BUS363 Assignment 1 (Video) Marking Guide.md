# BUS353: Video Presentation — Marking Guide (20%)

Marking rubric across five bands: Fail (below 50%) · Pass (50–59%) · Credit (60–69%) · Distinction (70–79%) · High Distinction (80–100%).

**Total: 20 Marks**

---

## 1. Introduction [2 marks]

| Band                           | Descriptor                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Fail (<50%)**                | The student ID card is not presented at the beginning of the video and there is no introduction. |
| **Pass (50–59%)**              | The student ID card is presented at the beginning of the video, but the introduction is poor.    |
| **Credit (60–69%)**            | The student ID card is presented at the beginning of the video.                                  |
| **Distinction (70–79%)**       | The student ID card is presented at the beginning of the video.                                  |
| **High Distinction (80–100%)** | The student ID card is presented at the beginning of the video.                                  |

---

## 2. Presentation Style [5 marks]

| Band                           | Descriptor                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fail (<50%)**                | The presenter does not look professional. Delivery is hesitant and choppy and sounds like the presenter is reading. Spoken work is muddled and unclear. Poor grammar is used throughout.                 |
| **Pass (50–59%)**              | The presenter does not look professional. Presentation appears unrehearsed, with uneven delivery. Occasional incorrect grammar is used throughout.                                                       |
| **Credit (60–69%)**            | The presenter looks professional. Some sections appear rehearsed; some sections are uneven in delivery, with some distracting rhythm during the presentation.                                            |
| **Distinction (70–79%)**       | The presenter looks professional. Rehearsed smooth delivery. Expression and pace are good throughout the podcast. Correct grammar is used.                                                               |
| **High Distinction (80–100%)** | The presenter looks professional. Well-rehearsed, smooth delivery in a conversational style. The presentation is well paced. Expression and rhythm keep the audience listening. Correct grammar is used. |

---

## 3. Optimization Process & Usability [8 marks]

| Band                           | Descriptor                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fail (<50%)**                | Explanation of the optimization process is not clear. Due to poor quality, the suggestions cannot be used to optimize the chosen activity/process.                                       |
| **Pass (50–59%)**              | Explanation of the optimization process is limited. Some suggestions can be used to optimize the chosen activity/process, but are significantly limited.                                 |
| **Credit (60–69%)**            | Some aspects of the optimization process are explained. With some limitations, suggestions generally can be used to optimize the chosen activity/process.                                |
| **Distinction (70–79%)**       | Good explanation of optimization process for the chosen topic. Suggestions can be fully used to optimize the chosen activity/process and are completed to a very good standard.          |
| **High Distinction (80–100%)** | An excellent explanation of optimization process for the chosen topic. Suggestions can be fully used to optimize the chosen activity/process and are completed to an excellent standard. |

---

## 4. Video Quality & Creativity [5 marks]

| Band                           | Descriptor                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fail (<50%)**                | The overall video quality is very poor. There is no expression of creativity in the video.         |
| **Pass (50–59%)**              | The overall video quality is average. Expression of creativity in the video is poor.               |
| **Credit (60–69%)**            | The overall quality of the video is good. Expression of creativity in the video is good.           |
| **Distinction (70–79%)**       | The overall quality of the video is very good. Expression of creativity in the video is very good. |
| **High Distinction (80–100%)** | Expression of creativity in the video is excellent.                                                |
