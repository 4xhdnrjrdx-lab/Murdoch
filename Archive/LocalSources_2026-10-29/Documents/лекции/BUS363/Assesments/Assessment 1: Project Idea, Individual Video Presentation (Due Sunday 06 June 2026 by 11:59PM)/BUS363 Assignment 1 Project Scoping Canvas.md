# Info in lms (not file)

Please use this 'Project Scoping Canvas' as your 1-page template start scope-out your project and clearly define what’s in & out of scope, risks, stakeholders, assumptions, constraints etc., as you decide on a specific solution or problem to solve.

You can use this 'scoping canvas' to define your project requirements, deliverables and measures of success criteria (i.e., how will you know you've successfully delivered your project?); and HOW could you optimise your project? what are some strategies you can use to turn a simple project idea into a global (project) venture?

Please be sure to submit a copy of your completed Project Scoping Canva with your video.
