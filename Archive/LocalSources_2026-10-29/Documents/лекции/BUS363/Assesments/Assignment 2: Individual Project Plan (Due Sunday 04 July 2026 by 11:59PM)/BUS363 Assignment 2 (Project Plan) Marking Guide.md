The document is a **marking rubric (grading matrix)**. The deliverable being assessed is a **Project Plan Presentation**, worth **35%**, marked out of **35 marks total**.

Five assessed **Elements** (with their mark allocations) are graded across five grade bands:

- **Fail** (below 50%)
- **Pass** (50–59%)
- **Credit** (60–69%)
- **Distinction** (70–79%)
- **High Distinction** (80–100%)

Below, each element is transcribed verbatim with the descriptor for every grade band.

---

## Element 1 — Structure [5 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** Does not contain clear project components.
- **Pass (50–59%):** Contains most of the required components for the project.
- **Credit (60–69%):** Contains all the required components to deliver a project.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** Contains all the required components to deliver a project and they are explained to a very good level.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** Contains all the required components to deliver a project and they are explained to an excellent level.

---

## Element 2 — Project Management Concepts [10 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** Student was not able to demonstrate that they had an understanding of the Project Planning. No use of the right terminology and concepts.
- **Pass (50–59%):** Student was not clearly able to demonstrate that they had an understanding of the Project Planning. Very limited use of terminology and concepts.
- **Credit (60–69%):** Student was able to demonstrate that they had some understanding of the Project Planning by correctly using some terminology and concepts.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** Student was able to demonstrate that they had a good understanding of the Project Planning by correctly using right terminology and concepts.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** Student was able to demonstrate that they had a well-developed understanding of the Project Planning by correctly using terminology and concepts.

---

## Element 3 — Usability [7 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** Due to poor quality, the Project Plan cannot be used to make any informed decisions.
- **Pass (50–59%):** The Project Plan can be used to make business decisions to some extent; however, it lacks some important information.
- **Credit (60–69%):** The Project Plan be used to make business decisions and covers core information. The plan shows medium to good project management skills.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** The Project Plan can be used to make strong / informed business decisions and covers more than only the core information. The plan shows very good project management skills.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** The Project Plan can be used to make strong / informed business decisions and covers more than only the core information. The plan shows excellent project management skills.

---

## Element 4 — Organisation & Quality [5 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** The slides are high level and not informative. The presentation lacks details. The story telling is poor.
- **Pass (50–59%):** The slides are high level and lack detailed explanations and justifications. The story telling is average.
- **Credit (60–69%):** The slides are good, have sufficient level of detail, explanations and justifications. The story telling is good.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** The slides are very good and describe the project and its steps really well. The story telling is very good.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** The slides are excellent and describe the project, its steps really well. All the project details are well thought through. The storytelling is excellent.

---

## Element 5 — Presentation and English expression [8 marks]

- **Fail (below 50%):** Errors in the plan distract the reader to the extent that meaning is unclear. Poor grammar is used throughout. Formatting/presentation of the work does not look professional.
- **Pass (50–59%):** Errors in the plan are distracting, but the meaning is still clear. Occasional incorrect grammar is used. Formatting/presentation of the work is basic.
- **Credit (60–69%):** Some errors in the plan are present, but overall, the meaning is clear. Correct grammar is used. Formatting/presentation of the work is good.
- **Distinction (70–79%):** There are no errors in the plan that distract the reader. The meaning is clear. Formatting/presentation of the work is very good.
- **High Distinction (80–100%):** The plan is free from grammatical and punctuation errors. Formatting/presentation of the work is excellent.

## Quick takeaways for the assignment

- The single most heavily weighted element is **Project Management Concepts (10 marks)** — top marks require correct, well-developed use of project-planning **terminology and concepts**.
- **Presentation and English expression (8 marks)** is the second-largest — grammar, punctuation and professional formatting matter a lot.
- **Usability (7 marks)** rewards a plan detailed enough to drive **strong/informed business decisions**, i.e. going beyond just core information.
- **Structure (5 marks)** rewards including **all required project components** AND explaining them well.
- **Organisation & Quality (5 marks)** is largely about slide detail, justifications and **storytelling**.
