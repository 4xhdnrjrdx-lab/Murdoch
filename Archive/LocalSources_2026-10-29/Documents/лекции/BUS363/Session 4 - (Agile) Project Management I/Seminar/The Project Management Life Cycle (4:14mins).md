## Part 1 — Delivery Process vs PM Process

Jennifer Whitt (Director, ProjectManager.com) addresses a common confusion: the difference between the **delivery process** and the **project management process**. They happen simultaneously and go "hand in hand," but involve different activities and responsibilities.

- A whiteboard cross-section shows activity levels:
  - **Delivery phase:** activity starts, builds in the middle, then tapers toward the finish.
  - **Project management phase:** overlays delivery with process groups whose activity levels vary throughout.

## Part 2 — The Delivery Process (team responsibilities)

The **delivery process** is where **team members** are mainly engaged — the "doing" parts. Stages (the "5 Ds"):

- **Definition:** set what the product/service will be.
- **Design:** create the blueprints/specifications.
- **Development:** the actual building/creation phase.
- **Deployment:** releasing the project output.
- **Departure:** team members rolling off as the project concludes.

## Part 3 — PM Life Cycle: Initiating and Planning

Defined from the **PMBOK** (produced by **PMI**):
**1. Initiating** — at the very start; main deliverable = **Project Charter**; key goal = formal approval / sign-off to proceed.
**2. Planning** — begins alongside initiating and continues throughout; main deliverable = **Project Plan**; activities = defining processes, the **schedule**, **milestones**, and all main deliverables.

## Part 4 — Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, and Closing

**3. Executing, Monitoring & Controlling:**

- **Performance reporting:** "where are we now and where should we be."
- **Control:** identify and implement actions to get the project back on track if it deviates.
- **Log management:** issue logs, risk logs, change logs.
- **Deliverable oversight:** monitor the deliverables the team produces.

**4. Closing** (aligns with "Departure"):

- **User acceptance:** formal sign-off from the end user.
- **Project approval:** final sign-off.
- **Final reports:** distributed to team members, stakeholders, and the **Change Control Board (CCB)**.
- **Project documents:** archive all relevant paperwork.
- **Lessons learned:** document what went well/badly for future projects.

## Key terms & references

- **PMI / PMBOK:** the body and standard defining the life-cycle phases.
- **Delivery process (5 Ds):** Definition, Design, Development, Deployment, Departure (team-led).
- **PM life cycle (PMBOK process groups):** Initiating → Planning → Executing → Monitoring & Controlling → Closing.
- **Key deliverables:** Project Charter (initiating), Project Plan + schedule + milestones (planning), issue/risk/change logs (M&C), user acceptance + final reports + archived docs + lessons learned (closing).
- **Source:** ProjectManager (Jennifer Whitt).
