## Part 1 — Defining the Scope and Documentation

- **"Baseline"** is described as the most powerful word in project management — it can define the difference between a successful and a failed project.
- **Step 1: define the scope**, done through documentation — a **Scope Statement**, which includes:
  - A description of the scope and what's being produced as a result of the project.
  - The **major deliverables** produced during and after the project.
  - **Assumptions and constraints** that might impact the scope.
- The process also uses a **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)** — taking high-level work and breaking it into finer detail. The detailed units are called **work packages**, essential for building the schedule and estimating costs.
- A **WBS Dictionary** provides a more detailed description of each work package.

## Part 2 — The Approval Process and Establishing the Baseline

- **Step 2: approve the scope**, handled by **stakeholders** (number depends on project size).
- **Change Control Board (CCB):** one or more stakeholders who manage the approval process.
- **Change control processes:** the CCB follows the documented change-control procedures specific to the project/organisation.
- **Review and agreement:** the board reviews the scope to ensure it's agreed and meets all objectives; any necessary changes are made here.
- Once stakeholders approve the documented scope, it officially becomes the **baseline** — then used to **monitor and track** progress along the way.
- The video suggests using PM software (ProjectManager) to help baseline scope.

## Key terms & references

- **Baseline:** the approved version of the project scope, used to monitor and track progress.
- **Scope Statement:** document describing scope, results, major deliverables, assumptions and constraints.
- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** hierarchical decomposition of the total scope of work.
- **Work packages:** the lowest level of WBS detail — used for scheduling and cost estimation.
- **WBS Dictionary:** detailed description of each WBS component.
- **Stakeholders:** those responsible for approving the scope.
- **Change Control Board (CCB):** formal group that reviews/approves/rejects changes.
- **Change control processes:** documented procedures for managing scope changes.
