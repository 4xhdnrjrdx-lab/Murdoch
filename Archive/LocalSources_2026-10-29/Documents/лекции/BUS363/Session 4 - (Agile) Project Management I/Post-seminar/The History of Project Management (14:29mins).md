Clayton divides PM history into **six ages**.

## Part 1 — Pre-history of project management

Massive ancient constructions that still stand.

- **4004 BCE:** Bishop Usher's calculated date of creation — Clayton's joke that this was the first project delivered ahead of schedule (God finished a day early, rested on the seventh).
- **2570 BCE:** completion of the **Great Pyramid at Giza** — its leaders must have used sophisticated planning, scheduling and resourcing.
- Ancient project leaders operated in highly political environments under intense pressure to deliver with allocated resources (though exact budgets/schedules are unknown).

## Part 2 — The Early Age (turn of 19th–20th century)

Birth of visual scheduling tools.

- **1896:** **Karol Adamiecki** developed the **Harmonogram** (a precursor to the Gantt chart; not published immediately).
- **1910–1915:** **Henry Gantt** published the **Gantt chart**, instantly associated with him.

## Part 3 — The Golden Age (post-WWII to 1975)

Formal processes standardised; major bodies founded.

- **1957:** **Critical Path Method (CPM)** — developed by **DuPont**.
- **1958:** **PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique)** — developed by the **US Navy** for the **Polaris program**.
- **1962:** **US DoD** mandated **Work Breakdown Structures (WBS)** in all its projects.
- **1965:** **IPMA** founded — originally IMSA, renamed **INTERNET** the same year, finally IPMA in 1996.
- **1969:** **Project Management Institute (PMI)** founded.
- **1970:** **Winston Royce** described the **Waterfall** process (a name for basic predictive PM).
- **1972:** **Association for Project Management (APM)** founded in the UK.
- **1975:** **PROMPT2** created (Project Resource Organization Management Planning Technique).

## Part 4 — The Silver Age (late 1970s–1980s)

Innovations born outside PM (production/manufacturing) get absorbed into the field.

- **1978:** **Toyota Production System (TPS)** — gives rise to **Lean** and the **Kanban** method.
- **1984:** **Eliyahu Goldratt** publishes **_The Goal_** — the **Theory of Constraints**.
- **1986:** **Takeuchi & Nonaka** publish the HBR article "**The New New Product Development Game**," using the rugby **Scrum** metaphor for effective product development.
- **1987:** **PMI** publishes the **Project Management Body of Knowledge** (progenitor of the later PMBOK Guide).
- **1989:** UK government's **CCTA** adapts PROMPT into **PRINCE** (PRompt In the CCTA Environment), later redefined as **PRojects IN Controlled Environments**.

## Part 5 — The Rebirth (1990s)

Agile emerges alongside major predictive-standard revisions.

- **1991:** **Alistair Cockburn** develops **Crystal Clear** (first Agile-like methodology).
- **1995:** **Jeff Sutherland & Ken Schwaber** develop **Scrum** for software.
- **1996:** **Kent Beck** (and others) create **Extreme Programming (XP)**.
- **1996:** PMI publishes the **first edition of the PMBOK Guide**.
- **1996:** **PRINCE2** published (major revision of PRINCE).
- **1997:** Goldratt publishes **_The Critical Chain_** → **Critical Chain Project Management (CCPM)**.
- **1997:** **IAPM** (International Association of Project Managers) founded.

## Part 6 — The Modern Age (2001–present)

Agile formalised; rise of hybrids.

- **2001:** **Agile Manifesto** and the **12 Principles** published.
- **2007:** **David Anderson** applies **Kanban** to software development.
- **2009:** major **PRINCE2** revision.
- **2012:** **Scott Ambler** develops **Disciplined Agile Delivery (DAD)** (Agile at enterprise level; precursor to hybrids).
- **Hybrids:** **FLEX** (Hilio Costa) and **P3.express** (Nader Rad & Frank Turley), both Creative Commons.
- **2017:** **APM** receives its **Royal Charter**.
- **2021:** PMI issues the **7th edition of the PMBOK Guide** — a "revolutionary" rather than evolutionary shift.

## Key terms & references

- **Tools:** Harmonogram (Adamiecki); Gantt chart (Gantt); CPM (DuPont); PERT (US Navy/Polaris); WBS (US DoD).
- **Bodies:** IPMA (1965); PMI (1969); APM (1972, Royal Charter 2017); IAPM (1997).
- **Predictive:** Waterfall (Royce, 1970); PROMPT2 → PRINCE (1989) → PRINCE2 (1996, rev. 2009).
- **Lean/agile lineage:** TPS → Lean & Kanban (1978); Theory of Constraints / _The Goal_ (1984) & _Critical Chain_ / CCPM (1997); Scrum metaphor (1986) → Scrum for software (1995); XP (1996); Agile Manifesto + 12 principles (2001); Kanban for software (2007); DAD (2012); hybrids FLEX, P3.express.
- **Standards:** PMBOK (1987 doc; 1st-edition Guide 1996; 7th edition 2021).
- **Presenter:** Mike Clayton, Online PM Courses.
