## Part 1 — Defining Project Scope

- **Project scope** is described as both the _easiest_ thing to define in project management and the _hardest_ thing to agree on. Negotiating scope is at the heart of good PM and the toughest challenge a PM faces.
- **The circle diagram:**
  - **Inside the circle** = everything you have to do / deliver — what is "**in scope**."
  - **Outside the circle** = everything that is **out of scope**, a.k.a. **project exclusions** / **exclusions from scope**.
  - Items sit outside the circle because they're not economically viable, not needed by the organisation, or will be delivered by other projects at other times.

## Part 2 — The Challenge of Scope Creep

- The core scope-management problem: stakeholders try to "feed additional functionality" into the project.
- **Scope creep:** extending the scope boundary **without** a matching increase in resources, budget, or schedule — getting more delivered without providing the means to deliver it.

## Part 3 — The Difficulty of Negotiation

- Negotiating scope is a **multi-way negotiation** against competing pressures.
- **Stakeholder dynamics:** different stakeholders have different preferences, needs, wants, desires, agendas and interests.
- **The PM's task:** satisfy multiple people simultaneously while working within strict resource limits — you can't please everybody.
- **Balancing act:** find the best possible balance to smooth over concerns and get **sign-off**.
- **Final authority:** sign-off must come from stakeholders with the ultimate authority to decide final scope, final budget, and delivery timeline.

## Part 4 — Conclusion

- The _concept_ of scope is easy; the _practice_ is "tremendously difficult." Negotiating it, documenting it, making it stick, and winning over stakeholders at the same time is the hardest challenge PMs face.

## Key terms & references

- **Project scope:** everything a PM must do or deliver.
- **In scope / out of scope:** inside vs outside the boundary (circle).
- **Project exclusions:** items explicitly defined as _not_ part of delivery.
- **Scope creep:** added functionality/requirements without added budget, time, or resources.
- **Stakeholders:** people with varying interests/needs who influence scope.
- **Sign-off:** formal approval of final scope, budget and schedule by those with authority.
- **The circle diagram:** visual tool separating in-boundary from out-of-boundary work.
