## What this actually is / how to do it

This is the **pre-workshop activity that was set in Session 3 and presented/discussed in Session 4**. The Session 4 lecture already works through the answer in detail, so the response can be built straight from that material. The lecture groups contemporary PM challenges into **five categories** (and gives a ~20-item brainstorm list). Pick at least 3, each with an example:

| Challenge (from lecture)                                                                                                | One-line example to use                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Change** (Waterfall → Agile/Scrum; "time is money", must stay flexible)                                               | A team locked into a rigid Waterfall plan can't respond when the client's requirements shift mid-project, so the product ships outdated. |
| **Risk management** (it's not the risk itself but _poor_ risk management / failing to anticipate that sinks a business) | A project ignores a single-supplier dependency; the supplier fails and the whole schedule collapses because no contingency existed.      |
| **Emerging technologies** (constant change; e.g. Blockchain/Bitcoin, AI)                                                | A PM must integrate AI tooling mid-delivery; the team lacks the skills, causing delays and rework.                                       |
| **Timescales vs Resources** (+ hubristic leadership — Hogan, Owen & Davidson)                                           | An over-confident leader commits to an unrealistic deadline with too few people; quality and morale suffer.                              |
| **Industrial landscape / International markets** (cultural differences, governance, regulation, e.g. GDPR)              | Scaling a product into the EU triggers GDPR compliance work that wasn't scoped, blowing the budget.                                      |

Other brainstorm items from the lecture slide (in case you want different ones): limited budget; poor communication; environmental/sustainability pressure on funding; remote-work / changing employee needs; motivation & reward systems; diversity & inclusion of ideas; VUCA / adapting to rapid change; "data vortex" (loss of online records); climate/disaster risk (flood/fire); pandemics (COVID-19); labour shortage; supply-chain cost & quality; cybersecurity / poor risk management; expected outcome not matching customer needs.

→ Full detail on all of these is in `BUS363_Session_4__Project_Management_1.md` (sections "Contemporary Challenges in Project Management", slides 50–54).
