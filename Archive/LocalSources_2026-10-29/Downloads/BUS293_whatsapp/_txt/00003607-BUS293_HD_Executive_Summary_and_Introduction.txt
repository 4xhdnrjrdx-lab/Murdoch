BUS293 Team Dynamics Report - HD Executive Summary and Introduction
1. Executive Summary
As one of Australia’s major lenders and an ASX200 stock,Westpac Banking Corporation(WBC) is an expansive, multi-business entity which operates by means of dynamic teams. In modern and complex organizations, teams have to perform effectively in areas of: communication, trust, cooperation and overall team efficacy. Moreover, as an economy that continually moves towards further digitisation and a hybridized working landscape, effective inter-organizational communication becomes indispensable for overall success and long term sustainability. This report is designed to analyze four primary team dynamics issues that impact Westpac Banking Corporation; communication issues between teams; team trust; remote working. 
Also, collaboration and team cohesiveness across department. Communication issues are regarded as significant barriers among organizations that deal with large numbers of departments that also operate with conflicting interests. Disparities between departments within, Technology, Risk, Compliance, Operations, and Customer Service for instance, create barriers to clear communication. Such barriers results to the creation of misconceptions, the slowing of decision making processes, and an interruption of overall information distribution.
The report further considers the role of trust in promoting team work. Trust is enabling workers to share knowledge, work constructively and participate in collaborative processes. Especially within hybrid and remote teams, trust is a vital commodity as there is a greater need for trust as a substitute for direct employee contact and trust to build informal relationship. The paper reveals how trust contributes to psychological safety, engagement of employees and organisational resilience.
Lastly, the paper addresses the effect of the remote and the hybrid work arrangement on employee well-being, quality of communication and employee integration in teams. In addition to the benefit of a work-life balance, employees are faced with new challenge related to connection to the organisation and with other employees. Last of all, the paper evaluates teamwork and team integration across various divisions of WBC, in that regard the communication technology implementation, Integrated Systems and cross-functional teams that were set up to foster good team collaboration, failed because organisation are too complex.
The research based upon organisational behaviour principles and academic literatures, points out the high relation between communication, trust, hybrid/ remote work and team integration. To increase the work life balance and improve the efficiency of the organisation it is needed to strengthen the psychological safety processes, reinforce good cross divisional communications, provide employees with more occasions to build a sense of togetherness and trust in the organisation. Also the introduction of a system where employees feel secure to provide the needed feedbacks in a non-anonymous manner, coupled with regular surveys on employee' satisfaction as well as organisation processes need to be put in place to insure organisational sustainability.
2. Introduction
 2. INTRODUCTION  2.a Report Topic  Team dynamics relate to the behavioural and interactive processes of how individuals communicate, cooperate and collaborate with one another in teams. Team dynamics is fundamental to organisational effectiveness by enhancing information exchange, building trust and team members cohesion, developing knowledge sharing, and boosting the level of employee engagement (Collier, LaRocca and Stoeber 2012). Poor team dynamics have a negative effect on an organisation by fostering conflict and decision making problems, reducing productivity levels, and lowering the level of employee satisfaction (Jones and Bartlett Publishers 2010). Westpac Banking Corporation (WBC) is one of Australia's larger financial institutions, and an ASX200 company. 
WBC functions on a larger scale, hence communication must take place across various departments of WBC such as, technology, risk management, compliance, operations, and customer service to provide financial solutions. These different departments rely upon effective communication, trust and collaboration to fulfill common objectives of the organisation.Therefore, factors that facilitate team dynamics can be analysed further by conducting this report on the issues impacting team dynamics within WBC. This study focuses on four primary challenges influencing team dynamics impacting on WBC’s performance, Firstly, challenges in communication between teams and impacting upon organisational performance will be researched. Second, this report will discuss the significance of team trust for building psychological safety, boosting the level of employee engagement and collaboration (Edmondson 1999; Mayer, Davis and Schoorman 1995). Thirdly, this report will research remote work arrangements and the impact they have on effective communication, employee well-being and loyalty to the organisation (Gilson et al. 2015). Lastly, this research will focus on collaboration and team cohesion across various departments of WBC and the impact on organisational performance (Mathieu et al. 2008; Beal et al. 2003).
