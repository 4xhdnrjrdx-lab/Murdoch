## Short answer to read aloud

I chose noon because it is also the company in our group project.

Its mission is to “ring every doorbell, every day.” This suggests becoming part of customers' everyday lives.

I would describe its vision as becoming the leading digital platform in the Middle East. This interpretation comes from its published regional ambition.

Its stated priorities include helping local businesses compete and encouraging innovation. Its customer service page also emphasises convenient shopping, fast delivery and reliable support.

Its values include acting quickly, being original, working hard, finding practical solutions and supporting colleagues.

So, the mission explains what noon wants to do, the vision describes its future, the goals show its priorities, and the values guide how people work.
