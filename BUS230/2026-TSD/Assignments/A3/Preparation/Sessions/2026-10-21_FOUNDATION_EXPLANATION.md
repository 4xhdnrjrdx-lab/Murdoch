## Teaching fragment

**Organisational change means a change in how an organisation works.** It may change people's roles, working methods, technology or structure.

Imagine a company combining two customer-service teams into one.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Each team has its own manager. | Both teams report to one manager. |
| Each team follows its own procedure. | Everyone needs a shared procedure. |
| Employees know their usual responsibilities. | Some responsibilities need to be agreed again. |

The decision to combine the teams creates an organisational change. **Change management is the work of helping the organisation and its people make that change work in practice.**

In this example, a manager needs to explain the purpose, clarify responsibilities, help people learn the shared procedure and respond to problems as they appear. Announcing a new structure leaves these practical questions to be worked through.

Коротко: organisational change — что меняется в работе организации; change management — как организовать переход и работать с возникающими трудностями.
