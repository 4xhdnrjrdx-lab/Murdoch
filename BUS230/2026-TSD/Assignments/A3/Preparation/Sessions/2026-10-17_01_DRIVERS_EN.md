## Useful words

- **external** — внешний
- **internal** — внутренний
- **outside** — снаружи / за пределами
- **inside** — внутри
- **competitor** — конкурент
- **because** — потому что

An external driver comes from outside the business.
An internal driver comes from inside the business.

## Your first sentence

This is a fictional practice example:

> A café has a new competitor.

Is this an internal or an external driver? Complete the sentence in English:

> A new competitor is an ___ driver because it comes from ___ the business.

One sentence is enough. Mistakes are part of practice. Write your own attempt before seeing a corrected answer.
