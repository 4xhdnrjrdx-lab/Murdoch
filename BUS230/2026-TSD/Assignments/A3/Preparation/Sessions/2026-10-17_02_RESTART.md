## Opening explanation prepared for delivery in the new chat

**Stage 1/14 · Topic 1/8: Drivers of Change**
**Step: Block 1.1 — organisational change and three approaches**

**Organisational change means a change in how an organisation works.** It can involve technology, people's roles, rules, structure, or everyday habits.

Imagine a café introducing an ordering app. This is a fictional example.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Customers order at the counter. | Customers can order through the app. |
| The cashier passes orders to the kitchen. | Orders appear on a kitchen screen. |
| Staff organise work around one queue. | Staff must coordinate counter and app orders. |

The app changes more than the technology. It also changes **who does what**, **how people work together**, and **what skills they need**.

**Change management** involves helping the organisation and its people handle these changes: preparing staff, adjusting responsibilities, dealing with problems, and checking what actually happens.

In this topic, we will examine the same change from three perspectives:

- **Tool-based:** What tools or steps can help us manage it?
- **Process-based:** How does the change actually develop over time?
- **Critical:** What assumptions should we question, and whose interests are being served?

We will unpack these gradually. For now, keep the café in mind: introducing the app also changes the way people work.
