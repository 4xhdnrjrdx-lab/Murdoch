How this café works
Arrival order: C001 → A001 → C002 → A002. All four orders have arrived.
  • One model step is one cook action, not a real minute.
  • The cook can prepare one drink OR bring one app ticket into the kitchen queue per step.
  • The cashier can forward one app ticket alongside cooking. Added cashier duties may affect counter service; that delay is not measured here.
  • A shared screen routes both feeds automatically. Setup is assumed feasible for the next shift; test the integration in reality.
  • All drinks take one preparation action. No new orders arrive. Staff already know both screens.
