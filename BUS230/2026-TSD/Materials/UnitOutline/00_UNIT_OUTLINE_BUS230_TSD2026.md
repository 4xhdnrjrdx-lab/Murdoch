## 1 Unit Details

Welcome to:

**BUS230**

**Change Management**

### 1.1 Unit Overview

This unit examines contemporary change management theories and the various contexts in which they may be applied. The unit looks at the theories of organisational change, change management techniques and role of leadership and organisational culture in the success or failure of organisational change initiatives.

### 1.2 Learning Outcomes

This unit enables you to demonstrate your achievement of the following unit learning outcomes.

| Unit Learning Outcomes |
| --- |

| Apply change management knowledge in practical contexts |

## 3 How to study this unit

### 3.1 Approach to learning

#### 3.1.1 Learning approach underpinning unit

An experiential approach to learning is taken in this unit. The workshops require participation in discussions, case analysis, presentations, and group and team exercises. The workshops are a collaborative learning process and experience demonstrates this is most effective when students are prepared and participate. Students are expected to watch online videos and undertake required readings prior to attending the workshop. There are online components for this unit for all students, and students are encouraged to access LMS weekly and actively participate in online discussions and download any additional material where indicated.

### 3.2 Learning activities & requirements

#### 3.2.1 Overall expectations

- Read the details provided in this Unit Outline document (UO) and review the resources on the Murdoch Learning Management System (LMS).
- Actively participate in your own learning by preparing for workshops, completing required activities, and asking questions to improve your understanding.
- Practice good time management and plan sufficient study time to do the readings and prepare notes and complete all assessment items.

#### 3.2.2 Learning activities and details

There are 12 x 2-hour workshops throughout the semester. Students are required to complete reading and activities before and after each workshop. Each workshop has three interconnected components: pre-workshop, workshop, and post-workshop. The workshop activities are designed to build on the knowledge gained through reading the required chapters and watching the lecture podcasts.

- Pre-workshop will include video podcasts, online and textbook readings, and other learning activities.
- Workshops will include discussion questions, case studies, student engagement activities and other applied learning activities.
- Post-workshop will include summarising, collaborating, and reflecting on the topic/s covered.

### 3.3 Expected time commitment

As this is a 3-credit point unit, we expect you to spend 150 hours overall working on this unit. These ten hours per week should be spent reviewing your lecture notes, reading for your weekly workshops, and preparing for and completing each of the assessment tasks, as well as attending classes and/or participating on-line.

**Students are required to maintain at least 80% attendance in all teaching and learning sessions (classes, tutorials, workshops, labs etc.). ### 5.2 Assessment information

#### 5.2.1 Assessment 1 - Individual Essay (30%)

**ASSESSMENT DESCRIPTION**

Reflect on and critically analyse a change you have experienced. This change can be personal or professional. This reflection and critical analysis should be written in an essay format and integrate theories and concepts from the first three topics in the unit.

Word Limit – 1500 words (+/- 10%). Essay word limit does not include the end reference list or the cover page.

**GUIDELINES FOR SUCCESS**

As this essay includes reflection, you can write in first-person perspective. You should justify your thinking by reading widely on the topic, from all types of sources, e.g., textbooks, journals, and electronic sources. Develop a clear argument and ensure that it is written in proper essay structure.

**HOW TO SUBMIT**

Your essay must be submitted electronically as a single word document file using the Assignment Tool in the LMS. Use a filename which follows the convention UnitCodeAssignmentNumberFirstNameLastNameStudentNumber. For example: BUS230Assign1SmithJohn12345678

**HOW IT IS ASSESSED (summary)**

- Essay structure (including quality of introduction, conclusion, flow, and topic sentences)
- Quality of reflection (including critically analysing your response to the change)
- Depth and breadth of analysis (this includes integration and application of theory)
- Presentation
- Research & Referencing

A detailed marking rubric is available through the submission portal on the LMS.

The assessment includes a moderation process to ensure reliable, just, and fair outcomes.

Your assessment outcome will be provided to you via LMS

**FEEDBACK FOR LEARNING**

Via myMurdoch Learning – Unit Learning Management System and Gradebook, feedback for learning will be given with the Marking Guide.

**GUIDELINES FOR SUCCESS**

The essay must be referenced using the Chicago 17th Edition Author-Date Referencing Style. It also must be formatted with size 12 font (either Arial or Times New Roman), 1.5 line spacing, justified alignment, and on A4 pages.

A minimum of 5 quality references are required for this essay.

**FURTHER DETAILS**

See myMurdoch Learning for further details, such as instructions, communication, resources, guides, exemplars and a descriptive rubric assessment tool.

#### 5.2.2 Assessment 2 - MultiMedia Presentation (40%)

**ASSESSMENT DESCRIPTION**

In teams of four, students are to analyse the limitations of a chosen change management tool or process, and develop a new, or modified, tool or process which would address these limitations.

Groups will present a simulated training session for managers on using the tool or process that was developed. You are allowed to use the short duration of videos only in your presentations, if you are using the videos. Please note: it should be a formal training session.

Presentations should be 30 minutes, with 30 minutes being the maximum. Groups will be stopped if they go for more than 1 minute over the maximum.

All members of the group are required to present during the prescribed session. Multimedia slides are required utilizing tools like Prezi, Powtoon, Visme, Zapier, Biteable, Vyond, etc, any other handouts etc are optional.

NOTE: Presentations will be recorded for moderation purpose.

**HOW TO SUBMIT**

To ensure your work does not get mixed up with others', use a filename that follows the convention: Unit Code, Assessment Task, and Group name. For example: BUS230TeamReportFTATeam3. Only one member of the team is required to submit the assignment. Students are required to keep a copy of all submitted assessments.

Presentations will occur in class during session 10 and 11. Your slides must be uploaded to “Assessment 2: Presentation Material (Group) Submission Portal” in the LMS, a night before your in-class presentation.

- Refer to LMS for details on submission.

**HOW IT IS ASSESSED (summary)**

The presentation will be assessed on these key criteria:

- Introduction
- Structure
- Depth and Breadth of Analysis
- Information Comprehensiveness
- Comprehensiveness and Practicality of Tool/Process Modification
- Research and Referencing
- Presentation
- Timing
- Conclusion

The individual presentation component will be assessed on these key criteria:

- Presentation style
- Formal dressing

A detailed marking rubric for each is available through the submission portal on the LMS.

The unit coordinator reserves the right to moderate individual marks if it is evident that there have been significant differences in the quantity and quality of contributions by team members. The assessment includes a moderation process to ensure reliable, just, and fair outcomes.

Your assessment outcome will be provided to you via LMS.

**FEEDBACK FOR LEARNING**

Via myMurdoch Learning – Unit Learning Management System and Gradebook, feedback for learning will be given with the Marking Guide.

**FURTHER DETAILS**

The presentation must be referenced using the Chicago 17th Edition Author-Date Referencing Style. Total number of the slides should not exceed 30. 15-20 academic references are required, with 20 being maximum. This reference count excludes the website references.

A minimum of 15 quality references are required for this assignment.

The presentation should include the following sections:

**1: Introduction & Context (Slides 1–4)**

- Title Slide – Presentation title, student names, unit code, and date.
- Overview / Agenda – Outline of presentation sections.
- Introduction to the Topic – Context, importance, and relevance.
- Objectives / Aims – What the presentation seeks to achieve.

**2: Analytical Foundation (Slides 5–10)**

- Background Information – Key concepts or theories.
- Organisational / Industry Context – Where the analysis is applied.
- Current Issues / Limitations(s) – Problem identification.
- Analytical Framework – Model or approach used (e.g., Kotter, Lewin, etc.).
- Key Factors Influencing Change / Problem – Data or evidence.
- Summary of Findings from Analysis – Concise synthesis.

**3: Tool or Process Modification (Slides 11–18)**

- Proposed Tool / Process Overview – What is being modified or introduced.
- Rationale for Modification – Why change is necessary.
- Design or Framework of the New Tool / Process – Visual diagram or model.
- Implementation Steps – How the change will be rolled out.
- Roles and Responsibilities – Who will lead or contribute.
- Anticipated Outcomes / Benefits – Measurable results expected.
- Feasibility and Practicality – Realistic assessment of adoption.
- Challenges and Risk Mitigation – Barriers and strategies.

**4: Research and Evidence (Slides 19–22)**

- Supporting Research Evidence – Relevant academic and professional references.
- Best Practices or Benchmarking – Comparison with existing cases.
- Data or Case Study Support – Application or example.
- Summary of Research Insights – Key takeaways.

**5: Communication & Engagement (Slides 23–26)**

- Visual Summary / Infographic – Summarise main insights graphically.
- Stakeholder Communication Plan – How outcomes will be communicated.
- Practical Implications for Change Management – Application to BUS230 context.
- Interactive Element / Reflection Question – Engage audience.

**6: Conclusion and Wrap-Up (Slides 27–30)**

- Conclusion Summary – Restate key findings and implications.
- Recommendations – Clear next steps or strategies.
- Reflection / Lessons Learned – Insights gained from project process.
- References / Acknowledgements – Proper referencing in APA format.

All the extra explanation needs to be added in the notes section of the presentation or provide a separate document.

**FURTHER DETAILS**

See myMurdoch Learning for further details, such as instructions, communication, resources, guides, and a descriptive rubric assessment tool.

### 5.3 Extensions and late submissions

In this unit, extensions and late submissions follow these requirements:

- If you are facing difficulties in completing your assignments, you should consult your Unit Tutor and Unit Coordinator before the due date.
- Extensions must be requested prior to the due date of the assessment to the Unit Coordinator, in writing, providing evidence to substantiate the relevant reasons for the request. It is at the Unit Coordinator’s discretion whether an extension is granted.
- Any assignment that is received after the due date and time without a formally approved extension by the Unit Coordinator is deemed to be late. The time and date as recorded by the Unit Learning Management System (LMS) will be used to determine timeliness.
- Late submissions without an approved extension will have 10% of the total available mark deducted for each calendar day or part thereof, in accordance with the Assessment Policy.
- Assignments submitted more than one week past the due date without an approved extension will not receive a mark but may receive feedback.

LATE COMMENCEMENT in the unit, or non-attendance/participation, is an insufficient reason to obtain an extension without other supporting evidence such as a medical certificate in accordance with the assessment policy.

This unit follows Murdoch policies and procedures with regards to extensions and late submissions, supplementary and deferred assessment.

Students who feel that their disability, medical condition or disability caring responsibilities may impact on their capacity meet assessment submission are strongly advised to visit Access and Inclusion as early as possible to discuss potential needs and assistance.

### 5.4 Determination of the final grade

Students will need to obtain an aggregated grade of 50% or more to pass this unit. There is no requirement to pass individual assessment components and/or complete all individual assessment components in BUS230. It is not a requirement to pass the final examination to pass the unit. Students who achieve a grade of 45 to 49% in the unit MAY be offered a supplementary examination at the unit coordinator’s discretion.

Refer to Reporting of Results in the Assessment Policy for information about marks and grades.
