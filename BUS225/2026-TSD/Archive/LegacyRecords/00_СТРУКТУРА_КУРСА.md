## Секция 5 — Session 4 - Developing Service Products

_This chapter introduces the idea of a service product, which is different from physical goods because it is mostly experienced rather than owned. A well-defined service product helps customers understand its value, branding, pricing, and how to buy it. The chapter explains the three key parts of a service product: the core service (what customers are actually buying), supplementary services (extra features that support and enhance the service), and the delivery process (how the service is provided). It also introduces the Flower of Service, which shows how different supporting services help improve the customer experience. Branding is an important topic, as it helps make services stand out, builds customer trust, and creates loyalty. {%} - Read relevant textbook Chapters - Watch online resources and answer the pre-workshop questions - Attend the workshop seminar. Work and participate in discussion on pre-workshop questions and workshop activities. - Test self-knowledge on 3 post-workshop MCQ questions{%} By the end of this session you will be able to: - Understand what a service product is and why defining it clearly is important. - Identify the three components of a service product. - Explain the Flower of Service model and how supplementary services enhance the core product. - Recognize the role of branding in services, including brand architecture and brand equity. - Understand how companies develop new services, from small improvements to major innovations. - Apply design thinking to service design and identify key factors for success. {%} Essential Textbook: Chapter 4 {%}_
