Fail points and proposed controls
F1 Unsupported advice: dated facts; uncertainty warning.
F2 False availability: scan > stock check > restock > verify > update.
F3 Exclusion or poor advice: staffed route; customer choice.
F4 Wrong charge: preserve basket; verify price.
F5 Repeated complaint: one case; named human owner.
F2 No stock: show unavailable; keep task open.
Arrows show sequence and information handoffs. W marks potential waiting. Red labels mark potential failures. The return arrow reopens unresolved cases.
Proposed standards: acknowledge in-store help within two minutes; human response to post-purchase cases within one working day. Measure resolution separately.
Note. Proposed design using the blueprint structure in Wirtz (2023), informed by Majid Al Futtaim (2022). The optional assistant, integrations and controls are recommendations, not a verified internal process.
