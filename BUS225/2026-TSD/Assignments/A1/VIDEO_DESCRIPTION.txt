Would you trust an AI to choose your groceries? | BUS225 A1

An individual BUS225 presentation by Faiaz Dzhafarov.

A critical discussion of Kennedy, He and Sarantopoulos (2026), applied to Carrefour through a proposed UAE grocery assistant. The shopping scenario and pilot are proposals, not reported test results. The company examples refer to dated launch announcements.
